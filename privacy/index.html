PRIVACY

Small footprint. Clear choices.

No accounts, advertising cookies or third-party analytics are active on this site.

Roster entries and editorial drafts are also stored locally in this browser. Export backups from their respective pages. Saved champion IDs are stored in your browser under fieldguide:saved. Use the button below to remove them. An earlier interactive version of the Doomscrip guide stored progress locally under summonerindex:doomscrip-tracker:v1. The current information guide does not read, update, or transmit this legacy data. It remains in your browser until you clear the site data. Searches are processed in your browser; search terms may appear in the page URL and hosting access logs.

Clearing a list cannot be undone here. Export a roster or draft backup first. Local data stays until you clear it, your browser removes it, or you import a replacement. Each button removes only the named Summoner Index data.

External services

The hosting provider may process standard request metadata. Pages on this site are accessible to anyone. Fonts are provided by your device; this build makes no external font requests. External source and YouTube links open their own services; no video player is loaded automatically.

Email feedback

Feedback goes to mcoc1all1@proton.me only when you send the prepared email yourself. Your email address, message and attachments are handled by your email provider and Proton Mail and read by the site operator to investigate and reply. Do not include sensitive account information. This website does not store the form. Automatically included page URLs omit query strings and fragments. Email the same address to request deletion of your feedback.

Analytics readiness

The application emits local browser events for page views, searches, tool use and outbound video clicks. There is no active collector or persistent analytics queue. Before enabling one, configure a consent policy, retention limits and query redaction. No retention metric is currently measured.

Advertising and privacy choices

Current status (15 November 2026): No AdSense ad code, advertising cookies or third-party ad scripts are active. This notice explains the current site; it is not a consent request.

If advertisements are introduced, this page will be updated before ads are served to identify the actual providers, purposes, cookie and device identifiers, potential IP-address processing, relevant retention information and user choices. Providers such as Google may use cookies and other data subject to their policies and your consent choices. See Google's explanation of advertising technologies. That link does not indicate Google advertising is currently active here.

If ads are enabled for visitors in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, the operator will configure an appropriate Google-certified consent management platform where required. A visual banner alone is not a substitute for a real consent platform and its configured provider data.

For privacy questions, or to request removal of feedback sent by email, contact mcoc1all1@proton.me.
