EDITORIAL UPDATE · 2026-11-23

Bounty points are no longer released weekly

Kabam's November 18 Bounty fix lowered the minimum personal contribution to 1 point and removed weekly 125,000-point installments. You can direct earned points toward one eligible Weekly Operation or distribute them. Check individual bounty deadlines, eligibility and active Operation scoring before claiming.
