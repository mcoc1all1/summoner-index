COMMUNITY PULSE · REVIEWED 2026-11-12

Reclamation War: Kabam clarifies how to plan your week

Kabam says there is enough content and Doomscrip spending to exceed the 350,000-point weekly personal milestone without delaying normal play. Saving 50,000 Doomscrip is an optional way to unlock the 50,000-point Overlord Location milestone promptly after Monday's rollover. Week 1 completed every Weekly Realm milestone even though the Overlord survived; Swift Rivalry daily rank 50 earns 6,000 points versus 7,000 for rank 1. Check in-game requirements and any later rule changes.

This post's exact original publication date was not independently verified; the date above is when this summary was checked.

EDITORIAL UPDATE · 2026-11-09

Weekly Operation points lowered for future weeks

Kabam's official forum confirms a 100,000-point reduction in the future weekly personal contribution cap, from 450,000 to 350,000. Staff also announced reductions to the top global milestone targets. Do not assume the new personal cap applies retroactively to the first week; check each in-game Weekly Operation's current milestones.

The announcement's original publication date was not independently confirmed.

How this stays current

News and event dates are curated manually using linked MCOC pages and forum discussions. The former RSS auto-publisher is disabled; new announcements require a reviewed edit. Status badges use Pacific calendar dates rather than a live game-server feed. A one-date event with no verified end is removed after its scheduled start date instead of being incorrectly marked ongoing. Check the game for exact hours and eligibility. Check the official link and game client before acting.
