TACTIC 1

Pick the weapon, not just the class

Select her relevant utility weapon before engagement. Heal Block and tracking solve different problems from raw Incinerate pressure; choose based on the specific defender mechanic rather than the class icon.

TACTIC 2

Apply on the correct combo hit

Second-medium hits are part of her debuff application. Endings that refresh or pause debuffs are useful only when the debuff is already present and the opponent lets you continue attacking.

TACTIC 3

Heavy is a deliberate switch

A heavy can help preserve existing effects while also changing the selected weapon. Avoid accidentally changing tools in the middle of a fight that depends on a particular Heal Block or Tracking state.

TACTIC 4

SP2 needs an established stack

Use SP2 with an actual debuff setup for meaningful Incinerate or mutant-specific Plasma pressure. Debuff immunity and opposing Purify can remove much of the expected payoff.

Attacker starting point
Special Damage

Omega Sentinel cycles pre-fight Nanite weapons, keeps non-damaging debuffs refreshed, and uses SP2 or SP3 to convert setup into damage. Compare Special Damage against Block Proficiency with identical weapon, armor consumption and debuff count. Do not assume a larger special hit changes the Incinerate/Plasma tick formula or improves Tracking and Heal Block uptime.

Alternative to test: Not reviewed

Defender starting point
Armor Rating

Armor Rating is relevant to Omega Sentinel's Armor Up and threshold-based Auto-Block defense, but an extra stat does not grant Armor Up buffs, change the required stacks, or bypass Armor Break. Compare Block Proficiency only where incoming attacks actually land on her block; track whether the attacker's kit ignores Auto-Block.
