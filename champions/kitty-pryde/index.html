BEST USE

Where the kit helps

Critical hits and Stuns build Prowess. Incinerating the opponent increases Prowess potency.

WATCH OUT

Limits & caveats

Cowardice removes her Prowess. Incinerate immunity limits damage ramp. Her base kit is not a complete answer to Evade, Auto-Block or Miss.

INVESTMENT

Before ranking up

Awakening
Unlocks prevention of damage from all sources while Phased, plus defensive power and Incinerate retrigger benefits. The benefit does not protect time spent outside Phase.

EDITORIAL FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

Champion-specific fight decisions

Champion-specific setup, burst, matchup and failure-condition guidance independently written from the research catalog. These decisions are not site-tested clears; confirm the current live kit and node before relying on them. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

TACTIC 1

Establish a real Phase window

Build Prowess from critical hits or Stuns before dashing into Phase. Check whether Phase is active before challenging a special; an ordinary dash without the prerequisite is not a safe substitute.

TACTIC 2

Plan how to gain damage

SP1 adds Incinerates, while a longer fight may reward SP3's sustained Incinerate setup. Choose the path according to the remaining health and whether the opponent actually permits Incinerates.

TACTIC 3

Spend Prowess with SP2

A developed Prowess stack makes SP2 attractive, but losing Prowess to Cowardice changes the output. Watch the stack total and active Phase state so the burst does not land during a vulnerable reset.

TACTIC 4

Awakening changes safety

Her stronger damage-prevention interaction requires the awakened state while Phase is active. Do not present an unawakened Kitty as universally safe against damage, and watch anti-Miss and anti-Phase nodes.

SYNERGIES

Build considerations

Tigra

Kitty becomes passively Unblockable while Phased with at least ten Prowess.

Requires Vicious Kitty; both the Prowess threshold and Phase must be active. Team synergy only: check the owned rarity and whether the mode enables team synergies.
Emma Frost

Adds reversed-controls immunity and rewards a triggered immunity with Prowess.

Requires Through Thick And Thin; it must not be treated as Kitty’s baseline immunity. Team synergy only: check the owned rarity and whether the mode enables team synergies.
