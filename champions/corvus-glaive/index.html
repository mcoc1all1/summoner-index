TACTIC 1

Pick fights that award mission progress

Corvus Glaive develops persistent mission charges by satisfying specific combat objectives. Identify which targets on the quest path can actually complete those objectives, rather than assuming every win grants another charge.

TACTIC 2

Budget Glaive Charges in the current fight

His critical damage relies on available Glaive Charges; repeated hits can exhaust that resource. Check the charge counter before entering a long damage race and use a safe pause or special window instead of spending every charge into a low-value block.

TACTIC 3

Use SP2 when the charged window is real

SP2 rewards a developed charge and Cruelty setup, but the defender's health, special meter and available charge count all matter. A quick safe burst can be better than stretching a combo to a theoretical maximum that never lands.

TACTIC 4

Separate damage immunity from survivability

His ability to survive certain damage while charged has precise conditions and does not cover every source of damage. Verify the defender's ability accuracy, passive damage and node interactions before using Corvus as a guaranteed death-proof attacker.

7★ stat focus · eligibility verified

Attacker starting point
Critical Damage Rating

Editorial test rationale: Corvus's charged Glaive hits already critically strike while his Glaive Immunity is active, so Critical Damage Rating may improve eligible charged-hit damage more directly than extra critical chance. Compare equal mission progress, Glaive charges and immunity uptime; do not assume the focus shortens recharge or improves hits after all charges are spent.

Defender starting point
Armor Penetration

Armor Penetration is a tentative outgoing damage/pressure setting against armored opponents; it does not add Glaive charges, expand his mission bonuses or prevent the post-charge downtime. Check whether the incoming defender's armor can actually be reduced before choosing this over a defensive mitigation focus.
