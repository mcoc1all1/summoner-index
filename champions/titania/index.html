EDITORIAL FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Editorial decision notes derived from identified game mechanics and attributed source research; these are not in-game performance tests, guarantees, or permissions to reuse third-party text. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Choose the debuff for the next window

SP1 can set up Weakness and Debilitate, while SP2 supports Physical Vulnerability. Decide which effect helps against the actual defender and node before spending power; a generic Special rotation loses value when debuffs cannot be maintained.

DECISION 2

Treat Haymaker as a timed resource

Haymaker changes Titania's safety and damage options, but certain actions can consume the window. Check whether it is ready before dashing or committing to a Heavy, and do not plan a long punish around a state that has already ended.

DECISION 3

Duplicate debuffs only from a safe Heavy

During Haymaker, the first Heavy hit can exploit existing personal debuffs before a follow-up Special. Use an actual stun or punish window and inspect the defender's debuff immunity; an unsafe Heavy can waste the entire setup.

Always active

1 effect
Buff
Scope not reviewed

Titania is immune to gaining Buffs; this does not prevent passive beneficial effects or all effects that punish an attempted Buff. Neutralize and some other opponent mechanics can still trigger before Buff Immunity prevents an applied Buff. This is self-Buff immunity, not immunity to incoming Buff Immunity Debuffs.

Synergy / pre-fight

1 effect
Bleed
Debuffs

Bleed immunity requires the unique Rogue's Romance synergy supplied by Absorbing Man on the team. Titania's base Buff immunity does not by itself grant Bleed immunity. Passive Bleed scope has not been independently tested.

Resistance / scope note: Bleed immunity is not a base-kit claim here; listed synergy protection must not be treated as unconditional.
