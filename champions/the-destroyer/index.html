DECISION 1

Verify Degeneration in this encounter

The current research index lists Degeneration among The Destroyer's tools. Verify its activation in the live kit and whether this defender resists or ignores the effect before treating it as a damage plan. Its presence on an ability list does not establish reliable uptime or a completed fight.

DECISION 2

Track Destroy before committing

The catalogue also records Destroy for The Destroyer. Watch for its actual trigger while also tracking Degeneration; do not assume the two effects activate together or survive the same countermeasure. Spend power only after confirming that the expected state is present, and reset the plan when it drops.

DECISION 3

Confirm Bleed protection

The structured records flag Bleed protection for The Destroyer. Check whether the incoming effect is a debuff, a passive or a node-specific interaction, and verify any activation condition before depending on that protection. One recorded protection does not establish blanket immunity to related effects.

Attacker starting point
Special Damage

For The Destroyer, Special Damage is a direct-special-hit experiment, not proof that resulting Poison, Shock, Incinerate, Rupture or Degeneration ticks increase. Keep the source-linked setup constant: Punish heavies and specials, intercept safely, and use Special 1 to accumulate Destruction Charges. Compare Critical Rating against the same opponent, rank and resources. Do not mistake a changed direct hit for proof of longer resource uptime or stronger debuff ticks.

Alternative to test: Not reviewed

Defender starting point
Physical Resistance

For The Destroyer on defense, Physical Resistance mitigates eligible incoming physical damage and should not be treated as protection from every energy attack or debuff. Kit limitation: Non-damaging debuffs other than Stun remove charges; Salve and Willpower are disabled. Compare Energy Resistance using the same attack type, defensive sequence, relic state and node; no across-mode ranking or gameplay result is claimed.
