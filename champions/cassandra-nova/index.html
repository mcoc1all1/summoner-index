TACTIC 1

Channel only when the defender is punishable

Cassandra Nova develops Daunted through a Channel action. Secure space or an opponent recovery first; beginning a channel while the defender has immediate attack access risks losing the setup before it becomes useful.

TACTIC 2

Track Daunted as the control resource

Confirm the actual Daunted state and how the next hit uses it. Her regeneration is a separate conditional advantage, not permission to ignore attack damage during an extended channel.

TACTIC 3

Convert developed Daunted into Neuroshock

A special or eligible follow-up can pressure the defender with Neuroshock once the resources align. Check whether the target can receive Neuroshock and whether the timing remains safe, rather than forcing the move at maximum channel duration.

TACTIC 4

Keep Daunted and regeneration counterconditions distinct

Purify, immunity, Heal Block and nodes that prevent channeling safely can dismantle different parts of Cassandra Nova's plan. Verify the live kit before treating Neuroshock as guaranteed damage.

Editorial comparison: Cassandra Nova Channels to build Daunted until Neuroshock becomes available. Critical Rating may help eligible direct attacks, but cannot remove the Daunted threshold or boost Neuroshock ticks by assumption. Compare equal Channel progress and avoid confusing critical-hit variation with damage produced by Neuroshock.
