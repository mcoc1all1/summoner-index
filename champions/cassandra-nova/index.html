INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Channel Daunted only from an opening you can protect

Cassandra Nova's Heavy-based Channel can develop Daunted toward her Neuroshock loop. The resource is useful only if the defender lets the setup finish, so choose a knockdown recovery rather than starting a long Heavy in neutral.

DECISION 2

Wait for the Neuroshock threshold before spending the setup

The recorded kit links a developed Daunted count to Neuroshock applications from basic hits. Track the actual count and the defender's Shock-family protection; do not assume the opening combo is already dealing the fully ramped damage.

DECISION 3

Choose SP1 sustain or SP2 pressure based on risk

SP1 supports a recovery objective, while SP2 can build more Neuroshock damage and SP3 spends a developed Daunted bank. Compare health, opponent power and remaining node time before choosing a slow burst at the cost of survival.
