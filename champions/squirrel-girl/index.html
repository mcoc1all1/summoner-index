INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Use Light hits for combo and Mediums for Bleed by objective

Squirrel Girl can develop combo through Lights while Mediums help establish Bleed. Decide whether the next SP2 needs combo or an active Bleed before choosing the ender, and check the defender's Bleed resistance.

DECISION 2

Spend SP1 on protection when combo retention matters

SP1 can offer Combo Shield or Distraction, which may help a long ramp survive mistakes. A short fight may not justify sacrificing damage to build a shield, so read remaining health and node pressure.

DECISION 3

Transfer Tippy with SP2 only from a prepared setup

An SP2 knockdown can transfer Tippy for Instant Bleed after combo and debuffs have developed. Confirm the activation before starting another Heavy recall; a Bleed-immune opponent changes the value substantially.
