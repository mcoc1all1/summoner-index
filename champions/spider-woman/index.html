TACTIC 1

Build Furies through controlled offense

Spider-Woman can develop Fury before using it as part of a special attack. Track the active buff count and whether the opponent can remove buffs before extending a combo into a high-power defender.

TACTIC 2

Choose whether the fight needs Wither or Power Sting

Her utility can interact with power generation or an opponent throwing specials. Verify which debuff is actually active; Wither and Power Sting affect different moments and are not interchangeable permanent power locks.

TACTIC 3

Spend Fury buffs on an SP1 conversion

SP1 can turn a developed Fury state into Shock or Power Sting pressure. Confirm the target is susceptible and use a real punish, rather than spending all buffs on a special when they cannot produce the intended effect.

TACTIC 4

Read Fumble and anti-buff counterplay separately

Fumble, Purify, Shock immunity and Nullify can interrupt different layers. A large Fury stack does not imply immunity to passive damage or protection against every defensive ability.
