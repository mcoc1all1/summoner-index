TACTIC 1

Recognize the active Exploit Weakness target

Taskmaster tracks a particular type of opponent attack for Exploit Weakness. Identify that target and its remaining window before attempting to repeat the same input; do not rely on a memorized sequence if the opponent changes its attack pattern.

TACTIC 2

Complete the match during a safe punish

A successful exploit can create direct damage and alter his ability-accuracy pressure. Choose a punish where the required attack is actually safe, and avoid blindly throwing a Heavy just to satisfy an input with an active defender in range.

TACTIC 3

Separate quest learning from quick fights

His Learn the Foe mechanic can reduce the potency of subsequent debuffs under its conditions. The advantage develops differently in a long path than in one short Battlegrounds fight, so read the accumulated state before counting on protection.

TACTIC 4

Read opponent accuracy and passive effects

An exploit may help against Evade or Auto-Block, but it does not guarantee that every effect or node is disabled. Check signature state, debuff type and any reset conditions, particularly when facing unavoidable specials or passive damage.
