TACTIC 1

Keep Armor Up available before converting it

Civil Warrior can use Armor Up both as a defensive resource and as fuel for offensive Fury-related effects. Check the actual armor count before using the action that converts it, because stripping every Armor Up may leave a vulnerable window.

TACTIC 2

Decide whether the fight needs healing control

The updated kit offers healing and power-related utility under specific circumstances. Confirm the target's Regeneration and whether the relevant Heal Block or power effect has applied before building a lengthy special sequence around it.

TACTIC 3

Take the special during an active Fury window

Once an appropriate Armor-to-Fury conversion exists, launch from a real opponent recovery and track buff duration. Against a Nullify-heavy defender, a shorter attack plan may preserve more value than waiting for another armor or fury stack.

TACTIC 4

Read Armor Break and buff denial carefully

Armor Break, Neutralize, Stagger and node effects can eliminate either the defensive armor supply or the converted Fury. Do not assume Civil Warrior's helmet and armor grant blanket immunity, and verify the current live power-control text.
