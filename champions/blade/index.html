Champion-specific fight decisions

Champion-specific setup, burst, matchup and failure-condition guidance independently written from the research catalog. These decisions are not site-tested clears; confirm the current live kit and node before relying on them. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

TACTIC 1

Establish Bleed only when permitted

Blade's offensive rhythm benefits from Bleed against eligible opponents. Verify the target's Bleed immunity and purification behavior before building the whole damage plan around repeated debuffs.

TACTIC 2

Preserve power for a useful special

A safe special can help maintain his pressure, but spending power without a Bleed or Danger Sense opportunity may be inefficient. Bait the defender's special and choose the next action around the actual state.

TACTIC 3

Check Danger Sense prerequisites

Danger Sense requires the relevant opponent or team conditions; it is not a universal bonus against every defender. Confirm tags and synergy partners on the actual quest team before counting on the matchup advantage.

TACTIC 4

Treat regeneration as a tradeoff

Blade's recovery has resource and timing constraints. Holding Block to recover can give the opponent space and power, so use it in a controlled window rather than presenting it as free healing during an unsafe node.
