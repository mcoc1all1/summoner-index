These decisions are not site-tested clears; confirm the current live kit and node before relying on them. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

TACTIC 1

Develop Hatred from the actual triggers

Mangog gains Hatred through combat interactions that allow him to reach his Enraged state. Watch the present Hatred count and the opponent's power, avoiding unnecessary blocked hits solely to chase another stack.

TACTIC 2

Enter Enraged when there is room to capitalize

Maximum Hatred can trigger an Enraged offensive window. Plan the next attack during a genuine opponent recovery, rather than entering Enraged just as the defender reaches an unavoidable special or the fight is already ending.

TACTIC 3

Choose the special while Enraged is active

His SP1 and SP2 options can be more valuable when the resource state has developed. Confirm the actual Enraged icon and whether the target can be affected by the planned damage or control before spending meter.

TACTIC 4

Don't confuse Unstoppable with invulnerability

Any Unstoppable or defensive effect during Enraged has limits. Slow, Nullify, ability-accuracy effects and passive damage nodes can undercut the plan, so check incoming attack form and the current state rather than willingly taking hits.
