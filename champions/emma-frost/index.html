TACTIC 1

Check the current Diamond Form

Emma Frost's defensive resistances and immunities change while Diamond Form is available. Confirm the actual form indicator before blocking a dangerous hit; she does not have identical protections in Telepath Form.

TACTIC 2

Use form changes to choose the attack plan

Moving between Diamond and Telepath states alters her offensive and defensive opportunities. Inspect the current matchup and choose a safe punish rather than forcing a transition while the defender is ready to intercept.

TACTIC 3

Cash out at a real special opening

Her special attacks and Critical or Prowess-related damage vary by state and kit version. Watch the form and meter together so the next SP2 or other payoff lands while the desired offensive effects are active.

TACTIC 4

Do not treat conditional immunity as permanent

Bleed and Shock interactions in one form do not prove unconditional immunity across the entire fight, and Armor Break or other effects can disrupt form safety. Read the node's passive-versus-debuff wording before relying on her defenses.
