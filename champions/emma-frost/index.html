INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Treat Diamond and Telepath as distinct defensive states

Diamond form has special immunities and resistance; Telepath instead supplies a more offensive, Prowess-oriented state. An immunity active in Diamond cannot be promised while Emma is in Telepath, so inspect her power differential before selecting her.

DECISION 2

Check whether the next special switches forms

Specials can spend Prowess and temporarily change Emma's form. Time a special around a planned Telepath damage burst rather than launching only because a power bar fills; defense changes with the active form.

DECISION 3

Use Armor Break as a targeted defender answer

Armor Break forces her out of Diamond and delays returning while the effect persists. That is valuable counterplay, but a defender immune to Armor Break or protected by a node may retain Diamond's protection.
