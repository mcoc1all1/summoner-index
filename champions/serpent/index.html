INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-04; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Separate persistent Dread from Culforce

Dread is a quest-level resource while Culforce is built and spent through fight actions. Before choosing a burst sequence, check the current charge state and wall position; do not assume one type of charge substitutes for the other.

DECISION 2

Do not confuse resistances with immunities

The Serpent protects against Power Lock, Special Lock, Heal Block, Fate Seal and Coldsnap, but reduces Bleed duration rather than being universally Bleed immune. His Power Drain/Burn interaction is also a potency reduction; read the node before assuming zero power loss.

DECISION 3

Check the special and knockout states

True Focus and opening Unblockable windows affect how the opponent can avoid his specials. His once-per-fight Death Immunity depends on the relevant Dread and knockout conditions, so it is not a permanent immunity to every source of damage.

Editorial rationale: The Serpent develops Dread and Culforce before a buff- and SP2-driven burst. Critical Rating may improve direct hits that are eligible to crit, but it is not a guaranteed boost to his Fury, Power Gain or any noncritical damage. Block Penetration

Block Penetration is a trial for his block pressure while cycling buffs and specials; it does not increase Culforce or extend Death Immunity. Evaluate it against resistance options when the defending fight is decided by incoming damage instead of hits into Block.
