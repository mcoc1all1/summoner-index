Champion-specific fight decisions

Champion-specific setup, burst, matchup and failure-condition guidance independently written from the research catalog. These decisions are not site-tested clears; confirm the current live kit and node before relying on them. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

TACTIC 1

Establish the two Senses

SP1 can help establish Tigra's Sense effects, and landing hits with Neutralize conditions supports uptime. Verify which Sense is actually active before attempting a difficult special punish.

TACTIC 2

Only heavy-counter known non-contact hits

Charging a heavy can make certain non-contact special hits Miss, creating Rupture opportunities. This needs practice on the defender's exact special animation; contact hits are not handled the same way.

TACTIC 3

Make SP2 follow established pressure

SP2 rewards a developed Rupture and Sense setup. If the opponent cannot accept Rupture or you missed your earlier special punish, rebuild rather than assuming SP2 is an automatic large burst.

TACTIC 4

Know the matchup before committing

Anti-Miss, Neutralize resistance, Unblockable special contact and spacing limitations can collapse the familiar heavy-counter plan. Choose safer bait-and-punish cycles until the special hit types are proven.

Attacker starting point
Block Proficiency

Our attacker pick: Tigra's Neutralize and heavy-charge Miss punish create Rupture through careful spacing, not a generic special-hit damage loop. Block Proficiency helps secure chip-heavy openings; Special Damage should be considered only if SP direct hits dominate. Compare Block Proficiency against Special Damage in the same fight at the same rank, awakening, relevant buffs, and node.

Our defender pick: Her Miss window is not guaranteed against contact attacks. Physical resistance protects physical hits that can connect when the specialized heavy charge fails. Compare Physical Resistance against Block Penetration versus the same attacker and mode before investing.
