Champion-specific fight decisions

Champion-specific setup, burst, matchup and failure-condition guidance independently written from the research catalog. These decisions are not site-tested clears; confirm the current live kit and node before relying on them. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

TACTIC 1

Build the clean combo and Dexterity Precisions

Okoye's damage route can reward an unbroken combo and Precision gained through eligible Dexterity movement. Use safe dodges without overcommitting to an intercept when the next enemy special is dangerous.

TACTIC 2

Maintain Bleed and Shock under their own rules

Both Bleed and Shock may contribute to the payoff, but their immunities are separate. Track actual debuff uptime and whether Vicious or Ensnare is active rather than assuming every hit applies both damage types.

TACTIC 3

Spend a special with the useful Precision and debuffs

Use a safe punish when the developed Precision and damaging effects overlap. Against a defender immune to one component, shorten the setup rather than waiting for a full dual-debuff burst.

TACTIC 4

Check Ensnare and defensive counters

Ensnare may affect specific avoidance, not all defensive abilities. Bleed immunity, Shock resistance, Purify and combo loss can each remove part of Okoye's intended offense.

7★ stat focus · eligibility verified

Attacker starting point
Critical Rating

Okoye has recorded bleed and shock mechanics, but those labels alone do not establish a damage multiplier or a guaranteed activation. Test Critical Rating for direct basic-hit critical frequency against Block Proficiency for health preserved during damage-over-time setup. Keep defender, node, ramp state and fight duration matched. In particular, do not infer that this stat automatically boosts every debuff tick, special side effect or personal resource.

Alternative to test: Not reviewed

Defender starting point
Energy Resistance

For Okoye on PvP defense, examine Energy Resistance for incoming energy-hit mitigation, excluding debuff immunity versus Block Penetration for damage through a blocking opponent. Recorded bleed and shock do not guarantee that either stat will matter in every matchup. Compare equal-rank attacks against the same defender, distinguish damage dealt through block from damage resisted, and do not assume defensive bonuses are immunities.
