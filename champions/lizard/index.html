DECISION 1

Check Rupture before the opener

Before bringing Lizard into this fight, inspect Rejuvenate, Retribution, Rupture and Tracking in the live kit and read the quest node. Start with one safe basic-attack punish and observe which listed mechanic actually activates. Treat this as a research-derived starting checklist, not a verified opening rotation.

DECISION 2

Develop Rupture and monitor Rejuvenate

For Lizard, Rupture is a recorded damage interaction, but its damage form and stacking rules are not independently validated here. Check the defender for relevant immunity, Purify or resistance before trying to maintain uptime. Track Rejuvenate separately: the appearance of one ability does not prove that the second triggers on the same attack or persists for the same duration. Reset spacing before committing to another sequence.

DECISION 3

Choose a safe special payoff

Choose an SP1, SP2 or SP3 only after checking the current meter, actual active effects and whether the defender has a safe punish window. For Lizard, the index highlights Rupture alongside Rejuvenate, but it does not demonstrate a validated special order or measured damage outcome. If the opponent is close to a dangerous special, bait that attack before attempting a longer payoff.

DECISION 4

Handle Rupture failure conditions

When the target blocks Rupture through immunity, resistance or purification, prefer a shorter direct-hit route instead of holding power for an effect that never applies. A structured Poison protection is also listed; verify its availability, effect form and conditions instead of calling it unconditional immunity. Recheck the defender after a special, cleanse or node transition, and avoid treating this research draft as a guaranteed counter to a named champion.

Lizard is self-Buff-immune only for the duration of his own Special Attacks. The immunity can remove active Buffs during that interval, but is not an always-on protection. It does not mean immunity to the opponent's Buff Immunity Debuff.

Scope not reviewed

Lizard is immune to Heal Block only while a Buff or Unstoppable effect is active on him. This is not permanent Heal Block immunity; check current effect and linked-node behavior.

Resistance / scope note: Poison immune; Heal Block immunity requires a Buff or Unstoppable effect.
