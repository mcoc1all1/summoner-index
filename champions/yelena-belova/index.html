INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Set up Bleed Vulnerability before trying to maximize combo damage

Yelena Belova can use a Heavy to apply Bleed Vulnerability and SP1 block pressure or SP2 Disarm to develop combo. Check the opponent's Bleed protection and a safe opening before committing to the ramp.

DECISION 2

Enter Elimination only after the actual combo requirement

The Elimination state is tied to combo progress and can be accelerated through the appropriate Special. Watch the current count and your ability to hold it rather than treating the mode as active immediately.

DECISION 3

Use SP2 to maintain Bleed Vulnerability during Elimination

SP2 can refresh useful debuff pressure in the Elimination window, but a long bait may let the state run out. Track remaining combo resources and defender immunity before spending two bars.
