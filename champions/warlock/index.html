INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-04; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Make Infection a confirmed state

Warlock builds his Techno-Organic Virus before Infection prevents recovery. Watch the actual Infection indicator instead of assuming one contact hit permanently blocks healing; champion and node interactions can change the buildup.

DECISION 2

Choose SP1 or SP2 for a reason

SP1 is a power-control option but consumes existing Infections. SP2 instead supports Armor Break and refreshes an Infection, making it a better consideration when preserving the anti-heal window matters more than draining power.

DECISION 3

Match the immunity to the hazard

Warlock has several specific damaging-effect immunities, but they do not remove every node penalty or non-damaging debuff. Identify whether the fight requires Bleed/Poison/Coldsnap/Frostbite protection, power management, or something unrelated.
