TACTIC 1

Build the correct Chemical Gas state

Mysterio develops Chemical Gas through his normal attack rhythm. Check how much Gas is available before using a Heavy or SP3; spending it without knowing the intended conversion can empty the resource at the wrong moment.

TACTIC 2

Do not confuse SP3 Frenzy with Poison

SP3 consumes Chemical Gas to develop Frenzy Gas duration; it does not itself turn every consumed Gas charge into Poison. If Poison damage is the intended payoff, rebuild the correct resource and find a safe Heavy afterward.

TACTIC 3

Use the correct defensive illusion

His Illusion Gas can change whether certain incoming attacks connect, but the state is conditional and not a promise that every opponent Special Attack will Miss. Verify the active Gas state and whether the defender has True Strike or another anti-Miss effect.

TACTIC 4

Protect the helmet and account for immunity

Helmet disruption, Armor Break and a defender's Poison immunity can change both safety and damage. Do not borrow proposed rework abilities from forum suggestions; verify the current version before treating a counter or protection as automatic.

Conditional

2 effects
Poison
Scope not reviewed

Poison immunity applies while Mysterio's helmet remains intact. Armor Break Debuffs and knockdowns by Spider-Verse Heroes crack the helmet for about 10 seconds, suspending it. Do not treat a current immunity index's broad label as unconditional.

Ability Accuracy Reduction
Scope not reviewed

Mysterio's own Ability Accuracy cannot be reduced while his helmet is intact. Armor Break Debuffs or knockdown by a Spider-Verse Hero crack the helmet for about 10 seconds and suspend the protection. This is not immunity to all effects that modify the opponent's abilities.

Synergy / pre-fight

1 effect
Reversed Controls
Scope not reviewed

Mysterio gains Reversed Controls immunity through the unique Mental Immunity synergy with Emma Frost. This immunity is not part of his standalone base kit.

Resistance / scope note: Poison immunity is lost while the helmet is cracked; Illusion Gas Miss is temporary.
