TACTIC 1

Keep the opponent's status in view

Elsa's skill kit can reward specific opponent debuff and defensive conditions. Check which debuffs and ammunition-related effects are active rather than assuming each basic combo creates the same damage state.

TACTIC 2

Use clean spacing for the opening

Her attacks can pressure opponents while preserving distance, but an aggressive defender can still intercept a predictable dash. Keep room to bait specials and use a reliable recovery punish before committing to longer sequences.

TACTIC 3

Choose which special fits the fight

Different specials can serve different damage and control purposes depending on the current debuff state. Check whether the defender can be affected by the intended effects before spending power on a fixed rotation.

TACTIC 4

Avoid blanket immunity claims

Purify, debuff immunity and ability-accuracy effects may change Elsa's interactions with the defender. Read current kit language and node effects before assuming her class or a single damage tag guarantees a favorable matchup.
