TACTIC 1

Observe the rotating blessing

Sorcerer Supreme cycles between blessings that affect survivability, power and offensive utility. Check the active blessing before holding for a special; the same attack can lead to different practical value depending on the current phase.

TACTIC 2

Time the rune and special together

Her runes and blessing states make special choice a timing decision. Use safe baiting and the indicated spell state before committing meter, rather than relying on a rigid sequence that ignores an approaching blessing change.

TACTIC 3

Use appropriate control for the opponent

Against a buff-heavy defender, Mystic nullification and power interactions may be more valuable than direct damage. Verify which protection or buff is actually present, and don't assume every passive effect can be removed through the same route.

TACTIC 4

Watch blessing disruption and protection limits

Ability accuracy changes, Neutralize and nodes that interrupt a spell setup can undermine the expected payoff. A protective blessing does not confer unconditional invulnerability, so check incoming attack type and recovery space before trading hits.

Attacker starting point
Block Proficiency

Our attacker pick: Sorcerer Supreme cycles Blessings and runes for recovery, Nullification and Slow. Block Proficiency protects well-timed blocks while storing the useful Blessing, whereas Special Damage only aids eligible direct hits. Compare Block Proficiency against Special Damage in the same fight at the same rank, awakening, relevant buffs, and node.

Our defender pick: Physical resistance complements her blessing-dependent sustain against ordinary contact attackers, without implying that it extends runes or Nullification Aura. Compare Physical Resistance against Energy Resistance versus the same attacker and mode before investing.
