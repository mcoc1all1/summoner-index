EDITORIAL FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Editorial decision notes derived from identified game mechanics and attributed source research; these are not in-game performance tests, guarantees, or permissions to reuse third-party text. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Match the form to the incoming damage

Black Uru and Magma protect against different effects. Choose based on what the actual defender or node deals rather than the opponent's class; a form's resistance and immunity are distinct categories.

DECISION 2

Refresh an active form with a purpose

Refining an active form extends its value and builds Morph Charges. Use the correct special or supported combo timing during a safe opening; if your form expires before the refresh, the next action may establish a new, unrefined form instead.

DECISION 3

Do not rely on the defensive counter without checking cooldown

When facing Absorbing Man, a Heavy during his special recovery can shut off the active form, but that removal has a cooldown. Check the visible form and cooldown before repeating the same punish; Unstoppable or power manipulation can complicate it.
