INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-04; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Build Blessings deliberately

Kushala's blocks, intercepts and heavy interactions produce different Blessing opportunities. In a long fight, watch which Blessing is weakest before repeating a trigger: maintaining a balanced set is more useful than blindly rushing specials.

DECISION 2

Choose the damage conversion

SP1 can remove opposing buffs and add Incinerates; SP2 uses the Ignition setup to produce Soul Barb. Against buff-heavy defenders, inspect actual buffs and the Soul Barb state before spending power instead of assuming every special produces the same damage.

DECISION 3

Check the opposing protections

Incinerate immunity, Nullify resistance and node-granted buffs can each change the rotation. Her own Bleed and Incinerate protections do not establish immunity to every energy effect or debuff; identify the mechanic separately.

Editorial rationale: Kushala builds Blessings through well-timed blocks and uses SP2 to apply Soul Barb before further Incinerates from SP1. Special Damage is worth testing against the direct special-hit portion of that rotation. It must not be assumed to boost Soul Barb or Incinerate damage over time; compare complete fights at equivalent Blessing stacks.

Block Penetration is a defensive-pressure trial only when Kushala's defender specials or attacks connect into an opponent's Block. It does not improve her Blessing acquisition, Soul Barb or Incinerate potency; compare a resistance focus if incoming damage or matchup survival is the priority.
