TACTIC 1

Track self-Shock without treating it as damage

Thor (Jane Foster)'s updated Mystic kit uses harmless personal Shock effects as a resource. Count them before a special and verify what builds them; this is distinct from an opponent's damaging Shock debuffs.

TACTIC 2

Use Stagger to preserve the intended setup

An eligible Stagger interaction can help Jane Foster maintain her personal resources under the current mechanics. Check that it actually applies to the enemy's buff and survives the node before relying on an extended setup.

TACTIC 3

Choose the special for a deliberate Shock cashout

Once the personal self-Shocks are established, decide when to convert them into offensive pressure. Bait an enemy special and inspect the next attack's actual trigger instead of waiting for a fictional unlimited stack count.

TACTIC 4

Distinguish Jane Foster from Classic and Ragnarok Thor

Other Thor variants have different Armor Break, Crowd Excitement and Shock mechanics. Shock immunity, Nullify prevention and ability-accuracy restrictions must be checked against this Mystic version rather than imported from a Cosmic or Skill kit.
