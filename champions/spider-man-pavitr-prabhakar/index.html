Champion-specific fight decisions

Champion-specific setup, burst, matchup and failure-condition guidance independently written from the research catalog. These decisions are not site-tested clears; confirm the current live kit and node before relying on them. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

TACTIC 1

Use Mystic Webs to control positioning

Pavitr Prabhakar builds his route through Mystic Webs and Spider-Seals. Track the defender's placement and web state before forcing another attack; moving the opponent away can waste a developing setup.

TACTIC 2

Accumulate Spider-Seals deliberately

Watch the seal count and any Marked or Neutralize state after the correct web interactions. Do not assume every opponent power or buff effect is controlled merely because one seal has landed.

TACTIC 3

Use SP2 when the seal-based damage route is ready

A developed Spider-Seal setup can support an SP2 payoff with Energy Vulnerability or Power Steal interactions. Launch from a real recovery and confirm that the target permits the intended effects.

TACTIC 4

Check anti-web and Neutralize limits

Neutralize affects eligible Buff Gain, not every passive. Power Steal may fail against immunity or node-generated power, while Root-like movement restrictions do not guarantee safety from specials.
