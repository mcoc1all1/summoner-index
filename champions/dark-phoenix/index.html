TACTIC 1

Count Embers before forcing the next flame interaction

Dark Phoenix develops Embers that strengthen her fire-related attacks. Track the current counter and relevant effects before using a special; an energy-themed animation does not prove the intended Nova Flame is active.

TACTIC 2

Keep the flame resource without taking unsafe contact

Use normal recovery punishes to continue building Ember and Fury. Check the opponent's power and whether their Incinerate or related resistances deny the damage plan before committing to a long ramp.

TACTIC 3

Cash out an SP2 during the Fury window

Dark Phoenix's SP2 grants Fury whose potency increases with her current Ember Charges, but it also consumes half of those charges for an attacker. Count Embers before launching instead of assuming the same Fury can be maintained through every SP2. Nova Flame follows a separate post-special channeling window that opposing hits can shorten, so distinguish the instant SP2 resource exchange from delayed flame damage.

TACTIC 4

Check passive and debuff flame restrictions

Nova Flame, Incinerate and ordinary energy resistance are not identical checks. Buff denial, Purify and node restrictions can undermine different portions of Dark Phoenix's payoff.
