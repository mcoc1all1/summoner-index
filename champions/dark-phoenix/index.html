EDITORIAL FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

Conditional fight checklist

Four-stage, profile-specific conditional research checklists generated from the site’s structured ability and protection records. These are not individually researched or playtested tactical guides and must not be taken as validated special rotations or matchup recommendations. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Check Fury before the opener

Before bringing Dark Phoenix into this fight, inspect Fury, Nova Flame, Power Gain and Pulverize in the live kit and read the quest node. Start with one safe basic-attack punish and observe which listed mechanic actually activates. Treat this as a research-derived starting checklist, not a verified opening rotation.

DECISION 2

Develop Fury and monitor Power Gain

Fury appears in Dark Phoenix's ability index as a potential buildup or payoff state. Check whether the current fight actually grants it and what action consumes it, rather than copying a fixed stack count from an older guide. Track Power Gain separately: the appearance of one ability does not prove that the second triggers on the same attack or persists for the same duration. Reset spacing before committing to another sequence.

DECISION 3

Choose a safe special payoff

Choose an SP1, SP2 or SP3 only after checking the current meter, actual active effects and whether the defender has a safe punish window. For Dark Phoenix, the index highlights Fury alongside Power Gain, but it does not demonstrate a validated special order or measured damage outcome. If the opponent is close to a dangerous special, bait that attack before attempting a longer payoff.

DECISION 4

Handle Fury failure conditions

If Fury stacks cannot be built or maintained, take the available safe special opening rather than prolonging the encounter for an unavailable theoretical maximum. A structured Incinerate protection is also listed; verify its availability, effect form and conditions instead of calling it unconditional immunity. Recheck the defender after a special, cleanse or node transition, and avoid treating this research draft as a guaranteed counter to a named champion.
