Champion-specific fight decisions

Champion-specific setup, burst, matchup and failure-condition guidance independently written from the research catalog. These decisions are not site-tested clears; confirm the current live kit and node before relying on them. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

TACTIC 1

Use the Overrun resource deliberately

Dash back and hold Block to begin Overrun and refresh Fury. Plan the window where you can attack instead of using the stance purely to reach a higher visible buff count.

TACTIC 2

Heavy at the correct time

A heavy can pause remaining Fury under the relevant conditions. Heavy intercepts or safe special recovery are useful, but forcing a raw heavy while the defender is ready risks losing the damage state.

TACTIC 3

Choose SP1 or SP2 by opponent

SP1 supports additional Fury; SP2 adds Incinerate pressure while attack is elevated. A defender immune to Incinerate may favor the shorter Fury route instead.

TACTIC 4

Keep class and utility distinct

Fury-driven offensive ability-accuracy reduction may help, but it is not universal immunity to defensive abilities. Purify or nodes that shorten buffs require you to rebuild the plan more often.

Synergy / pre-fight

2 effects
Nullify
Scope not reviewed

Crossbones requires the unique Hydra Recruitment synergy with Punisher 2099 or Red Skull. That synergy protects him against Nullify while his Contempt bonus applies, ordinarily against #Hero defenders. Without the synergy or the qualifying Contempt condition, this protection is not part of his base kit.

Stagger
Scope not reviewed

Stagger immunity requires the Hydra Recruitment synergy with Punisher 2099 or Red Skull and an active Contempt bonus. Do not classify Crossbones as universally Stagger immune when fighting without that synergy or a qualifying opponent.

Resistance / scope note: Purify is not immunity. Critical stats can affect Fury generation or potency despite his inability to crit.
