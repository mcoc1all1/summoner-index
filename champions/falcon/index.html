BEST USE

Where the kit helps

Lock On passively reduces defensive ability accuracy on his attacks and improves basic attack damage. Its duration depends on the opponent’s class.

WATCH OUT

Limits & caveats

Defensive ability suppression is not blanket control over every mechanic. Check ability-accuracy immunity, resistance and node exceptions before relying on it.

TACTIC 1

Enter Recon safely

Dash back and hold Block until Recon produces Lock On. Build a safe distance first, because a rushed backdash near the wall can be punished before the target is actually locked.

TACTIC 2

Punish during Lock On

The timed Lock On window suppresses eligible defensive abilities during Falcon's attacks. Use SP1 for additional Bleed or SP2 for critical pressure according to the opponent's vulnerability.

TACTIC 3

Respect the cooldown

Once Lock On expires, the same target may Evade or trigger other abilities again. Disengage and plan the next scan rather than continuing the prior combo as though suppression never ends.

TACTIC 4

Defensive effects are not all equal

Check whether the troublesome effect is affected by Falcon's Lock On or is protected by ability-accuracy rules. Bleed immunity and high armor can also make the short burst less rewarding.
