DECISION 1

Check Power Drain before the opener

Before bringing Captain America (Sam Wilson) into this fight, inspect Armor Up, Physical Vulnerability, Power Drain and Rupture in the live kit and read the quest node. Start with one safe basic-attack punish and observe which listed mechanic actually activates. Treat this as a research-derived starting checklist, not a verified opening rotation.

DECISION 2

Develop Power Drain and monitor Rupture

The catalog identifies Power Drain as a potential power or control interaction for Captain America (Sam Wilson). Confirm the exact trigger and duration in the live kit; do not assume this mechanic stops every special or every source of power gain. Track Rupture separately: the appearance of one ability does not prove that the second triggers on the same attack or persists for the same duration. Reset spacing before committing to another sequence.

DECISION 3

Choose a safe special payoff

Choose an SP1, SP2 or SP3 only after checking the current meter, actual active effects and whether the defender has a safe punish window. For Captain America (Sam Wilson), the index highlights Power Drain alongside Rupture, but it does not demonstrate a validated special order or measured damage outcome. If the opponent is close to a dangerous special, bait that attack before attempting a longer payoff.

DECISION 4

Handle Power Drain failure conditions

When Power Drain does not control the defender under this node, bait a normal special and return to ordinary recovery punishes instead of taking damage while waiting for the missing effect. No independently confirmed protection is established by this checklist; check incoming damage types before deliberately trading hits. Recheck the defender after a special, cleanse or node transition, and avoid treating this research draft as a guaranteed counter to a named champion.
