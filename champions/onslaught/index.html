EDITORIAL FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Editorial decision notes derived from identified game mechanics and attributed source research; these are not in-game performance tests, guarantees, or permissions to reuse third-party text. Checked 2026-11-04; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Separate setup from cash-out

SP1 establishes Neuroshock pressure, whereas SP2 converts accumulated Neuroshocks into Degeneration. Record the active stacks before deciding to launch SP2; a defensive node that changes power or damage-over-time behavior may change the result.

DECISION 2

Avoid accidental Daunted triggers

A well-timed block can place Daunted and restrict Willpower healing. When fighting Onslaught, do not blindly Parry for every opening. A backward dash can remove a Neuroshock under the kit's conditions, but Metal champions have an additional restriction.

DECISION 3

Resistance is not immunity

Onslaught reduces the potency of several damage effects; that is not equivalent to an immunity to Shock, Bleed or Incinerate. Damage modifiers and Willpower interactions still need a specific matchup check, especially before using a Shock-focused attacker.

Attacker starting point
Special Damage

Editorial rationale: Onslaught builds Neuroshock and converts it into Degeneration using SP2. Special Damage is a trial for connected special-hit damage, not an automatic multiplier of Neuroshock or Degeneration ticks. Compare the same Neuroshock stacks and total fight time before treating it as the preferred attacker setting.

Alternative to test: Not reviewed

Defender starting point
Block Penetration

Block Penetration could add pressure only on attacks the opposing player blocks. It does not strengthen Neuroshock, Degeneration, Delirium or Crush, so compare it with a defensive mitigation option in matchups where the attacker does not block.
