EDITORIAL FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Editorial decision notes derived from identified game mechanics and attributed source research; these are not in-game performance tests, guarantees, or permissions to reuse third-party text. Checked 2026-11-04; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Select a Wushu Strike on purpose

Shang-Chi's combo endings trigger different utility: Slow, Cleanse, Unblockable or Stun. Decide which response the matchup demands before committing to a sequence; an accidental finisher can leave that strike unavailable.

DECISION 2

Reset the useful strikes safely

A Heavy can ready spent Wushu Strikes and help prepare the critical-damage window. Do not use a slow Heavy merely to reset the toolkit if the defender can interrupt or punish the charge.

DECISION 3

Convert accumulated Chi at the right time

SP2 rewards a deliberate Chi setup with guaranteed critical attacks. Compare the added damage against the risk of extending the fight, and remember that Cleanse removes debuffs rather than making him innately immune.

Attacker starting point
Critical Damage Rating

Editorial test rationale: Shang-Chi builds Chi with Wushu Strike sequences and spends it on a SP2 whose hits are guaranteed critical. Critical Damage Rating may therefore affect the direct critical-hit portion more predictably than raising critical chance for those hits. Compare matched Chi totals and note that critical resistance and node effects can alter the result.

Alternative to test: Not reviewed

Defender starting point
Block Penetration

Block Penetration is a defender pressure option only when attacks hit into the opponent's Block; it does not grant additional Chi, Slow or guaranteed criticals. Compare a mitigation focus if the attacker rarely blocks or defeats Shang-Chi through special punishes.
