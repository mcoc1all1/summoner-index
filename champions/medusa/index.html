INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Build Armor Shattered only against removable armor

Medusa's Armor Break buildup can convert into Armor Shattered and provide useful robot-facing control. Confirm the defender can receive the relevant effects; Armor Break immunity or node restrictions can prevent the expected setup.

DECISION 2

Choose a Bleed follow-up based on the target's immunity

Once Armor Shattered is established, basic-attack choices can support a Bleed damage line. A Bleed-immune opponent changes that payoff, so plan around actual control and damage rather than assuming both work.

DECISION 3

Track the Shatter duration before relying on control

Armor Shattered can be a strong tactical state, but it is not an indefinite guarantee in every fight. Watch the remaining timer and rebuild if necessary before taking a risk that depends on suppressing the defender's abilities.
