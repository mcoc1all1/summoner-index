TACTIC 1

Reach Armor Shattered first

Repeated Armor Breaks can combine into Armor Shattered. Keep track of how many effects are active and their timing before assuming the defender's Robot abilities are already suppressed.

TACTIC 2

Choose targets where Shatter matters

Armor Shattered is especially relevant against many Robot opponents, but the exact ability suppression can depend on the defender and node. Don't treat any Armor Break stack as full ability shutdown.

TACTIC 3

Add damage after control

Once the Shattered condition is established, normal light or heavy interactions can support a Bleed plan. Verify Bleed eligibility and keep the Shatter state visible while spending power.

TACTIC 4

Rebuild instead of overcommitting

Shatter expiry, Armor Break immunity or debuff Purify can undo the setup. If the opponent cannot be Shattered reliably, use safe specials and re-evaluate rather than blindly pursuing the standard sequence.
