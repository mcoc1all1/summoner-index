INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Establish a Bleed only when the matchup permits it

Carnage can use Heavy attacks to build Bleed pressure before his Mutation cycle. An opponent that is Bleed-immune removes this part of the normal damage plan; avoid risking an unsafe Heavy for an effect that cannot stick.

DECISION 2

Choose the desired permanent Mutation buff before the timer ends

Carnage's Mutation can convert an eligible attack choice into a longer-lasting offensive buff. Decide which effect the fight needs rather than ending the timer with a random attack and expecting the same ramp every time.

DECISION 3

Use Unblockable Special pressure only under the recorded conditions

The interplay with active Bleed or Armor Break can change a Special's Unblockable opportunity. Confirm the relevant debuff and defender protections first; an immune target or cleansed debuff invalidates the assumption.
