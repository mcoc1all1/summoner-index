INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Build Panther's Might from the correct resource sequence

Black Panther's reworked recorded rotation uses unfinished-combo movement and Cruelty to build toward Panther's Might. Watch the inactive charge state rather than assuming a normal full combo activates the mode.

DECISION 2

Use Heavy or SP1 to enter the damage window safely

A properly prepared Heavy or SP1 can enter Panther's Might, but an exposed Heavy is a poor way to rush the transformation. Choose the activation that matches the current defender recovery and node restrictions.

DECISION 3

Save SP2 for an actual Cruelty-enhanced opportunity

His SP2 can benefit from developed Cruelty while Panther's Might is maintained. Check the buffs and remaining duration; an early Special may give less value than a clean setup, but waiting too long also loses uptime.
