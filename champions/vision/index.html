TACTIC 1

Choose SP1 when an opponent has power to burn

The publisher explains that Vision's SP1 and SP2 primarily deal damage through Power Burn, not ordinary special-hit damage. Check the defender's actual power before launching; an SP1 against an empty power meter may not deliver the expected burst.

TACTIC 2

Use SP2 to control a dangerous special threshold

When the defender has substantial power and permits the effect, SP2's burn can serve both control and damage. Bait a safe recovery first and inspect the opponent's meter afterward instead of assuming a node-based power source can always be stopped.

TACTIC 3

Don't expect Power Shield to multiply the burn

Kabam's combat FAQ clarifies that Power Shield does not boost Vision's SP1 or SP2 Power Burn damage like ordinary Special Attack damage. Use that distinction when choosing a boss counter rather than treating the node as a universal damage amplifier.

TACTIC 4

Check Power Burn immunity and the Vision variant

Vision (Age of Ultron) and other Vision profiles should not automatically share every signature or immunity. Confirm the exact roster entry, the live Power Burn wording and the defender's ability before relying on a fixed drain.

Attacker starting point
Special Damage

Our attacker pick: Vision's power-burn specials offer both direct damage and control. Special Damage targets direct special hits after power is drained, not the amount of power burnt or Heal Block duration. Compare Special Damage against Block Proficiency in the same fight at the same rank, awakening, relevant buffs, and node.

Energy Resistance

Our defender pick: Energy resistance is a reasonable defense against energy-special attackers while Vision's control is unavailable; block penetration is the alternative for attackers who hold block.
