TACTIC 1

Establish Degeneration and Torment first

Blade (Stellar Forged) can inflict Degeneration Passives through Well-Timed Blocks, so confirm that the relevant effect was actually established instead of expecting ordinary attacks to create it automatically. His SP1 has a separate Atrophy and Torment role. Watch the opponent's existing Degenerations and the fractured meter before choosing an attack; classic Skill Blade's Danger Sense and regeneration rules must not be treated as a substitute rotation.

TACTIC 2

Control Fractured Power rather than holding indefinitely

His Fractured Power Bar changes how specials cycle. Watch the current fractured state, Atrophy and the defender's power while setting up; a simple two-bar assumption can select the wrong special.

TACTIC 3

Use SP2 in the developed Torment window

The community-reported damage plan emphasizes building Degeneration through repeated SP3 use, while SP1 contributes Atrophy/Torment and SP2 has situational Power Steal. That player account does not establish the fastest or safest sequence for every node. Verify the current Degeneration count and duration before choosing whether a safe SP3 setup or a power-control SP2 is actually useful; do not advertise an automatic infinite SP2 loop.

TACTIC 4

Check Degeneration and power-rule counters

Power Steal and Atrophy may not defeat independent node power generation. A defender that rejects Degeneration or is protected from the specific Torment interaction can force a shorter alternative route.

Attacker starting point
Special Damage

Our attacker pick: Torment and Degeneration define his output, but those periodic effects should not be credited to Special Damage. Select it only to strengthen the direct SP2 portion of the fractured-power rotation. Compare Special Damage against Block Proficiency in the same fight at the same rank, awakening, relevant buffs, and node.

Block Penetration

Our defender pick: His unusual power-bar tempo can pressure a defender who blocks repeated specials. Block penetration is offensive pressure, not an increase to Torment or defense against damage. Compare Block Penetration against Energy Resistance versus the same attacker and mode before investing.
