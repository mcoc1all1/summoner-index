TACTIC 1

Establish Degeneration and Torment first

Blade (Stellar Forged) has distinct Torment and Degeneration interactions. Confirm these effects and the correct variant before attempting a long special loop; do not import the ordinary Skill Blade's Danger Sense rotation.

TACTIC 2

Control Fractured Power rather than holding indefinitely

His Fractured Power Bar changes how specials cycle. Watch the current fractured state, Atrophy and the defender's power while setting up; a simple two-bar assumption can select the wrong special.

TACTIC 3

Use SP2 in the developed Torment window

Spend a special after Degeneration and Torment are active and a safe opening exists. The actual damage depends on target resistance and duration, so do not promise a guaranteed infinite SP2 cycle.

TACTIC 4

Check Degeneration and power-rule counters

Power Steal and Atrophy may not defeat independent node power generation. A defender that rejects Degeneration or is protected from the specific Torment interaction can force a shorter alternative route.

Attacker starting point
Block Proficiency

Blade (Stellar Forged) has recorded danger sense and atrophy mechanics, but those labels alone do not establish a damage multiplier or a guaranteed activation. Test Block Proficiency for health lost while managing the defender between control opportunities against Special Damage for the damage from eligible special attack hits. Keep defender, node, ramp state and fight duration matched. In particular, do not infer that this stat automatically boosts every debuff tick, special side effect or personal resource.

Alternative to test: Not reviewed

Defender starting point
Block Penetration

For Blade (Stellar Forged) on PvP defense, examine Block Penetration for outgoing defender pressure on a blocking attacker versus Physical Resistance for mitigation against physical attacks that land. Recorded danger sense and atrophy do not guarantee that either stat will matter in every matchup. Compare equal-rank attacks against the same defender, distinguish damage dealt through block from damage resisted, and do not assume defensive bonuses are immunities.
