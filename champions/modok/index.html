INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Track the hit counter before the critical-rating trigger

MODOK's offense can reward reaching a particular attack-count state before a Special. Inspect the visible counter and use a reliable punish rather than assuming a Special launched at any point has the same critical potential.

DECISION 2

Choose Heavy Incinerate or SP2 Bleed by target protection

Heavy attacks may create Incinerate opportunities while SP2 can supply Bleed. These are separate damage effects: verify the target's immunity and avoid a risky Heavy when neither effect can be applied.

DECISION 3

Use SP1 for the healing or Power Gain problem it answers

SP1 can interfere with specific healing or power behavior under the recorded kit. Because reduction amounts and activation can change, rely on the current ability card and node rules instead of claiming that all Power Gain is disabled.
