TACTIC 1

Watch the periodic critical-hit opportunity

M.O.D.O.K. can gain a periodic critical-hit bonus. Monitor its actual availability before choosing the next special; spending meter a moment before the bonus activates may undercut the intended burst.

TACTIC 2

Read Flux as an active state, not permanent defense

Flux and Auto-Block can change how he handles eligible attacks. Confirm the defensive state and opponent attack form rather than assuming a projectile, an unblockable, and a contact hit all behave identically.

TACTIC 3

Align the chosen special with the critical window

When the critical bonus is ready, choose a safe SP1 or SP2 route suited to the opponent's immunities. Check whether the intended Incinerate or Bleed applies; one damage-over-time type may be blocked while the other works.

TACTIC 4

Check anti-Auto-Block and damage-over-time counters

True Accuracy and ability-accuracy changes may defeat Auto-Block, while Poison, Incinerate or Bleed immunities change the special payoff. A high Auto-Block chance is not universal safety.
