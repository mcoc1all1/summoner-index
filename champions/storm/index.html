INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Build Prowess without ignoring the opponent's critical defenses

Storm can gain Prowess through eligible critical hits and some interactions with incoming effects. Check whether the target resists critical damage and whether the developing buff count justifies waiting for SP2.

DECISION 2

Use a safe Heavy to pause a developed Prowess bank

A Heavy knockdown can help maintain the prepared Prowess window, but the timing and incoming Special threat matter. Do not force a Heavy at neutral range solely to extend a buff by a short interval.

DECISION 3

Choose SP2 damage or SP1 follow-up based on confirmed Stun

SP2 may create a strong damage and Stun opportunity; SP1 can be useful against an already Stunned defender. Confirm the Stun landed before planning an extended combo, especially against Stun-immune nodes.

Attacker starting point
Special Damage

Storm builds Prowess when eligible critical hits or immunities trigger, then spends a special window whose direct damage benefits from Prowess. Compare Special Damage with Critical Damage Rating at the same Prowess count and recorded critical hits. Neither pick generates Prowess by itself nor proves increased SP1 Shock damage over time.

Defender starting point
Physical Resistance

Physical Resistance offers a trial against ordinary physical counterattacks while Storm's Prowess and Special timing are contested; it is not equivalent to Shock immunity or a safeguard against energy specials. Compare Block Proficiency only under identical opponent actions that result in blocked hits.
