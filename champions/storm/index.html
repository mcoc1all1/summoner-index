TACTIC 1

Identify the version and prowess rules

Storm's effectiveness depends on which version and ability text the current profile represents. Confirm how Prowess is earned and retained before entering a long chain; don't import a rotation from an alternate Storm champion.

TACTIC 2

Build a safe special opportunity

Use reliable basic-attack punishes while tracking active Prowess rather than chasing an arbitrary maximum count. If a defender is about to reach a difficult special, prioritize baiting it over extending an idealized setup.

TACTIC 3

Spend Prowess for an appropriate burst

Select the special according to the current Prowess state and the target's Shock or stun resistance where relevant. A direct-damage window is worth more than a theoretical high stack if you cannot finish the sequence safely.

TACTIC 4

Plan around control restrictions

Shock immunity, stun immunity, purify and ability-accuracy modifiers can remove parts of Storm's utility. Check whether the actual kit effect is a passive or debuff before relying on the same interaction in every node.

7★ stat focus · eligibility verified

Attacker starting point
Special Damage

Storm builds Prowess when eligible critical hits or immunities trigger, then spends a special window whose direct damage benefits from Prowess. Compare Special Damage with Critical Damage Rating at the same Prowess count and recorded critical hits. Neither pick generates Prowess by itself nor proves increased SP1 Shock damage over time.

Alternative to test: Not reviewed

Defender starting point
Physical Resistance

Physical Resistance offers a trial against ordinary physical counterattacks while Storm's Prowess and Special timing are contested; it is not equivalent to Shock immunity or a safeguard against energy specials. Compare Block Proficiency only under identical opponent actions that result in blocked hits.
