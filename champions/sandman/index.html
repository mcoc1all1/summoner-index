DECISION 1

Verify Decelerate in this encounter

Sandman is catalogued with Decelerate. Confirm what action triggers it in the current champion version, then check whether the active quest node changes that interaction. Choose a repeatable safe opening based on what you see rather than assuming a copied rotation fits this encounter.

DECISION 2

Track Petrify before committing

The catalogue also records Petrify for Sandman. Watch for its actual trigger while also tracking Decelerate; do not assume the two effects activate together or survive the same countermeasure. Spend power only after confirming that the expected state is present, and reset the plan when it drops.

DECISION 3

Confirm Bleed protection

The structured records flag Bleed protection for Sandman. Check whether the incoming effect is a debuff, a passive or a node-specific interaction, and verify any activation condition before depending on that protection. One recorded protection does not establish blanket immunity to related effects.

7★ stat focus · eligibility verified

Attacker starting point
Block Proficiency

Block Proficiency can reduce chip damage while building Resistance Ups and preparing a rooted Heavy Unstable cycle; it does not shorten the path to maximum stacks or strengthen the root. Compare Special Damage at matched stacks.

Alternative to test: Not reviewed

Defender starting point
Physical Resistance

Physical Resistance reduces eligible physical damage only; it cannot compensate for a critical-hit trigger that removes the Resistance needed to remain Unstable. Compare Block Proficiency for block-heavy attacker matchups.
