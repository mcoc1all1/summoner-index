DECISION 1

Check Poison before the opener

Before bringing Nico Minoru into this fight, inspect Buff Immunity, Halt and Poison in the live kit and read the quest node. Start with one safe basic-attack punish and observe which listed mechanic actually activates. Treat this as a research-derived starting checklist, not a verified opening rotation.

DECISION 2

Develop Poison and monitor Buff Immunity

For Nico Minoru, Poison is a recorded damage interaction, but its damage form and stacking rules are not independently validated here. Check the defender for relevant immunity, Purify or resistance before trying to maintain uptime. Track Buff Immunity separately: the appearance of one ability does not prove that the second triggers on the same attack or persists for the same duration. Reset spacing before committing to another sequence.

DECISION 3

Choose a safe special payoff

Choose an SP1, SP2 or SP3 only after checking the current meter, actual active effects and whether the defender has a safe punish window. For Nico Minoru, the index highlights Poison alongside Buff Immunity, but it does not demonstrate a validated special order or measured damage outcome. If the opponent is close to a dangerous special, bait that attack before attempting a longer payoff.

DECISION 4

Handle Poison failure conditions

When the target blocks Poison through immunity, resistance or purification, prefer a shorter direct-hit route instead of holding power for an effect that never applies. A structured Poison protection is also listed; verify its availability, effect form and conditions instead of calling it unconditional immunity. Recheck the defender after a special, cleanse or node transition, and avoid treating this research draft as a guaranteed counter to a named champion.

Poison
Scope not reviewed

Nico gains Poison effect immunity only while the once-per-quest Ward pre-fight spell is selected. Her Buff Immunity ability is inflicted on the opponent; she is not inherently self-Buff-immune.

Rupture
Scope not reviewed

Nico gains Rupture effect immunity through the Ward pre-fight spell, which can be chosen once per quest. Other pre-fight choices do not supply this immunity.

Resistance / scope note: Poison and Rupture immunity require the Ward pre-fight spell; they are not always active.
