TACTIC 1

Charge before a heavy

Use well-timed blocks to build Kinetic Potential, then inspect the charge count before beginning a heavy. A heavy started into the opponent's recovery is safer than relying on a Parry that the node may prevent.

TACTIC 2

Convert the Fury into SP2

A charged heavy can supply Fury for a heavy-to-SP2 punish. Commit only if the opponent cannot intercept the heavy startup; if control is unreliable, return to building charges instead of forcing the conversion.

TACTIC 3

Change the special for utility

When defensive ability suppression matters more than immediate damage, inspect the charged SP1 route rather than automatically taking SP2. Check the defender's ability-accuracy immunity and the specific node wording.

TACTIC 4

Do not spend charges blindly

If Kinetic Potential was consumed on an earlier interaction, a copied heavy-to-SP2 timing produces a weaker result. Rebuild through safe blocks and check the current awakened/class-synergy conditions before planning a long quest.
