INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Bank Kinetic Potential only through safe timed blocks

Captain America (Infinity War) gains Kinetic Potential from eligible blocks, but accepting repeated block damage to reach maximum can be counterproductive. Track the charges and stop blocking when the defender threatens an Unblockable attack.

DECISION 2

Use Heavy into SP2 only when Fury will last through the attack

A safely started Heavy can create Fury before an SP2 damage window. Check the current Kinetic Potential and opponent recovery rather than forcing an exposed Heavy startup against an aggressive defender.

DECISION 3

Reserve SP1 suppression for a defender ability you can affect

SP1 can support a different defensive-ability suppression objective with adequate setup. Node-protected abilities or ability-accuracy immunity may limit the payoff, so do not advertise it as a universal shutdown.

Critical Rating

Editorial comparison: Captain America (Infinity War) gathers Kinetic Potential through well-timed blocks and converts a Heavy-triggered Fury window into a Special 2. Critical Rating could raise eligible direct-hit critical frequency, but cannot add Kinetic Potential, lengthen Fury or replace the heavy-cancel timing. Compare equal potential and the same special openings.

Defender starting point
Armor Penetration

Armor Penetration is an outgoing damage experiment on armored matchups, not a defensive survival improvement. It does not stop Kinetic Potential decay, prevent charges being spent by specials, or bypass every armor-based node. Compare a blocking or resistance setting when taking hits while charging is the limiting factor.
