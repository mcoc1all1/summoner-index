TACTIC 1

Build Fury for the quest length

Sabretooth can develop Fury through his fighting rhythm and reward deliberate resource preservation across a longer quest. Count the current passive Fury effects before deciding whether a short fight is worth a setup-heavy plan.

TACTIC 2

Use a Heavy during a real opening

A safe Heavy can affect his Fury management and damage route. Avoid forcing one into an alert defender simply to maintain a long-term resource; use recovery from the defender's Heavy or Special Attack when available.

TACTIC 3

Watch persistent resources between fights

The advantage of a multi-fight Sabretooth run is not the same as his opening state in an isolated duel. Inspect the persistent charge and Fury state before each new defender and adapt the special choice to the resources actually carried forward.

TACTIC 4

Confirm synergies and updated kit

His stronger long-path interactions can rely on roster-specific partners, while opponent Purify or buff-related restrictions may change their value. The publisher announced a rebirth-era review; verify the current live kit before recommending old charge limits or synergy-dependent numbers.

Attacker starting point
Critical Damage Rating

Our attacker pick: Sabretooth carries Fury across fights, so sustained direct hits become stronger as persistent charges grow. Critical Damage Rating is tested during that high-Fury ramp, rather than being credited with more persistent charges. Compare Critical Damage Rating against Special Damage in the same fight at the same rank, awakening, relevant buffs, and node.

Defender starting point
Physical Resistance

Our defender pick: Regeneration and Fury help him survive and pressure opponents but cannot eliminate physical burst damage. Physical resistance targets damage that gets through during the persistent-Fury fights. Compare Physical Resistance against Block Penetration versus the same attacker and mode before investing.
