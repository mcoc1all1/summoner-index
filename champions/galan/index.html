INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Build Planetary Mass without feeding dangerous specials

Galan uses his buff interactions and enemy Armor Up to accumulate Planetary Mass. If a defender gains Armor Up safely, that can accelerate setup; but chasing stacks through an opponent's ready special is a poor trade.

DECISION 2

Enter Harvest only when you can finish it

A Heavy can start Harvest after enough Mass has accumulated. Plan the next safe hits and SP1 before triggering the timer, because a special knockdown can remove Mass and interrupt the anticipated damage sequence.

DECISION 3

Separate power-control immunity from every form of power denial

Galan has several named immunities to Power Drain, Burn, Lock and Steal. A node that prevents power gain or changes how power is earned is not necessarily the same thing; confirm its exact mechanic before choosing him.

Attacker starting point
Critical Rating

Galan builds Planetary Mass before beginning Harvest and cashes out through a special-driven sequence. Critical Rating is only a trial for eligible direct hits during that preparation and execution; it should not be described as increasing fixed Harvest payouts or the Mass gained from Armor Up. Compare equal starting Mass, buff state and SP1 timing, with Special Damage as the separate direct-hit alternative.

Alternative to test: Not reviewed

Defender starting point
Energy Resistance

Energy Resistance is an incoming-damage mitigation choice for energy-heavy attackers, not an enhancement to Harvest, Mass accumulation or Galan's own resistance buffs. Compare Physical Resistance against a physical-damage attacker; keep attacker class, special usage and defensive buff cycle consistent rather than declaring one setting universally best.
