TACTIC 1

Build Necrotic Energy through eligible combat

Deathless Thanos uses Necrotic Energy for his offensive engine. Check the current charge and whether the opponent permits the required interactions before pushing a long combo for extra energy.

TACTIC 2

Combine Fury with Armor Shattered only if it sticks

Armor Shattered and Fury can support a stronger attack when both are present. Verify the target's armor and immunity, and preserve room to bait their special before starting another resource-conversion sequence.

TACTIC 3

Spend the developed state on a Corrosion payoff

The current resource combination can support Corrosion in a later special. Confirm the effect and choose a safe punish; against Corrosion or Armor Break immunity, a smaller reliable hit may be the better route.

TACTIC 4

Avoid confusing Deathless and base Thanos

Deathless Thanos is a separate champion from base Thanos and his boss version. Necrotic Energy, Counterpunch and Armor Shattered do not confer universal survival or guaranteed power control.
