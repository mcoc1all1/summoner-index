TACTIC 1

Check Brother Daniel's possession state

Doctor Voodoo's attack and spell behavior changes with his Brother Daniel rhythm. Look at the relevant state before spending power; following a special rotation without tracking possession can produce the opposite control or damage outcome.

TACTIC 2

Choose SP1 for a buff-heavy opponent

SP1 can interact with opponent buffs through his Mystic utility. Confirm that the defender has ordinary, removable buffs and isn't protected by Nullify immunity; a special spent on passive effects may not produce the same control.

TACTIC 3

Use SP2 only when the relevant setup exists

His Poison and power-control routes depend on current states and the opponent's susceptibility. Check whether the defender can receive the intended effect before saving for SP2, and preserve a safe punish against a fast power-gain node.

TACTIC 4

Do not overstate regeneration or immunity

Doctor Voodoo's sustain and signature-based resource effects vary by awakening and timing. Treat Poison immunity, Purify and buff denial separately, and avoid assuming the Loa interaction prevents every source of damage.
