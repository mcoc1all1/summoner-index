TACTIC 1

Use heavy to establish pressure

A charged heavy during the proper short opening can help build Confidence through the subsequent Intimidate exchange. Don't assume a free heavy exists whenever Gladiator's power bar is ready.

TACTIC 2

Build Confidence before damage

The next special has more value once Confidence has developed. Watch the actual charge and opponent behavior instead of forcing a heavy if they are simply holding block or baiting you.

TACTIC 3

SP2 after a controlled heavy

An established Confidence setup can support a heavy-to-SP2 damage window, including useful Unblockable or Incinerate effects. If spacing is poor, use the special normally rather than risking a counterattack.

TACTIC 4

Respect defense and immunity conditions

Confidence and Vigilance do not make every defensive ability disappear. Incinerate immunity, anti-Unblockable tools and nodes that change heavy recovery alter the fight substantially.
