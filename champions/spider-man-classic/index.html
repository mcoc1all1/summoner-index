TACTIC 1

Read the actual Spider-Sense opening

Classic Spider-Man has an Evade-oriented defensive identity, but the current chance and triggering attack must be confirmed in the live kit. Start with short reliable basic attacks rather than assuming his defensive Evade will create a guaranteed opening.

TACTIC 2

Choose a debuff plan only when the target permits it

Older community proposals describe selectable Venom-Web or Sticky-Web modes, but those are hypothetical revamps. Check which web-related effects Classic Spider-Man actually applies and whether the defender can retain them before building a combo around them.

TACTIC 3

Use a special after a real punish window

A special is safest when the defender has finished its own attack. If the target's power is close to a dangerous threshold, bait that special rather than trying to trigger a random Evade; compare the current debuff state with the next attack.

TACTIC 4

Don't mistake Evade for reliable unblockable defense

True Strike, True Accuracy, Slow and other anti-Evade tools can make a historical defensive strategy unreliable. Distinguish Classic Spider-Man from Miles Morales and Spider-Gwen when assessing anti-Miss, movement and special interactions.
