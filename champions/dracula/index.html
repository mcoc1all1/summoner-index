Champion-specific fight decisions

Champion-specific setup, burst, matchup and failure-condition guidance independently written from the research catalog. These decisions are not site-tested clears; confirm the current live kit and node before relying on them. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

TACTIC 1

Activate Dire Degeneration deliberately

Dracula's damage route depends on Dire Degeneration being active before the next attack sequence. Confirm the intended status and available meter; committing to a long setup without it may waste the most useful offensive window.

TACTIC 2

Keep pressure while the degeneration persists

Use safe basic attacks to maintain pressure after activation, watching whether the target can remove or resist the effect. Slow and Nullify serve different control functions; verify which actually applies.

TACTIC 3

Spend a special during the prepared damage window

Choose the next special while Dire Degeneration can still contribute. Bait the defender's attack if required and check whether other current effects reinforce the payoff; don't treat Degeneration as guaranteed against every boss.

TACTIC 4

Check debuff form and power-control assumptions

Purify, degeneration resistance and anti-Mystic nodes can disrupt the plan. Slow immunity and Nullify immunity may block separate answers; this is not a blanket restriction on all Unstoppable or buffs.
