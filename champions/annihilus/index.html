INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Confirm Cosmic Control Rod status first

Annihilus's Armor Break and Stifle pressure depends on his Cosmic Control Rod state. If the Rod is absent or suppressed, expected effects change; check the visible icon before following a familiar debuff plan.

DECISION 2

Use SP1 to manage active Stifles

A timed SP1 can refresh or pause Stifle while preparing a larger payoff. First verify those effects are present on the defender; debuff prevention or a disruptive node can otherwise waste the Special.

DECISION 3

Cash out with SP2 only at favorable spacing

The Stifle-driven SP2 plan is more attractive when the opponent is near the wall, but cornering a defender also narrows your escape options. Commit only with manageable power and a reliable punish opening.
