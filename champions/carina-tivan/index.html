DECISION 1

Check Power Gain before the opener

Before bringing Carina Tivan into this fight, inspect Armor Break, Corrosion, Myriad and Power Gain in the live kit and read the quest node. Start with one safe basic-attack punish and observe which listed mechanic actually activates. Treat this as a research-derived starting checklist, not a verified opening rotation.

DECISION 2

Develop Power Gain and monitor Armor Break

The catalog identifies Power Gain as a potential power or control interaction for Carina Tivan. Confirm the exact trigger and duration in the live kit; do not assume this mechanic stops every special or every source of power gain. Track Armor Break separately: the appearance of one ability does not prove that the second triggers on the same attack or persists for the same duration. Reset spacing before committing to another sequence.

DECISION 3

Choose a safe special payoff

Choose an SP1, SP2 or SP3 only after checking the current meter, actual active effects and whether the defender has a safe punish window. For Carina Tivan, the index highlights Power Gain alongside Armor Break, but it does not demonstrate a validated special order or measured damage outcome. If the opponent is close to a dangerous special, bait that attack before attempting a longer payoff.

DECISION 4

Handle Power Gain failure conditions

When Power Gain does not control the defender under this node, bait a normal special and return to ordinary recovery punishes instead of taking damage while waiting for the missing effect. No independently confirmed protection is established by this checklist; check incoming damage types before deliberately trading hits. Recheck the defender after a special, cleanse or node transition, and avoid treating this research draft as a guaranteed counter to a named champion.
