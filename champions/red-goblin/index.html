INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Choose the combo ender for a relevant personal buff

Red Goblin can develop Energize, Fury or Aptitude from different basic finishing attacks. Decide whether power, offense or burst scaling matters before triggering the next Special rather than collecting buffs without purpose.

DECISION 2

Use Heavy or SP2 for Carnage Bomb setup only when safe

The available buff mix can contribute to damaging Carnage Bomb effects through Heavy or SP2. Check damage-effect immunity and find a real punish window; a rushed Heavy can waste more than the additional debuff provides.

DECISION 3

Use SP1 with developed debuffs or SP3 for Frenzy in longer fights

SP1 can gain value from several active debuff types, while SP3 can enable a more committed Frenzy route. If effects are cleansed or the fight will end soon, choose an earlier direct damage option.
