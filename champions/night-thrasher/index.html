TACTIC 1

Choose the Gadget before SP1

Night Thrasher's SP1 gadget effect depends on the input made immediately before the special. Identify whether the next fight needs a damaging effect or a control debuff; do not mash the same combo ending and expect a different gadget.

TACTIC 2

Preserve active Gadgets through specials

The 2025 rebirth update lengthened Gadget Debuff duration and pauses them during specials. Track each distinct gadget on the defender and time the follow-up without needlessly letting the setup expire between attacks.

TACTIC 3

Use SP2 when the setup is ready

SP2 Precision and unique gadget debuffs can reinforce his offensive payoff. Decide whether you have enough time for another SP1 setup or should cash out immediately, especially against defenders who remove debuffs rapidly.

TACTIC 4

Cleanse and Taunt are conditional

The rebirth change adds debuff purification when Night Thrasher knocks down the opponent, while Signature Taunt and Fury incentives have separate prerequisites. Don't confuse that conditional Cleanse with constant immunity or a guaranteed special bait.

Our defender pick: Physical resistance can protect Night Thrasher while he switches Gadgets or powers up; block penetration matters more if the attacker blocks his precision-enhanced specials. Compare Physical Resistance against Block Penetration versus the same attacker and mode before investing.
