Where the kit helps

Bleed and Incinerate immunity provide hazard coverage. Five Judgments trigger a Power Lock stage followed by Armor Break conversion.

WATCH OUT

Limits & caveats

Fate Seal clears Judgments and prevents new ones while active. A memorized rotation may change when nodes or teammates supply extra buffs.

INVESTMENT

Before ranking up

Awakening
Awakening upgrades the five-Judgment Power Lock into Damnation, adding damage, Heal Block and Fate Seal. The base Judgment damage cycle is separate.

TACTIC 1

Order Judgments instead of button-mashing

A medium-ending combo and a light-ending combo develop different buffs. Heavy charging and SP2 add others, but an early Dexterity-triggered Precision may change the next Judgment; read the icons before the burst.

TACTIC 2

Make the heavy safe

Use a known punish or recovery window to gain Aptitude from the heavy. Charging one against a free opponent can waste the entire Judgment setup before you even reach SP2.

TACTIC 3

Turn the completed set into Armor Break

After enough distinct Judgments, the kit can convert them into Armor Break pressure. The payoff depends on the active debuffs and buffs at that moment; do not assume every opponent takes the same burst.

TACTIC 4

Handle buff disruption explicitly

Fate Seal, Buff Immunity, shortened buffs or Armor Break immunity can interrupt the expected sequence. If a needed Judgment cannot be built, adopt a shorter damage route rather than repeating the same combo blindly.
