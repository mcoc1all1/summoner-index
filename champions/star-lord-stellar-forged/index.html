TACTIC 1

Build persistent Cruelty at the required combo milestones

Star-Lord (Stellar Forged) rewards combo progression through Cruelty. Verify the exact milestone and current persistent state rather than copying ordinary Tech Star-Lord's signature combo-scaling advice.

TACTIC 2

Protect the ramp with the available Combo Shield

A Combo Shield can make certain mistakes less costly, but only while present and under its current conditions. Watch enemy power and shielding before attempting an extended combo with a risky defender.

TACTIC 3

Spend SP2 when the Cruelty setup benefits

Choose an SP2 punish while the developed Cruelty and Precision resources can contribute. Check the target's critical resistance, and avoid forcing an unnecessary extra combo milestone when a safe finish is available.

TACTIC 4

Keep Stellar-Forged and Classic versions distinct

This Skill champion does not share Classic Star-Lord's exact Tech gun or signature assumptions. Critical damage protection, combo loss and node chip damage can erase the advantage of an extended ramp.
