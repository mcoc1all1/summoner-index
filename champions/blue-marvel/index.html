INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Start Overload with a safe Heavy before expecting the ramp

Blue Marvel can apply Overload through Heavy attacks and extend the setup with a timed block input. Confirm the effect and spacing first, especially when an aggressive defender can punish the Heavy or negate the debuff.

DECISION 2

Use SP1 Provoke and Wither for a control objective

SP1 can supply Provoke, Wither and help maintain debuff pressure while charges develop. Check whether the opponent's power gain is actually affected and whether Provoke makes the next Special bait safer.

DECISION 3

Wait for Stable Release before the stronger SP2 conversion

SP2 can reward the Stable Release state and its timing interactions, while SP3 offers a longer ramp. In a brief Battlegrounds-style fight, this setup may take too long; do not project fully ramped damage onto a fresh fight.
