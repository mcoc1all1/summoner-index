INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Check whether the defender is actually blocking

Hulkbuster gains stronger special effects when specials break Block. Throwing SP1 or SP2 into a wide-open opponent may deal damage but will not necessarily trigger the enhanced Stun or power-control sequence.

DECISION 2

Use Armor Up as a defensive budget

Armor boosts Hulkbuster's protection and his value in some nodes, but armor removal changes both the damage taken and the available synergies. Don't overstate self-repair or protection when the opponent can strip those buffs.

DECISION 3

Choose power denial with knowledge of immunity

SP2 can generate Power Burn in a block-break setup and interact with Power Lock, but defenders immune to particular forms of control will not follow the usual script. Confirm which effect actually applied before attacking again.

Comparison priority: compare · Statcast attribute to investigate: Critical Rating

Low base Critical Rating makes a same-rank, same-matchup Statcast Critical Rating trial meaningful, but a direct-hit improvement must be distinguished from Shock and Power Burn damage.

Battlecast comparison: The Hulkbuster Battlecast is worth comparing only with its actual selected rune and an attack sequence that can satisfy the trigger; a Striker alone is not evidence of extra power control.

Test plan: Compare equal Armor Up and Shock setups against one defender; record SP1 into block control, SP2 damage and health lost using each relic at the same rank.
