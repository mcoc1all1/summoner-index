TACTIC 1

Identify the available spell before building Soul Wisps

Pixie cycles spells and can produce Soul Wisps as an offensive resource. Check which spell is currently selected and what conditions create Wisps rather than expecting a specific spell on every opening.

TACTIC 2

Build Prowess and Wisps while controlling power

Track Soul Wisps and any Prowess or Sleep effect during safe attacks. If a defender resists Sleep, do not assume the next heavy or combo will become an uninterruptible setup.

TACTIC 3

Convert through SP2 while the Wisp state exists

A developed Wisp and Prowess state can strengthen SP2, including Neuroshock when eligible. Confirm the target's immunity and launch from a real recovery window instead of waiting for a maximum that may not occur.

TACTIC 4

Respect Sleep and Neuroshock counterplay

Stun/Sleep restrictions and immunity to specific damage forms can prevent the expected payoff. A high Wisp count does not guarantee defense against passive damage or independent node power.
