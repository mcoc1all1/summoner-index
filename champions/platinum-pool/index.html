TACTIC 1

Read the drawn card before choosing the opening

Platinum Pool changes his available advantages through cards. Identify the active card and the opponent's current node before settling on a Bleed or Prowess plan; one fight's favorable draw need not recur.

TACTIC 2

Use SP2 only when more cards are worth the risk

A safe SP2 can improve card options, but the value depends on the current hand and the opponent's power. Do not force a two-bar special merely to chase a card that the current encounter does not require.

TACTIC 3

Spend the useful card's offensive window

When the right Prowess, Bleed or Stun resource exists, launch the best special from a genuine recovery. If a drawn effect is irrelevant to the defender, use an earlier safe direct-hit punish.

TACTIC 4

Avoid promising a fixed card sequence

Card randomness, Stun immunity, Bleed immunity and Heal Block can remove different benefits. Platinum Pool's regeneration and comic-style kit are not interchangeable with Goldpool or Deadpool.

7★ stat focus · eligibility verified

Attacker starting point
Critical Rating

Platinum Pool has recorded bleed and prowess mechanics, but those labels alone do not establish a damage multiplier or a guaranteed activation. Test Critical Rating for eligible ordinary critical-hit frequency against Special Damage for a direct-special-hit comparison. Keep defender, node, ramp state and fight duration matched. In particular, do not infer that this stat automatically boosts every debuff tick, special side effect or personal resource.

Alternative to test: Not reviewed

Defender starting point
Physical Resistance

For Platinum Pool on PvP defense, examine Physical Resistance for damage from physical hits that connect despite defensive utility versus Block Penetration for pressure on opponents that rely on blocking. Recorded bleed and prowess do not guarantee that either stat will matter in every matchup. Compare equal-rank attacks against the same defender, distinguish damage dealt through block from damage resisted, and do not assume defensive bonuses are immunities.
