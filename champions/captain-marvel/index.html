INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Verify the Movie version before using Binary

The Captain Marvel roster entry here uses Binary Charges and Binary Ignition, unlike Captain Marvel (Classic). Gain charges through appropriate blocks or SP1, then activate Binary deliberately instead of using an older Fury-only rotation.

DECISION 2

Spend SP2 while Binary has time left

Binary can strengthen a follow-up Special, but charge timers and opposing power are live constraints. Choose the SP2 window when a counterattack is unlikely, and use a safe Heavy only if charges genuinely need extending.

DECISION 3

Do not give signature protection to unduped copies

Awakened Movie Captain Marvel can gain an Indestructible interaction around a sufficiently charged Binary entry. That protection has activation and timing conditions; it is neither permanent nor available on an unawakened copy.
