INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Repeat the correct basic attack to build Incinerate and Maniacal

Hobgoblin's attack rhythm can develop Incinerate and Maniacal charges through repeat hits and third Lights. Plan a safe combo and check which effect the defender can receive rather than defaulting to a full string regardless of immunity.

DECISION 2

Choose the SP1 setup that matches the preceding attack

His SP1 can contribute to charge development, with different utility tied to the preceding basic attack selection. Review the active resources and timing before spending power so the Special delivers the control needed.

DECISION 3

Spend a developed Lunatic Laugh window with a safe cancel

A Heavy charge can establish the Laugh setup for an SP2 follow-up. Confirm the defender is recovering and that the chosen damage effect is not protected; starting a vulnerable Heavy purely to maximize damage is not required.
