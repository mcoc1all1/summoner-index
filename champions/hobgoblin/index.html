TACTIC 1

Build Maniacal charges with controlled attacks

Hobgoblin uses Maniacal charges before a later Incinerate payoff. Track the live charge counter and defender power, because pushing a long combo for another charge can create a dangerous special threshold.

TACTIC 2

Prepare the Heavy-cancel special setup

His intended damage route involves a Heavy-to-special interaction. Use a real opening where the Heavy can begin safely; do not assume every defender is locked down just because the charge count is high.

TACTIC 3

Cash out with Incinerate during the enabled window

A developed Maniacal state may accelerate Incinerate on a special. Confirm the target can be Incinerated and check any available Heal Block or Energy Vulnerability before committing both the Heavy and meter.

TACTIC 4

Beware of Incinerate immunity and control interruption

A defender that Purifies debuffs or interrupts Heavy charging can defeat this plan. Steadfast is not invulnerability; verify exactly which incoming attack or control effect it protects against.

Attacker starting point
Special Damage

Our attacker pick: Maniacal charges prepare an accelerated heavy-cancel SP2, whose direct impact can benefit from Special Damage. Do not treat the accompanying Incinerate as automatically scaling with that stat. Compare Special Damage against Block Proficiency in the same fight at the same rank, awakening, relevant buffs, and node.

Defender starting point
Energy Resistance

Our defender pick: Energy resistance is relevant against energy-based counters trying to survive his Incinerate pressure; block penetration is the alternative when those attackers depend on guard. Compare Energy Resistance against Block Penetration versus the same attacker and mode before investing.
