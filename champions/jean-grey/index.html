Conditional fight checklist

Four-stage, profile-specific conditional research checklists generated from the site’s structured ability and protection records. These are not individually researched or playtested tactical guides and must not be taken as validated special rotations or matchup recommendations. DECISION 1

Check Neuroshock before the opener

Before bringing Jean Grey into this fight, inspect Daunted, Delirium, Neuroshock and Purify in the live kit and read the quest node. Start with one safe basic-attack punish and observe which listed mechanic actually activates. Treat this as a research-derived starting checklist, not a verified opening rotation.

DECISION 2

Develop Neuroshock and monitor Daunted

For Jean Grey, Neuroshock is a recorded damage interaction, but its damage form and stacking rules are not independently validated here. Check the defender for relevant immunity, Purify or resistance before trying to maintain uptime. Track Daunted separately: the appearance of one ability does not prove that the second triggers on the same attack or persists for the same duration. Reset spacing before committing to another sequence.

DECISION 3

Choose a safe special payoff

Choose an SP1, SP2 or SP3 only after checking the current meter, actual active effects and whether the defender has a safe punish window. For Jean Grey, the index highlights Neuroshock alongside Daunted, but it does not demonstrate a validated special order or measured damage outcome. If the opponent is close to a dangerous special, bait that attack before attempting a longer payoff.

DECISION 4

Handle Neuroshock failure conditions

When the target blocks Neuroshock through immunity, resistance or purification, prefer a shorter direct-hit route instead of holding power for an effect that never applies. No independently confirmed protection is established by this checklist; check incoming damage types before deliberately trading hits. Recheck the defender after a special, cleanse or node transition, and avoid treating this research draft as a guaranteed counter to a named champion.
