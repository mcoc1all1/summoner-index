TACTIC 1

Stack passive Incinerates on eligible attacks

Negasonic Teenage Warhead applies passive Incinerates as part of her offense. Check the target's protection against passive Incinerate rather than treating it identically to a purifiable ordinary debuff.

TACTIC 2

Use Light attacks to maintain the stack

Her kit can maintain Incinerates through eligible Light attacks. Use a controlled hit sequence and watch duration; extending a combo for another Light is not worth pushing a dangerous defender to SP3.

TACTIC 3

Spend the special when vulnerability can amplify damage

Incinerate Vulnerability and the existing stack can support a useful burst. Confirm both are active and the defender is susceptible before committing to a higher-power special.

TACTIC 4

Check anti-Evade and passive-damage counters

Her defensive Evade has particular availability, not unconditional safety. Passive Incinerate immunity and a target's energy protection can reduce damage, while a node may punish defensive triggers.

7★ stat focus · eligibility verified

Attacker starting point
Block Proficiency

Our attacker pick: Passive Incinerates are accumulated through contact and light attacks, so extra critical chance cannot simply be counted as extra Incinerate damage. Block Proficiency keeps the repeated setup safer. Compare Block Proficiency against Critical Rating in the same fight at the same rank, awakening, relevant buffs, and node.

Alternative to test: Not reviewed

Defender starting point
Energy Resistance

Our defender pick: Energy resistance reduces incoming energy-hit damage in matchups where her Incinerates do not deter the attacker; block penetration is an alternative against blocked special sequences. Compare Energy Resistance against Block Penetration versus the same attacker and mode before investing.
