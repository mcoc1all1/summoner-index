INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-04; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Know when Glancing is protecting you

Sersi can activate Glancing around a Heavy charge, but it is not a shield against every passive or damage-over-time interaction. Confirm the opponent's damage type before relying on it as your only safety plan.

DECISION 2

Convert Transmutation with positioning

Medium endings help build Transmutation; close-range specials can convert the resource toward Fury or True Damage. Choosing the wrong distance may refresh a different effect and delay the burst.

DECISION 3

Refresh buffs without feeding the wrong defender

Far-range SP1 can maintain useful buffs, but the opponent may punish or remove them using Stagger or Fate Seal. Read those enemy tools before building an otherwise strong buff engine.
