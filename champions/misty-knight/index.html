TACTIC 1

Check the cybernetic charge state

Misty Knight develops specialized charge-based pressure involving her metal arm and cryogenic effects. Watch the actual charge and defensive icons before a heavy or special; the fight may not start with the same resources as an earlier matchup.

TACTIC 2

Use Cold effects deliberately

Crouching behind a generic Bleed or energy-damage tag misses her control options. Verify whether the opponent can receive the relevant Cold or Coldsnap effect and whether their Evade mechanics are actually affected before treating it as an anti-Evade solution.

TACTIC 3

Select a safe special conversion

Spend power when the relevant charge state is developed and the defender is recovering. Against an opponent with repeated Purify, a shorter direct-hit plan may produce more value than saving meter for an effect that cannot remain active.

TACTIC 4

Read defensive immunity separately

Her defensive passives and cryogenic interactions do not grant blanket immunity to all incoming damage. Check the node's effect form, Armor Break and ability-accuracy restrictions, and do not assume every cybernetic effect works against all Robot champions.
