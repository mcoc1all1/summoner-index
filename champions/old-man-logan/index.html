TACTIC 1

Treat the self-Bleed as a resource, not incoming damage

Old Man Logan can accumulate harmless self-Bleed effects through eligible interactions. Inspect the self-Bleed count and its duration before converting it; do not intentionally accept an unsafe enemy Special Attack just to accelerate the buildup.

TACTIC 2

Convert self-Bleeds into Fury at a real opening

His Fury setup depends on turning developed self-Bleeds into offensive buffs. Check how many are available and whether the next Heavy or other trigger can be performed safely, then use the conversion rather than waiting for an unrealistic maximum.

TACTIC 3

Launch the special with useful Fury still active

A special during an established Fury window can be more valuable than one thrown before the conversion. Keep the defender's power manageable and choose a genuine recovery punish; buff timers and aggressive nodes can cut the window short.

TACTIC 4

Respect Heal Block and buff denial

His regeneration and offensive Fury are different advantages, both with counters. Neutralize, Nullify, Heal Block and Purify-related nodes may change the plan; verify today's kit instead of copying a Wolverine or Weapon X rotation.
