TACTIC 1

Select the Control Box mode for a specific enemy action

Psycho-Man's Control Box modes influence how the defender's actions are punished. Check whether the opponent tends to block, attack or use specials before choosing the relevant mode; random switching can waste the intended control window.

TACTIC 2

Build control through an observable trigger

A particular mode may enable Concussion, Shock or Power Drain. Confirm which effect actually applies after the corresponding action, and do not keep feeding opponent power simply to recreate an uncertain trigger.

TACTIC 3

Spend a special while the correct mode is engaged

Use a clean recovery punish that matches the selected Control Box state and intended effect. If the defender is immune to the planned debuff, choose a different mode rather than spending meter on an empty conversion.

TACTIC 4

Respect mode-specific and power-source limits

Concussion does not suppress every node, Power Drain may not affect independent Power Gain and Shock immunity changes damage. A control mode is not proof that the defender cannot retaliate.
