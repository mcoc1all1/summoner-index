TACTIC 1

Develop Heavy Proficiency on a safe opening

Doctor Bong's heavy-focused kit rewards Heavy Proficiency. Bait a defender special or punish a predictable Heavy before charging yours; avoid treating every blocked attack as a free opportunity.

TACTIC 2

Build Reverb without standing in danger

Doctor Bong gains Reverb Buffs from the separate hits of his Special Attacks, while activating a Special grants Heavy Proficiency. Reverb can shorten the Heavy Proficiency duration, although the special hits and Critical Hits can restore some time. Track the active Reverb count and Heavy Proficiency timer together before extending the fight; using Panic from SP1 is a different choice from simply stockpiling Reverb.

TACTIC 3

Use the developed Heavy damage window

Charging Doctor Bong's Heavy grants an Unblockable Buff for that attack, but it does not prevent an opponent from interrupting him. His SP1 or SP2 can Stun at nine or more Reverb Buffs, creating a more reliable punish than an unsafe neutral Heavy. Check the Reverb threshold, the Heavy Proficiency timer and the defender's Stun immunity first, then take the safe attack window rather than treating a listed Unblockable effect as full protection.

TACTIC 4

Watch Stun immunity and heavy-punish counters

Unblockable does not equal Unstoppable or invulnerability, and an opponent may still intercept a charged Heavy. Passive damage and ability-accuracy restrictions can change the risk of this plan.

Attacker starting point
Critical Damage Rating

Our attacker pick: Heavy Proficiency and Reverb lead to heavy-centered payoffs, and combo milestones can create guaranteed critical hits. Critical Damage Rating is more relevant than raising critical chance during those confirmed windows. Compare Critical Damage Rating against Armor Penetration in the same fight at the same rank, awakening, relevant buffs, and node.

Block Penetration

Our defender pick: His Reverb setup encourages attackers to retreat or block during heavy pressure. Block penetration tests chip in those defensive openings, with physical resistance reserved for direct-hit survival.
