DECISION 1

Check Bleed before the opener

Before bringing Korg into this fight, inspect Armor Break, Bleed, Rock Shield and Unblockable in the live kit and read the quest node. Start with one safe basic-attack punish and observe which listed mechanic actually activates. Treat this as a research-derived starting checklist, not a verified opening rotation.

DECISION 2

Develop Bleed and monitor Armor Break

For Korg, Bleed is a recorded damage interaction, but its damage form and stacking rules are not independently validated here. Check the defender for relevant immunity, Purify or resistance before trying to maintain uptime. Track Armor Break separately: the appearance of one ability does not prove that the second triggers on the same attack or persists for the same duration. Reset spacing before committing to another sequence.

DECISION 3

Choose a safe special payoff

Choose an SP1, SP2 or SP3 only after checking the current meter, actual active effects and whether the defender has a safe punish window. For Korg, the index highlights Bleed alongside Armor Break, but it does not demonstrate a validated special order or measured damage outcome. If the opponent is close to a dangerous special, bait that attack before attempting a longer payoff.

DECISION 4

Handle Bleed failure conditions

When the target blocks Bleed through immunity, resistance or purification, prefer a shorter direct-hit route instead of holding power for an effect that never applies. A structured Bleed protection is also listed; verify its availability, effect form and conditions instead of calling it unconditional immunity. Recheck the defender after a special, cleanse or node transition, and avoid treating this research draft as a guaranteed counter to a named champion.

Attacker starting point
Special Damage

Korg spends Crowd Excitement on his pressure cycle and can use Special 2 Bleed stacks while Rock Shield remains a separate defensive resource. Compare Special Damage with Critical Damage Rating on equivalent direct special hits and the same Crowd Excitement; do not infer stronger Bleed ticks, an earlier Unblockable state, or more Rock Shield from these stats.

Defender starting point
Physical Resistance

Physical Resistance is a candidate against incoming physical blows, but it should not be described as increasing Rock Hard Thorns reflection or refreshing Rock Shield charges. Compare Energy Resistance in a matched fight with energy-damage contact, because Korg's kit can be more exposed to that damage type.

Original relic attribute research · editorial trial

Comparison priority: compare · Statcast attribute to investigate: Physical Resistance

Physical Resistance is a defensive survival experiment against ordinary physical hits, separate from Korg's Rock Shield Thorns reflection, whose damage scales with base attack. It cannot create or replenish Rock Shield charges.

Battlecast comparison: A skill Battlecast could help Korg build Crowd Excitement or land an SP2 Bleed sequence if its rune allows it, but it cannot be assumed to improve his Rock Shield or activate defensively in PvP.

Test plan: Use one physical-contact attacker and one energy-contact attacker on identical nodes. Record shield recharge, incoming damage, Crowd Excitement, and SP2 Bleeds; compare a defensive Statcast against a rune-tested Battlecast on attack.

The Battlecast does not provide PvP defense effects; energy hits bypass Korg's usual Thorns interaction, and a generic physical-mitigation win must not be advertised as an unconditional counter. No exact Statcast statue or secondary variant has been verified here.
