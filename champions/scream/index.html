TACTIC 1

Build distinct Hybrid buffs before the special

Scream's damage plan develops Hybrid buffs and Madness-related resources. Check which personal buffs actually exist; one repeated buff is not necessarily equivalent to a diversified setup.

TACTIC 2

Use Bleed and Armor Break when the defender permits them

Her effects may help develop the next special, but a Bleed-immune or Armor Break-immune target can change the value dramatically. Monitor active debuffs and the opponent's special threshold as Madness builds.

TACTIC 3

Cash out with SP2 while the state is developed

The intended burst uses active Hybrid buffs, Madness and related Undermine or Fervor effects. Choose a safe opening, then confirm the target's immunity instead of assuming an SP2 at any moment yields peak damage.

TACTIC 4

Watch Nullify and Purify as separate threats

Nullify can remove buffs; Purify can erase damaging debuffs. This symbiote kit is distinct from Carnage or Gwenom, and a personal Fervor effect is not unconditional defense against passive damage.

Special Damage

Our attacker pick: Madness and Hybrid buffs prepare SP2 for a burst while Bleed and other debuffs form separate channels. Special Damage is the initial trial for the direct SP2 impact, with critical damage as a direct-hit alternative. Compare Special Damage against Critical Damage Rating in the same fight at the same rank, awakening, relevant buffs, and node.

Block Penetration

Our defender pick: The Hybrid buff cycle may force an opponent to guard more often.
