EDITORIAL FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Editorial decision notes derived from identified game mechanics and attributed source research; these are not in-game performance tests, guarantees, or permissions to reuse third-party text. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Build Madness while rotating the Hybrid buff you actually need

Scream's fourth-Light finishers refresh Madness and cycle the selected Hybrid buff; second-Medium hits can then collect the active choice. Check the current icon before spending attacks on a defensive buff when the next fight needs offense.

DECISION 2

Use SP1 to begin a prepared damage chain, not an empty burst

SP1 can convert Madness into Fervor pressure, while other attacks add Bleed and Armor Break. Monitor the effects and available power so the subsequent SP2 has sufficient debuffs to benefit from its burst mechanic.

DECISION 3

Cash SP2 while Madness and debuffs are available

SP2 consumes Madness and scales with personal debuffs, with an Unblockable condition at the necessary count. Check the count, Bleed immunity and defender power before assuming a maximum burst from every SP2.
