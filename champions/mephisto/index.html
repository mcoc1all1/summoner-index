Champion-specific fight decisions

Champion-specific setup, burst, matchup and failure-condition guidance independently written from the research catalog. These decisions are not site-tested clears; confirm the current live kit and node before relying on them. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

TACTIC 1

Track the Soul Imprisonment opening

Mephisto can use Soul Imprisonment to constrain certain opponent buff and soul interactions. Check that the defender can be affected and whether the suppression is active before assuming a power or regeneration mechanic is shut down.

TACTIC 2

Manage Aura of Incineration with spacing

Aura of Incineration can pressure a nearby enemy but does not make standing in front of a defender safe. Watch aura duration and opponent specials, then use controlled movement rather than forcing proximity during an unavoidable attack.

TACTIC 3

Decide when SP1 control is preferable

SP1-related Soul Imprisonment can be useful against buff-reliant targets, while a different special may better suit a direct-damage situation. If the opponent has no relevant removable effects, do not spend the whole fight waiting for that payoff.

TACTIC 4

Respect immunity and signature limitations

Incinerate resistance, power-related node effects and immunity to the intended debuff can reduce his value. Verify the current signature and defensive regeneration rules independently instead of assuming Mephisto cannot be hurt by every energy or passive effect.

Attacker starting point
Special Damage

Our attacker pick: Soul spending balances recovery and offensive specials, so Special Damage tests the immediately dealt special hit at equal Souls. It does not boost the emergency healing or Aura damage by assumption. Compare Special Damage against Critical Rating in the same fight at the same rank, awakening, relevant buffs, and node.
