TACTIC 1

Protect shield Durability

Use well-timed blocks and deliberate movement rather than spending shield resources on every attack. The value of the next SP2 depends on what remains of the shield when the special starts.

TACTIC 2

Apply Slow before Unstoppable

SP1 can establish Slow for matchups where stopping Unstoppable matters. Confirm that the target's Unstoppable is actually affected and that the Slow will remain active through your punish.

TACTIC 3

Cash out and recover

SP2 uses an intact shield for damage and can fracture it. Plan the next stage after the burst: a broken shield needs restoration rather than repeating an immediately weaker SP2.

TACTIC 4

Keep Trauma and Fragility alive

Safe heavies can refresh useful debuffs, while SP3 can restore the shield. Choose between maintaining pressure and spending time rebuilding based on the defender's actual openings, not a rigid fixed loop.

Attacker starting point
Special Damage

Our attacker pick: Shield Durability and Slow are developed before a damaging SP2. Special Damage is evaluated only for the direct SP2 impact at equal Durability; it is not a shortcut to extra Trauma or shield strength. Compare Special Damage against Block Proficiency in the same fight at the same rank, awakening, relevant buffs, and node.

Physical Resistance

Our defender pick: His shield and immunity conditions do not make every physical hit safe. Physical resistance is a sensible fallback against physical attackers during broken-shield windows. Compare Physical Resistance against Block Penetration versus the same attacker and mode before investing.
