INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Select the Trick Arrow for this defender

Start by naming what the matchup requires: Coldsnap to suppress Evade, Tranquilize to soften incoming debuff accuracy, or another arrow for damage. Her customizable utility is documented by Kabam; an arrow that cannot apply to this defender is not useful just because it looks strong on paper.

DECISION 2

Prove Perfect Release before investing in ramp

Practice the Release timing on SP1 and SP2. A missed release can change how many arrow effects you apply, so count the visible effects rather than assuming every special supplied the optimal stacks. Dialed In and the arrow debuffs are distinct resources.

DECISION 3

Keep the opponent where the refresh works

A wall-side Heavy can refresh existing arrows, but corner pressure is unsafe into Unstoppable, Stun immunity, or short recoveries. If you cannot maintain an opening, choose a shorter burst instead of sacrificing health to preserve a long-fight damage-over-time plan.
