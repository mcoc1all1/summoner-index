Champion-specific fight decisions

Champion-specific setup, burst, matchup and failure-condition guidance independently written from the research catalog. These decisions are not site-tested clears; confirm the current live kit and node before relying on them. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

TACTIC 1

Acquire Light Energy safely

Use well-timed blocks and the kit's buff-removal interaction to gain Light Energy. Check whether the defender's buffs and the active node actually permit your chosen source; never assume backdash conversion always works.

TACTIC 2

Convert with a protected heavy

The heavy-charge interaction turns Light into Dark Energy when properly timed around a basic attack. Set it up against a predictable attack instead of charging blindly near the wall.

TACTIC 3

Deliver the SP2 before expiry

Dark Energy is the resource behind a strong SP2 Degeneration burst. Confirm the active energy amount and timer before committing; an early or late special can waste the setup.

TACTIC 4

Respect energy and debuff counters

The plan becomes less attractive against Degeneration protection or defenders whose accuracy rules frustrate a heavy counter-Evade. If the energy conversion is unsafe, fall back to a simpler control route.
