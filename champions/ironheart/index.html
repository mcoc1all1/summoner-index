INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Choose the Repulsor effect before the fight

Ironheart's pre-fight allows distinct damage effects such as Incinerate or Rupture. Select it based on the defender's actual immunity and the node, then track applications instead of assuming both damage types are available simultaneously.

DECISION 2

Refresh debuffs without losing Armor Up

SP1 can pause or maintain Repulsor pressure while Light attacks help build Armor Up for an SP2 conversion. Armor Break can strip important protection; check current Armor Up before relying on her shield or blocked-hit benefits.

DECISION 3

Recognize Tracking as its own utility

Tracking gives anti-Miss interaction and may support extra blocking utility, but it is not a blanket counter to Evade, Unstoppable or every defensive trigger. Pick the right utility for the specific opponent mechanic.

Frostbite immunity requires an active Armor Up; it is not unconditional during Armor Break or other loss of Armor Up.

Reversed Controls
Scope not reviewed

Reversed Controls immunity requires an active Armor Up effect. Her indefinite Armor Up can be removed and its restoration delayed by Armor Break, so the protection is not guaranteed while Armor Up is absent.

Coldsnap
Scope not reviewed

Coldsnap immunity applies only while Ironheart has an active Armor Up. Armor Break can delay restoration of the indefinite Armor Up, creating a period without this immunity.

Resistance / scope note: Reversed Controls, Incinerate and cold immunities require an Armor Up effect.
