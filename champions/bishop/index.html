Champion-specific fight decisions

Champion-specific setup, burst, matchup and failure-condition guidance independently written from the research catalog. These decisions are not site-tested clears; confirm the current live kit and node before relying on them. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

TACTIC 1

Budget Prowess and power

Well-timed blocks help build Prowess; that creates pressure but can also approach an Overload threshold. Check the next bar of power and avoid blindly holding charges when a defender can force contact.

TACTIC 2

Pick SP1 or SP2 by fight

SP1 favors nearby Incinerate pressure, while SP2 supplies a larger burst and related follow-up. If the defender resists Incinerate, the optimal choice may shift toward the direct damage window.

TACTIC 3

Save recovery as an option

Holding Block can consume Prowess for recovery and Bleed Purify under the kit's conditions. That is a defensive trade-off, not an extra source of damage: spend offensive resources only when survival needs it.

TACTIC 4

Check persistent quest state

Bishop can carry power-related context between quest fights. Re-read his opening resources and the next node instead of assuming every encounter starts with the same Prowess and Overload pattern.

Editorial comparison: Bishop uses Prowess to strengthen a special-attack burst after building power through blocks and energy exposure. Critical Rating can be trialled for direct-hit crit frequency at equal Prowess, but cannot be treated as an automatic boost to his Incinerate or Overload damage. Compare the same starting power and Prowess.

Defender starting point
Special Damage

Special Damage on defense may increase eligible direct special hits only if the attacker is caught by them. It does not speed Bishop's persistent power, eliminate Overload risk or improve his passive Incinerate. Compare Block Proficiency when predictable blocked-hit survival is more relevant.
