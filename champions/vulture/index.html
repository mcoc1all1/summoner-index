TACTIC 1

Identify a genuine Thermal Feedback target

Vulture's power-management route depends on his Thermal Feedback and related energy interactions. Start by observing how the defender gains power; a node that supplies power through a separate rule may not respond the same way as normal combat power gain.

TACTIC 2

Manage the Chitauri resource

His Chitauri-related charges and special-attack effects are resources, not a universal background power lock. Track them during basic attacks so the next special is launched with the intended state rather than after the pressure window expires.

TACTIC 3

Choose an SP1 or SP2 for control

A special can convert the developed resource into a different form of power control or offensive pressure. Compare the defender's current bar and Special Attack animation before spending meter; baiting safely may be preferable to a second immediate special.

TACTIC 4

Recheck resistance and effect form

Power Drain immunity, Shock or energy protection, node-based power gain and ability-accuracy reduction can defeat the expected feedback loop. Do not assume every Robot defender automatically offers an ideal matchup simply because Vulture is Tech.
