TACTIC 1

Develop the Cold Aura and Armor Break connection

Vision (Aarkus) can pressure nearby opponents through Cold Aura, Armor Break and subsequent Coldsnap interactions. Check whether the target can receive Armor Break first; without that precursor, the expected Coldsnap route may not occur.

TACTIC 2

Use the current improved attack-side armor setup

Kabam's January 2026 preview announced faster, longer-lasting attacker Armor Break effects. Inspect the actual debuff duration and Armor Shattered state before applying a historical rotation based on shorter or slower pre-buff timers.

TACTIC 3

Pause the relevant states through SP1

The same preview described SP1 pausing Armor Shattered, Fury and Cold Aura in the updated kit. Launch only from a safe punish when those effects are active; a special thrown too early cannot extend a setup that has not developed.

TACTIC 4

Respect armor immunity and true damage counters

Armor Break-immune defenders may prevent the Coldsnap route, while opponents with reliable buff control or suitable protection can reduce the value of his Power Gain. Don't assume a Cold Aura label alone wins against every evade or stun-immune node.
