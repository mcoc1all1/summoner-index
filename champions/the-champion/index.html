INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Plan around Persistent Charges and opponent buff removal

The Champion builds persistent resources over time, but enemy combo progress and Nullify can disrupt them. Track the count rather than treating the high-charge Fury and Unstoppable cycle as available at the opening bell.

DECISION 2

Use intercept utility only after its charge requirement

Certain Light and Medium intercepts can offer True Accuracy or Armor Break after enough Persistent Charges. Check both the charge count and the enemy's avoidance or armor mechanics before taking an unsafe intercept.

DECISION 3

Choose SP1 healing or SP2 critical damage based on the fight

An eligible SP1 can spend charges on Regeneration, while SP2 can turn a developed resource bank into a damage window. The Champion's final-node benefits are separate conditions, not guaranteed in every attacker assignment.
