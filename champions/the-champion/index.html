These decisions are not site-tested clears; confirm the current live kit and node before relying on them. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

TACTIC 1

Track persistent charges against the right foe

The Champion earns persistent resources that strengthen his Fury-related pressure over a quest. Check the current charge count and whether this opponent meets the kit's stronger-fighter conditions before entering; not every win yields identical buildup.

TACTIC 2

Develop Fury without taking unnecessary contact

When the relevant Fury window activates, use safe attacks and keep the defender's power manageable. A prolonged block trade or an over-aggressive dash can remove the advantage if the next enemy special is already threatening.

TACTIC 3

Spend a special while the buffs are useful

A short damage sequence within active Fury may outperform a delayed special after the buffs expire. Watch current resource and buff duration instead of prescribing an old fixed number of hits for every quest node.

TACTIC 4

Account for Nullify and persistent charge loss

Certain incoming hits or Nullify effects can remove The Champion's persistent resources under the publisher's kit rules. Avoid treating the charge-based damage plan as permanent or assuming his defensive Indestructible state is always available.
