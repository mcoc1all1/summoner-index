TACTIC 1

Count the Poison exposure

Toad's Poison pressure rewards attention to which effects have actually landed and how long they remain active. Use safe basic-attack openings first rather than assuming that every defender can be poisoned or that a large damage outcome will arrive automatically.

TACTIC 2

Track the passive and attack state

His offensive damage and defensive retaliation can change with active personal states. Check the current ability text and stack indicators before attempting a riskier heavy or special conversion; older footage may show different values after the announced rebalance review.

TACTIC 3

Pick the special when the debuffs stick

If the opponent is susceptible to Poison and keeps the stacks long enough, a special can capitalize on that state. Against fast Purify or Poison immunity, avoid spending a full two-bar setup on a damage-over-time payoff that cannot develop.

TACTIC 4

Treat 2026 rebalance details as version-sensitive

The publisher named Toad among champions in a rebalance batch. Until the exact live-kit implementation is independently confirmed, do not present pre-update percentages, timers or fixed rotation counts as current facts. Bring a different matchup answer when Poison is denied.
