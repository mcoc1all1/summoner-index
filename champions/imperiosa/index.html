INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Clear the opening Special Lock before planning a normal rotation

Imperiosa starts with a special power situation and an opening self-lock that requires eligible actions to clear. Watch the lock state instead of assuming a Special is immediately usable because the power bar appears partially filled.

DECISION 2

Choose SP3 Corrosion for an encounter long enough to refresh it

An SP3 can introduce Corrosion, while SP1 can refresh relevant damaging effects under the recorded kit. Against a short encounter or corrosion-resistant target, holding three bars may not be worth the risk.

DECISION 3

Use SP2 Root while managing fractured power thresholds

SP2 can Root the opponent and change which power-dependent buffs are active. Check the resulting bar and the defender's Special threat before staying close for a follow-up; Root does not automatically suppress enemy power.
