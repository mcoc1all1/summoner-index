TACTIC 1

Balance Incinerate against fuel

Sunspot needs Solar Charges to maintain Flare State. Use Incinerates and safe heavies to reclaim resources; a reckless attack cycle can leave insufficient charge for the next useful special.

TACTIC 2

Choose SP1 only when sustainable

SP1 builds a stronger Flare setup, but each stage increases the resource demand. Before repeating it, look at remaining Solar Charges and whether the defender can still be Incinerated.

TACTIC 3

Cash out with SP2

An SP2 after a developed setup can reward the investment. If charge supply is low, take the damage opportunity rather than extending Flare purely to reach an idealized stack count.

TACTIC 4

Reset carefully on long fights

SP3 can recover Solar Charges, but it costs meter and timing. Incinerate immunity, aggressive special denial or nodes that punish heavy charging can force a shorter and less flashy plan.

Attacker starting point
Special Damage

Sunspot spends Solar Charges to maintain Flare State, then builds Prowess for a Special 2 damage window. Special Damage should be compared with Critical Damage Rating at equal Flare stacks, Solar Charges and critical outcomes; neither option creates charges, avoids Shock ending Flare, or can be assumed to multiply all Incinerate ticks. Compare the complete SP2 damage and subsequent Incinerate damage in separate columns over identical fight length.

Defender starting point
Block Proficiency

Block Proficiency is relevant only on defender blocks and must not be confused with Sunspot's Perfect Block interaction against Incinerated opponents. Compare Physical Resistance when the attacking champion instead lands physical hits into openings; match the same nodes and record blocked versus unblocked damage.

Original relic attribute research · editorial trial

Comparison priority: compare · Statcast attribute to investigate: Special Damage

Sunspot's Flare State and Prowess make a charged SP2 direct-hit comparison useful, provided the Solar Charge budget is held fixed. A larger connected special does not imply stronger Incinerate ticks.

Battlecast comparison: A Battlecast striker may extend a Flare combo or create a safe reset, but using it can change the number of Solar Charges spent. Compare only after confirming the equipped rune can activate with the planned combo.

Test plan: Choose one non-Shock defender, identical mastery and Solar Charge starting state. Record SP2 direct hit, Incinerate damage, final Solar Charges and full fight time for both relic choices.

Do not call any Battlecast the best choice without its actual rune and class binding; a Shock or high-Purify opponent invalidates a simple damage comparison. No exact Statcast statue or secondary variant has been verified here.
