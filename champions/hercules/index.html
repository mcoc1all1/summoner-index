EDITORIAL FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Editorial decision notes derived from identified game mechanics and attributed source research; these are not in-game performance tests, guarantees, or permissions to reuse third-party text. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Take feats through safe skill checks

Hercules' Strength rewards intercepts and heavies against unstunned opponents. Building those feats is valuable, but deliberately taking a hit for an opening is a poor trade against damaging nodes. Get the Feat only when the enemy's timing makes it safe.

DECISION 2

Use specials with a chosen buff payoff

SP1 offers Precision and True Sense; SP2 can follow with Cruelty and burst. The special-intercept bonus requires actual timing and spacing, so take a regular safe special launch when attempting the intercept would cost a revive.

DECISION 3

Never promise Immortality against SP3

Kabam clarified that a Special 3 can remove his signature Immortality and that the interaction operates as intended. Awakening is required for the protection, the buff can be removed, and you should not use it as an unconditional answer to a defender's SP3.
