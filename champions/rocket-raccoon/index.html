TACTIC 1

Set up the damage without treating him as durable

Rocket Raccoon can deal substantial attack damage but is vulnerable to dangerous contact. Start with controlled spacing and short punishes; a long combo attempt into an aggressive defender may cost more health than its payoff.

TACTIC 2

Use Bleed only when the defender permits it

Some of his basic and special attacks can inflict Bleed or other effects under the current kit. Check the actual target immunity rather than assuming the damage-over-time portion always contributes to his burst.

TACTIC 3

Choose an offensive special around the opening

A special launched from a genuine recovery is more valuable than one delayed simply to chase another critical hit. Watch the opponent's bar and make sure Rocket has enough distance to avoid the counterattack after the animation.

TACTIC 4

Keep base kit and synergy bonuses separate

Synergies may materially affect his damage, critical-hit behavior or utility. Confirm the team and current champion version before recommending a recorded damage number, and do not mistake Robot-related tags for automatic defensive immunity.
