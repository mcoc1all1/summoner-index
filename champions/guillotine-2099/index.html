INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Decide whether to spend Digi-Souls at the pre-fight

Guillotine 2099's selectable functions have real opportunity costs, including protection and recovery resources. Check whether the encounter calls for a specific function rather than activating every available option automatically.

DECISION 2

Treat the combo meter as a path resource

Combo Shields and controlled power denial can keep the longer-fight engine intact. If you want the next fight to start with a developed combo, arrange the SP3 finish while the defender is low enough for it to secure a knockout.

DECISION 3

Recheck the SP3 carryover instead of promising it blindly

Older community reports describe occasions when an SP3 finish did not deliver the expected next-fight combo. Treat those as historical bug reports, not current proof of failure, and confirm the present in-game behavior on critical paths.
