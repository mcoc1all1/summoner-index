TACTIC 1

Treat the combo meter as persistent value

Guillotine 2099 can build enormous combo-based damage over a quest. Preserve the current count through careful recovery punishes rather than forcing extra hits into a node that can wipe the entire ramp with one mistake.

TACTIC 2

Plan the SP3 finish instead of assuming it

Her quest progression can carry combo value when the previous fight is ended using the required SP3 condition. Check the target's actual remaining health and your available meter; do not sacrifice the fight pursuing an SP3 that will fail to finish.

TACTIC 3

Decide whether to spend a pre-fight upgrade

Her pre-fight choices trade offensive properties against defensive value from unused resources. Read the next defender's critical-hit and armor rules before consuming a charge solely to reproduce a high-damage screenshot.

TACTIC 4

Keep combo-shield history distinct from live rules

An old reported Dexterity-triggered combo-shield bug was later listed as fixed in official change notes. Do not rely on early videos as proof of present behavior; check signature status and the actual shield icon during the next fight.
