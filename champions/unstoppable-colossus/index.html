TACTIC 1

Establish Fury without forcing unsafe contact

Unstoppable Colossus can build Fury through basic attacks. Watch its active duration and the defender's meter; a developing Fury state is not worth letting the opponent obtain an unavoidable special.

TACTIC 2

Use an awakened Unstoppable window precisely

His Unstoppable behavior has signature and timing conditions. Confirm the effect is actually active before attempting an aggressive sequence, and remember Unstoppable does not prevent the damage from a hit.

TACTIC 3

Spend a special while Fury can contribute

A safe special during an active Fury window may be better than prolonging the setup. Check the target's armor or damage resistance and use a genuine opponent recovery rather than assuming Unstoppable provides a free launch.

TACTIC 4

Watch Slow, Nullify and Armor Break restrictions

Slow can affect Unstoppable and buff removal can erase Fury; Armor Up may provide separate protection but not immunity. This Mystic variant differs from original Mutant Colossus in both kit and matchup.
