INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-04; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Manage the random Genetic Memory set

Venom's Klyntar Mutation adds Genetic Memory buffs over time. A Heavy can reroll the newest one, so use it to pursue a useful buff instead of assuming your opening buffs will match every opponent.

DECISION 2

Know the cost of the SP2 finish

SP2 trades accumulated Genetic Memory buffs for a shorter Fury-oriented burst. That may suit a low-health finish, but it also means rebuilding afterward; compare that choice against continued buff stacking in longer fights.

DECISION 3

Check Klyntar dependence

His Spider-Verse Evade control and other opponent-specific interactions rely on Klyntar conditions. Incinerate or buff removal can interrupt those advantages; SP1's buff theft and healing also depend on suitable opposing buffs.

7★ stat focus · eligibility verified

Attacker starting point
Critical Rating

Venom's critical hits can apply Bleed, while a Precision Genetic Memory Buff already increases his chance to crit. Critical Rating is worth testing for eligible attack frequency and the resulting Bleed opportunities, but its incremental value may shrink when Precision is active or the opponent cannot Bleed. Keep the same Genetic Memory set and rotation when comparing Critical Damage Rating as an alternative.

Alternative to test: Not reviewed

Defender starting point
Armor Penetration

Armor Penetration is an outgoing-damage trial against armored attackers, not an incoming-damage shield. Venom's own Armor Up and Physical Resistance buffs depend on his Genetic Memory cycle; the focus does not generate or preserve those buffs. Compare Block Proficiency if the defender is losing health while blocking, and separate that result from the matchup's armor level.
