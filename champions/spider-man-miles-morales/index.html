INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Progress the correct challenges before promising full Camo utility

Miles can develop different challenges using well-timed blocks, intercepts and dodged attacks. Check each current objective rather than treating every combo as progress; the full Special-triggered Camo plan depends on completing them.

DECISION 2

Bait a manageable Special to enter Camo

Opponent Specials can trigger Camo, opening an opportunity for a Heavy Taunt and Rupture plan. Check the opponent's anti-Miss or True Strike and available space, since Camo is not blanket immunity to the next attack.

DECISION 3

Spend SP2 only with a developed personal-debuff mix

SP2 can benefit from Camo and several applied personal debuffs, while SP1 offers Weakness utility. Against Rupture-resistant or cleansing defenders, a shorter control route can be safer than holding two bars for a weakened burst.
