Champion-specific fight decisions

Champion-specific setup, burst, matchup and failure-condition guidance independently written from the research catalog. These decisions are not site-tested clears; confirm the current live kit and node before relying on them. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

TACTIC 1

Place the Evolutionary Field before stacking damage

High Evolutionary's payoff relies on an Evolutionary Field. Confirm the field is positioned correctly and the opponent remains where it can interact; pushing the defender away prematurely may waste the opening.

TACTIC 2

Build Combo Detonation under the field's conditions

Observe the Combo Detonation state and whether Severe Wither, Exhaustion or Devolve is active. These effects solve different problems; don't assume that an ability keyword means every opponent power source is stopped.

TACTIC 3

Choose the detonation cashout from the current setup

Spend the relevant special or attack after the field and detonation resource overlap. If the defender is about to reach an unavoidable special, take a safe earlier punish rather than extending the field sequence.

TACTIC 4

Treat Devolve and power reduction as conditional

Debuff immunity, Purify or independent node-generated power can defeat the control route. The field's presence does not guarantee damage against a defender that cannot receive the intended effects.
