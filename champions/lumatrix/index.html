Champion-specific fight decisions

Champion-specific setup, burst, matchup and failure-condition guidance independently written from the research catalog. These decisions are not site-tested clears; confirm the current live kit and node before relying on them. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

TACTIC 1

Build Cunning through the intended special route

Lumatrix uses Cunning as a setup resource. Check which eligible special and effect adds Cunning, and avoid assuming that every attack raises the same counter; the next safe window matters more than a copied stack number.

TACTIC 2

Use heavy-charge Evade only with its condition met

The kit can use Evade during a heavy-charge interaction. Confirm the defensive state and opposing attack before charging; a conditional dodge is not permission to ignore an enemy unblockable or unavoidable special.

TACTIC 3

Attack in the Invisibility and Fury window

When Invisibility and Fury are active, use a safe attack sequence to convert developed Cunning into damage. Check that the opponent lacks anti-Miss or Vigilance before relying on Invisibility as protection.

TACTIC 4

Treat anti-Miss and anti-Evade counters separately

True Accuracy, Vigilance, Slow and ability-accuracy restrictions can disrupt different defenses. Personal Cunning does not provide immunity to passive damage, and losing Invisibility can invalidate an aggressive route.

Our attacker pick: Cunning and Invisibility are resources used to prepare a powerful SP2 Fury window. Special Damage targets the direct special strike rather than Evade or invisibility uptime. Our defender pick: Physical resistance covers the contact hits that land when the stealth plan is unavailable; block penetration is better if the attacker stays defensive throughout Cunning buildup.
