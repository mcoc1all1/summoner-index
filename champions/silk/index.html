INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-04; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Manage the wall before committing

Silk benefits from forcing space toward the boundary, but chasing a retreat can open you to a counterhit. Use measured combos to keep pressure while maintaining enough space to react to specials.

DECISION 2

Treat Slow as setup-dependent

Awakened Slow is useful against Evade and Unstoppable, but check whether the correct attack or wall condition has triggered it. Do not assume every Silk hit suppresses those defensive abilities.

DECISION 3

Know when her safety net is unavailable

Her special-attack Evade has conditions and downtime, and being critically hit can change access to it. Check the active state before attempting a risky intercept or relying on an automatic escape.
