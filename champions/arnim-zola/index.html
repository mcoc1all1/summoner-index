TACTIC 1

Establish Energize before the long special plan

Arnim Zola's offensive route depends on Energize buildup. Check the personal Energize effect and the opponent’s current bar before extending the fight; an empty opening SP2 does not equal a developed conversion.

TACTIC 2

Maintain pressure while avoiding Sleep mistakes

Sleep can change when an opponent may be safely punished, but only if its actual trigger applies. Keep the intended control and Fury resources visible and do not deliberately attack into an unsupported Sleep assumption.

TACTIC 3

Convert Energize into SP2 Disintegration

Use SP2 once the resource and safe opening align, and verify Disintegration can affect the target. If the opponent Purifies immediately or has relevant immunity, choose a simpler damage plan instead of rebuilding indefinitely.

TACTIC 4

Distinguish personal Energize from node Power Gain

Energize changes Zola's own power handling; it does not automatically stop the defender’s node-driven power or prevent an SP3. Recheck special thresholds and damage-over-time restrictions.
