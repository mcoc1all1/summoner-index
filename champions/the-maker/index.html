TACTIC 1

Prepare the Plasma Mine before committing to damage

The Maker's damage route involves a Plasma Mine. Confirm the mine is placed and currently usable, then plan the defender's movement so the next attack can benefit; a mine on the field is not proof of damage.

TACTIC 2

Use safe Heavy openings to develop the mine

Heavy attacks may support further mine setup and overcharge. Bait a heavy or special recovery before charging yours; an aggressive defender can interrupt an otherwise promising resource sequence.

TACTIC 3

Cash out with the prepared overcharged burst

When the mine, Plasma and current personal effects align, take a safe special opportunity. Verify whether the target can receive the damage type or Heal Block rather than holding meter for a payoff that cannot apply.

TACTIC 4

Account for Suppression and power reroute rules

Suppression targets certain power interactions; Power Reroute may alter the defender's timing differently. Immunity, Purify and independent node power can disrupt control while leaving the mine visible.

Attacker starting point
Critical Damage Rating

For The Maker, Critical Damage Rating can affect eligible direct critical-hit damage; separate that from Fury potency, Damage-over-Time and attacks that do not naturally crit. Keep the source-linked setup constant: Select useful Planned Obsolescence effects before the fight and place a mine with Special 1. Compare Critical Rating against the same opponent, rank and resources. Do not mistake a changed direct hit for proof of longer resource uptime or stronger debuff ticks.

Defender starting point
Armor Penetration

For The Maker on defense, Armor Penetration may improve the defender's outgoing direct hits against armored attackers; it does not reduce incoming damage, so a mitigation choice is a real alternative. Kit limitation: Mine positioning and Evade cooldown matter; being hit by an opponent’s Heavy Attack removes awakened Special Evade charges. Compare Block Proficiency using the same attack type, defensive sequence, relic state and node; no across-mode ranking or gameplay result is claimed.
