Champion-specific fight decisions

Champion-specific setup, burst, matchup and failure-condition guidance independently written from the research catalog. These decisions are not site-tested clears; confirm the current live kit and node before relying on them. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

TACTIC 1

Plan the next meter investment

Treat Force Field as a stockpile to protect: consider SP1 when you need more defensive resources and Taunt, then examine the existing Armor Up before spending any of those charges on the next special.

TACTIC 2

Don't drain power by accident

A light combo reaching its fourth hit can spend Force Field for Power Drain. That may solve a power-heavy matchup, but it competes with the stored resources needed for a bigger damage payoff.

TACTIC 3

Choose immediate or delayed SP2

A short fight may reward using SP2 with the charges already built, whereas SP3 offers a slower path to more persistent Force Field. Verify whether Armor Up is available if you intend to produce Shock.

TACTIC 4

Watch for layered counterplay

When the opponent removes Armor, resists Shock, or ignores Taunt, more than one part of Guardian's plan can fail at once. Preserve spacing and bait specials normally rather than counting on a single defensive layer.
