TACTIC 1

Select the default Tempest before the fight

Storm (Pyramid X) has Lightning and Ice Tempest interactions that answer different encounter requirements. Kabam's 2026 buff changed how the default versus alternate Tempest works, so identify the intended default first rather than copying an old always-active Glancing rule.

TACTIC 2

Use Heavy to reach the alternate Tempest

Her updated Signature Ability allows a Heavy Attack to activate the alternate Tempest, with duration influenced by the Heavy charge time. Attempt it only during a genuine recovery and verify awakening; the charge timing is not permission to take a hit.

TACTIC 3

Spend Prowess for the matching damage route

The updated SP1 Coldsnap and SP2 Shock can scale with Special Damage, while certain Lightning Tempest effects need that Tempest to be the default. Track Prowess and the active Tempest, then launch from safe recovery rather than forcing an outdated six-stack routine.

TACTIC 4

Read Skill, Endurance and Power Burn exceptions

The 2026 change added passive Parry Stun when an opponent has Endurance and new rules for Glancing Skill specials; Power Burn can now remove Tempests. Confirm those conditions and the target's Shock/Coldsnap immunity before assigning a matchup.
