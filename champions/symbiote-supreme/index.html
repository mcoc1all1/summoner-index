TACTIC 1

Track the blessing phase

Symbiote Supreme's blessing cycle changes his available utility. Identify the current phase before attacking into a defender's buffs or planning the next special; the same button press does not have equal value in every phase.

TACTIC 2

Favor buff-rich opponents with caution

His Nullify-oriented toolkit can be strong when the defender generates removable buffs. First check whether those are ordinary buffs, passive effects or node-protected states before expecting any control payoff.

TACTIC 3

Line up the special with the cycle

A well-timed special can exploit the current blessing and the opponent's buff state. Keep space to finish the animation and do not spend power purely because the preferred phase is approaching if the defender can punish immediately.

TACTIC 4

Know when the engine is missing

Buff immunity on the defender, Nullify immunity, Neutralize prevention or aggressive special pressure may reduce his utility. Switch to patient punishes when the buff-removal interaction cannot be established reliably.
