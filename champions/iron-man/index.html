INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Track Armor and Reactor before planning the burst

The recorded Iron Man kit uses Armor development and a Reactor payoff. Check the current Armor state, including losses from Armor Break, before assuming the next Special has the complete setup.

DECISION 2

Choose SP1 control or SP3 Incinerate intentionally

A Reactor-supported SP1 can provide Armor Break, while SP3 can support Incinerate pressure. Match the Special to the defender's protection; Armor Break immunity and Incinerate immunity demand different plans.

DECISION 3

Recheck older Iron Man advice after kit changes

Iron Man has changed since the game's launch period, so legacy one-line damage comparisons may not describe the current kit. Verify the installed champion description and Reactor behavior before an expensive rank-up.

Armor Penetration
