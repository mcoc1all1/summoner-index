TACTIC 1

Bank Rock Stacks before committing

Thing's Rock Stacks are part of both his resilience and his offensive timing. Watch the stack count during ordinary exchanges, then choose a safe punish rather than assuming every blocked hit will preserve the same setup.

TACTIC 2

Plan the protection window

When the opponent threatens a heavy burst, check whether the current kit's Protection condition is actually active. Do not treat a high Rock Stack count as unconditional immunity to Special Attack damage or to quest-node damage.

TACTIC 3

Spend a special with purpose

Decide whether the next special is intended to support damage or control based on the buffs and stacks visible in this fight. Bait an opponent special first if staying near maximum power would create an unsafe exchange.

TACTIC 4

Respect stack manipulation

Ability-accuracy reduction, Nullify interactions, and nodes that punish defensive buffs or prolonged fights may undermine the plan. Recheck the current stack state after each special rather than copying a fixed sequence.

Attacker starting point
Critical Damage Rating

Our attacker pick: Thing converts Rock Stacks into persistent Fury, then uses ordinary direct hits for long damage. Critical Damage Rating exploits eligible critical hits after Fury builds rather than increasing Rock Stack acquisition. Compare Critical Damage Rating against Special Damage in the same fight at the same rank, awakening, relevant buffs, and node.
