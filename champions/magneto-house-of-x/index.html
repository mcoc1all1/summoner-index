DECISION 1

Verify Armor Break in this encounter

Magneto (House of X) is catalogued with Armor Break. Confirm what action triggers it in the current champion version, then check whether the active quest node changes that interaction. Choose a repeatable safe opening based on what you see rather than assuming a copied rotation fits this encounter.

DECISION 2

Track Bleed before committing

The catalogue also records Bleed for Magneto (House of X). Watch for its actual trigger while also tracking Armor Break; do not assume the two effects activate together or survive the same countermeasure. Spend power only after confirming that the expected state is present, and reset the plan when it drops.

DECISION 3

Confirm Bleed protection

The structured records flag Bleed protection for Magneto (House of X). Check whether the incoming effect is a debuff, a passive or a node-specific interaction, and verify any activation condition before depending on that protection. One recorded protection does not establish blanket immunity to related effects.

Attacker starting point
Special Damage

Magneto (House of X) gains Prowess through combat and Heavy Attacks, then channels it into Specials, while pre-fight Guidance spends persistent charges for team utility. Special Damage is an attack trial at a fixed Prowess count; compare Critical Damage Rating for the same SP2 window and avoid treating either as extra Guidance charges or guaranteed Bleed/Degeneration ticks. Compare outgoing direct-hit Special damage with the alternative at matching Prowess stacks and Guidance state.

Alternative to test: Not reviewed

Defender starting point
Physical Resistance

Physical Resistance can mitigate relevant basic hits while this support-oriented champion is defending; it does not boost Guidance, alter persistent-charge spending, or protect from every energy interaction. Compare Energy Resistance against an attacker whose key damage is actually energy, with the same PvP node package.

Original relic attribute research · editorial trial

Comparison priority: compare · Statcast attribute to investigate: Special Damage

Prowess accumulated through Heavies and combat can enhance Magneto (House of X)'s Specials. A Special Damage Statcast is a targeted direct-hit experiment at equal Prowess count, not an increase to Guidance charges or automatic Bleed potency.

Battlecast comparison: A mutant Battlecast striker may provide an extra safe heavy or maintain Prowess, but its actual rune must be known and any gain should be separated from the pre-fight Guidance team bonus.

Test plan: Use the same quest persistent-charge count, either with Guidance enabled in both runs or disabled in both. Compare SP2 direct hit and Bleed/Degeneration results with identical Prowess and striker use.

The Guidance pre-fight is a shared team effect and should not be counted as a personal relic gain; no publisher-sanctioned Magneto pairing or owned Statcast secondary is identified. No exact Statcast statue or secondary variant has been verified here.
