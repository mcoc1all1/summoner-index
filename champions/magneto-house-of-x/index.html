TACTIC 1

Assign Guidance before the correct fight

Magneto (House of X) can spend persistent resources to provide Guidance through a pre-fight choice. Make the selection on the intended encounter and confirm the effect appears; the team should not assume it was applied just because White Magneto is present.

TACTIC 2

Understand the first Parry exception

The passive-Stun benefit for eligible Metal or Hero attackers is conditional: the first Parry interaction has a different role from subsequent Parry Stuns under the spotlight's Guidance rules. Verify eligibility and do not promise every Parry will work on a Stun-immune node.

TACTIC 3

Manage his own special timing

If using Magneto as the attacker, plan around the active magnetic effects and available special openings rather than treating support-mode Guidance as his entire offensive kit. Bait the next defender special before committing to a long setup.

TACTIC 4

Separate support from guaranteed immunity

Guidance can change how eligible allies' Stuns behave, but it does not override all node restrictions or make the team immune to contact damage. Check champion tags, persistent charges and current kit text at each quest entry.

Attacker starting point
Special Damage

Magneto (House of X) gains Prowess through combat and Heavy Attacks, then channels it into Specials, while pre-fight Guidance spends persistent charges for team utility. Special Damage is an attack trial at a fixed Prowess count; compare Critical Damage Rating for the same SP2 window and avoid treating either as extra Guidance charges or guaranteed Bleed/Degeneration ticks. Compare outgoing direct-hit Special damage with the alternative at matching Prowess stacks and Guidance state.

Alternative to test: Not reviewed

Defender starting point
Physical Resistance

Physical Resistance can mitigate relevant basic hits while this support-oriented champion is defending; it does not boost Guidance, alter persistent-charge spending, or protect from every energy interaction. Compare Energy Resistance against an attacker whose key damage is actually energy, with the same PvP node package.
