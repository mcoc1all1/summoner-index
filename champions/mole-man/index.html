TACTIC 1

Decide whether you need utility or burst

At lower Monster Mass outside Frenzy, Mole Man's True Accuracy can be the reason to select him. Adding too much Mass can change the ability package, so don't build resources automatically.

TACTIC 2

Build Mass only when ready to commit

Heavies and SP1 can help advance the Mass state. If the defender is hard to heavy-punish, it may be safer to keep utility than force the Frenzy transition.

TACTIC 3

Use Frenzy for a timed damage phase

Frenzy opens a stronger critical-hit and SP2 opportunity. Plan your special meter before it begins, then make use of the brief state rather than wasting time baiting a special.

TACTIC 4

Watch what your Mass level disables

Anti-Evade utility and Frenzy damage are not fully interchangeable. Purify, Shock and node-based effects also change the plan; verify the exact current Mass threshold before attempting a defender whose Evade is your primary problem.

7★ stat focus · eligibility verified

Attacker starting point
Critical Damage Rating

Mole Man's Frenzy can make his hits guaranteed Critical Hits, so Critical Damage Rating is a more relevant experimental direct-hit lever than merely increasing crit chance during Frenzy. Compare Special Damage over the same Monster Mass and Frenzy window; neither setting extends Frenzy or makes the associated Bleed and Shock damage scale automatically.

Alternative to test: Not reviewed

Defender starting point
Physical Resistance

Physical Resistance must be evaluated alongside Mole Man's Monster Mass protection and its expiry during Frenzy; another defensive stat does not confer permanent True Accuracy, Unstoppable or Debuff Purify. Compare Block Proficiency only for defender sequences that involve actual blocked hits and record where Frenzy suspends defensive utility.

Comparison priority: statcast · Statcast attribute to investigate: Critical Damage Rating

During Frenzy, Mole Man can guarantee critical hits, making Critical Damage Rating worth testing on eligible direct contact attacks. It does not alter Monster Mass buildup, Bleed ticks or the Frenzy cooldown.

Battlecast comparison: A skill Battlecast striker may extend the attack sequence, but the trigger and duration could change exactly when Monster Mass expires; verify the rune rather than crediting it with Frenzy access.

Test plan: Compare SP2 damage at fixed Monster Mass, identical Frenzy start/end, and no class-specific enemy protections. Match secondary attributes and relic rank, recording crit hit values, Bleed damage and full cycle time.
