Champion-specific fight decisions

Champion-specific setup, burst, matchup and failure-condition guidance independently written from the research catalog. These decisions are not site-tested clears; confirm the current live kit and node before relying on them. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

TACTIC 1

Choose which randomly generated buffs are useful

Venom the Duck develops Symbiote buffs through his Aberration cycle. Inspect whether the next quest fight needs survival, power or damage, and do not assume a particular buff will be generated on the next cycle.

TACTIC 2

Bank a good buff when the rules allow

His RNA Bank can preserve eligible Symbiote buffs across quest encounters. Confirm capacity and the current buff before using a Heavy or special to store it; preserving a useful long-path resource may beat immediate damage in this fight.

TACTIC 3

Plan specials around the bank and opponent

A developed offensive bank can make later specials more attractive, but Bleed-resistant and buff-denial defenders change the equation. Recheck active stored buffs and the target's immunity rather than relying on a copied quest-opening combo.

TACTIC 4

Separate fan proposals from a released rework

Community discussions have suggested new RNA Bank limits and different abilities. They are not proof of a shipped kit update; the 2026 Venom the Duck chase event confirms availability, not that those proposed mechanics became real.

Attacker starting point
Critical Damage Rating

Our attacker pick: Banked Aberration buffs can include Fury and Precision, and when that setup is available Critical Damage Rating rewards direct crits across quest fights. It does not make the buff bank more predictable. Compare Critical Damage Rating against Special Damage in the same fight at the same rank, awakening, relevant buffs, and node.

Physical Resistance

Our defender pick: Randomized buffs can produce sustain but not dependable immunity. Physical resistance limits the incoming physical hits during unfavorable buff rolls. Compare Physical Resistance against Block Penetration versus the same attacker and mode before investing.
