Limits & caveats

EDITORIAL FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Editorial decision notes derived from identified game mechanics and attributed source research; these are not in-game performance tests, guarantees, or permissions to reuse third-party text. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Pick Sting chemistry from the defender

Choose the Sting type at the pre-fight screen based on the defender's damage-effect protection rather than defaulting to one option. Check the chosen setting's defensive benefit separately, instead of claiming every incoming effect is covered.

DECISION 2

Bait a Special before the Sting expires

Apply Stings through a manageable combo and SP1, then create a safe opportunity for the defender to launch a Special while those effects are still active. Stings can expire during a long bait; monitor the timer rather than promising a delayed burst.

DECISION 3

Make Heavy to SP2 a spacing decision

A Heavy charge can develop Torment for the SP2 payoff, but only try the linked attack when the opponent is recovering or otherwise vulnerable. Losing the fight to squeeze in one extra resource cycle is not a useful rotation.
