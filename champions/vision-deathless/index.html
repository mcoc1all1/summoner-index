TACTIC 1

Burn power across the required thresholds

Deathless Vision's offense can earn Phase charges by dropping the opponent below power thresholds. Check the actual enemy meter and Power Burn result; a special at zero power will not necessarily grant useful phase progress.

TACTIC 2

Develop the Phase and Fury state carefully

Track available Phasing and Fury after eligible burns. Phasing is conditional protection, so do not dash into an active defender solely on an assumed permanent Miss window.

TACTIC 3

Convert the prepared state through the next special

When Phase charges and Fury can contribute, use a safe punish. Heal Block can be useful against a healing target, but confirm it applies before expecting the full special damage and control plan.

TACTIC 4

Separate Deathless Vision from other Vision variants

Original Vision and Age of Ultron have different power and signature loops. Power Burn immunity, independent node power and anti-Miss effects can undermine separate parts of the Deathless route.
