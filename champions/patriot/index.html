TACTIC 1

Build Resolve before the Reversal window

Patriot relies on Resolve to prepare a strong attack sequence. Track the actual resource and do not assume a single Heavy automatically gives the maximum payoff in every fight.

TACTIC 2

Charge a Heavy only at a genuine safe opening

His Reversal interaction involves Heavy charging. Bait a defender special or heavy first, then verify the Reversal state rather than taking a dangerous hit while holding a Heavy input.

TACTIC 3

Follow the Reversal quickly with SP2

A developed Reversal can create a short SP2 opportunity. Confirm the effect and relevant Rupture or Vigilance condition, then launch while the defender is punishable instead of delaying to rebuild resources.

TACTIC 4

Watch anti-Heavy and Rupture restrictions

Rupture immunity, ability-accuracy reduction and node punishment of Heavy attacks can break the route. Resolve does not grant universal unblockable or passive-damage protection.
