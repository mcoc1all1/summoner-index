TACTIC 1

Find critical openings for Armor Break

Immortal Iron Fist can use critical hits to weaken enemy Armor. Verify the Armor Break actually lands and that the opponent's armor is relevant before building an entire strategy around it.

TACTIC 2

Separate Heavy utility from basic damage

An eligible Heavy can remove selected offensive buffs under the current kit. Create a safe Heavy punish and check whether the target has an ordinary removable buff; do not charge a Heavy solely because the opponent has a passive effect.

TACTIC 3

Spend the special when the target is weakened

If Armor Break or Precision is active, use a safe special to exploit the window. A defender immune to Armor Break may be a poor choice for the longer version of this plan.

TACTIC 4

Do not confuse both Iron Fist variants

Immortal Iron Fist and ordinary Iron Fist have different rarity and signature context. Nullify needs an eligible Buff and does not imply universal power control; verify the current variant and node.
