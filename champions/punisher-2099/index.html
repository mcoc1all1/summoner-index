DECISION 1

Verify Armor Break in this encounter

Punisher 2099 is catalogued with Armor Break. Confirm what action triggers it in the current champion version, then check whether the active quest node changes that interaction. Choose a repeatable safe opening based on what you see rather than assuming a copied rotation fits this encounter.

DECISION 2

Track Disintegration before committing

The catalogue also records Disintegration for Punisher 2099. Watch for its actual trigger while also tracking Armor Break; do not assume the two effects activate together or survive the same countermeasure. Spend power only after confirming that the expected state is present, and reset the plan when it drops.

DECISION 3

Check the defender's countermeasures

Before using Punisher 2099, look for opponent immunities, resistances and node effects that interrupt Armor Break. If the planned trigger is missing, adapt the fight instead of assuming a favorable matchup from a source list. This profile does not present a documented current-client win.
