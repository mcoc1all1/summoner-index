TACTIC 1

Treat the Nano-Tech battery as a clock

Punisher 2099's Battery recharges until Overdrive, where it becomes a limited combat window. Watch its meter independently of your own Special Attack bars; a premature Overdrive can expire before the desired damage and control opportunity.

TACTIC 2

Use the Heavy to control entry timing

Under appropriate kit conditions, landing a Heavy can consume Battery and provide a Stun. Use this only during a genuine punish; holding Overdrive back for an SP2 setup is not worth getting intercepted by a Stun-immune defender.

TACTIC 3

Convert SP2 into controlled pressure

An SP2 Armor Break and the Overdrive damage state can reinforce one another. Plan the first SP2 around current Battery and defender Armor immunity, then decide whether SP1's battery support is more useful than saving for another expensive special.

TACTIC 4

Do not call Overdrive a total power lock

Overdrive heavily reduces the opponent's combat power rate but may not suppress other power sources or all rank/node modifiers. Monitor the actual power bar every combo and keep a safe bait window; Battery-based healing and Heal Block also require their own triggers.
