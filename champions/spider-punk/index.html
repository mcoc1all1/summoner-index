TACTIC 1

Build Crowd and Ruptures without feeding unsafe power

Spider-Punk uses Crowd buildup alongside Rupture. Check the active Crowd state and target's susceptibility before extending a combo; a long offensive loop can become unsafe if the defender reaches SP3.

TACTIC 2

Use Taunt for a controlled bait when possible

A Taunt may encourage a special to create another opening, but is not reliable against every node or AI behavior. Track Physical Vulnerability and defensive Evade separately as you maintain the setup.

TACTIC 3

Cash out through SP1 when Crowd pressure is ready

The intended SP1 payoff depends on the developed Crowd and Rupture resource. Confirm the active stack, target immunity and opponent recovery rather than throwing SP1 simply because one bar became available.

TACTIC 4

Check Rupture immunity and anti-Evade tools

Purify, Rupture immunity and anti-Evade effects can remove separate strengths. Crowd is not automatic Unblockable safety, and Taunt cannot force a particular enemy special against every defender.
