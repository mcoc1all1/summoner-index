TACTIC 1

Use defensive blocking as a tool, not a rotation

Classic Captain America can provide Perfect Block utility. Verify the shield interaction under the current node and use short basic attacks to build special meter; do not block an unsafe Unblockable simply for a theoretical trigger.

TACTIC 2

Find a genuine Stun or heavy punish window

When Stun applies, use it to gain controlled momentum; if Stun is prevented, bait the defender's heavy or special and punish its recovery. A successful block is not permission to throw a heavy immediately.

TACTIC 3

Spend specials rather than endlessly turtling

Choose the special that fits enemy power and defensive immunity in this fight. If the kit lists a damaging debuff, verify that it appears before making the entire plan depend on it.

TACTIC 4

Avoid importing variants' resource engines

Classic Captain America is not Captain America (Infinity War), WWII or Sam Wilson. Do not assume kinetic charges, high-frequency Bleed or Tech Lock-On, and check passive-damage nodes before relying on Perfect Block.
