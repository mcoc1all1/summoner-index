EDITORIAL FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Editorial decision notes derived from identified game mechanics and attributed source research; these are not in-game performance tests, guarantees, or permissions to reuse third-party text. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Set up Falter before asking the opponent for a Special

Prowler can build Falter through eligible second-Medium finishers and use it to influence Special baiting. Check whether the opponent can Miss; True Strike or anti-Miss nodes can make this a poor control option.

DECISION 2

Punish emptied power with a Heavy for Incinerate

A drained defender after a Special can provide a safer Heavy opening that applies Incinerate. Watch the actual power bar and recovery animation rather than assuming every enemy Special leaves the same window.

DECISION 3

Use SP2 when Power Creep and damaging effects are established

SP2 can build and detonate Ruptures with the right Power Creep and Incinerate state. Against Incinerate or Rupture protection, reevaluate the cash-out rather than saving two bars for a blocked effect.

Reversed Controls
Scope not reviewed

With Spider-Punk's Splitting Heads synergy present, Prowler can choose a pre-fight option that makes him immune to subsequent reversed-controls effects. The trade-off is that his own controls stay inverted for the entire fight. Do not classify him as naturally reverse-controls immune without that synergy and selected pre-fight.

Critical Damage Rating

For Prowler, Critical Damage Rating can affect eligible direct critical-hit damage; separate that from Fury potency, Damage-over-Time and attacks that do not naturally crit. Keep the source-linked setup constant: Use second-medium finishers to establish Falter and bait enemy specials. Compare Critical Rating against the same opponent, rank and resources. Armor Penetration

For Prowler on defense, Armor Penetration may improve the defender's outgoing direct hits against armored attackers; it does not reduce incoming damage, so a mitigation choice is a real alternative.
