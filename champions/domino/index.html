INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Check which Lucky or Unlucky state actually appears

Domino's kit has random components, so repeated attempts do not promise the same Bleed or Evade. Base the next special on the visible state rather than assuming the most favorable probability has occurred.

DECISION 2

Avoid a fixed Heavy script against Stun immunity

A Heavy during an opponent's safe Stun recovery can increase offensive value, but forcing that window against Stun-immune or quick-recovery defenders invites damage. Use another opening if the condition is unavailable.

DECISION 3

Recognize that Tech changes Unlucky interactions

The documented kit excludes Tech opponents from becoming Unlucky. Do not attribute a failed anti-ability plan to bad luck when the matchup fundamentally lacks that state; choose an ability-control specialist instead.
