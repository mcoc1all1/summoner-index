TACTIC 1

Treat Lucky as a window

Lucky and Unlucky are chance-dependent states, not a deterministic rotation. Build a clean combo and inspect which state is active before expecting enhanced critical effects or an opposing ability failure.

TACTIC 2

Heavy only on a safe stun

A heavy against a stunned defender can reward the opening. If the opponent is Stun immune or can Purify, don't turn a probabilistic advantage into an unsafe heavy punish.

TACTIC 3

Decide how to spend power

SP1 while Lucky can support an existing Bleed/Stun setup; SP2 is an alternative for larger Bleed pressure. Choose from the debuffs already applied rather than assuming either special is always optimal.

TACTIC 4

Build a plan for bad RNG

Domino can be strong without forcing every proc, but nodes that punish a failed ability, Bleed immunity and unstable defensive recovery make outcomes uneven. Leave room for ordinary damage when Lucky doesn't arrive.
