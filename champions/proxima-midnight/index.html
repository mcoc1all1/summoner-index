INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Identify the current mission before attacking

Proxima Midnight's missions can require well-timed blocks, intercepts or knockdowns. Read the active objective before choosing a basic attack sequence, because completing the wrong action does not progress the intended mission.

DECISION 2

Spend mission Prowess on a Special with a real opening

Completed missions can provide Prowess and improve Special payoff. Check the visible buff and opponent's power first; a high-damage sequence is not useful if the defender can interrupt before the Special lands.

DECISION 3

Choose SP1 control or SP2 Stun by actual defender needs

SP1 can support healing control and SP2 provides another possible Stun opening. Heal Block and Stun immunity are different matchup constraints, so use the effect that addresses the fight rather than selecting by damage alone.
