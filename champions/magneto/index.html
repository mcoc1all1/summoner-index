BEST USE

Where the kit helps

Metal opponents receive Magnetized, reducing ability accuracy and regeneration rate. This matchup-specific utility is the reason to check tags before selecting him.

WATCH OUT

Limits & caveats

Do not extend Metal-specific benefits to every opponent. Ability-accuracy protections and fight nodes can change which effects are suppressed.

INVESTMENT

Before ranking up

Awakening
Awakening adds a per-source damage cap excluding Special 3, critical resistance, block penetration and heavy-attack energy burst. It does not unlock base Magnetism.

EDITORIAL FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Editorial decision notes derived from identified game mechanics and attributed source research; these are not in-game performance tests, guarantees, or permissions to reuse third-party text. Checked 2026-11-04; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Read the Metal tag before selecting him

Magneto's Magnetized suppression and signature damage advantages are strongest in Metal matchups. Verify the opposing champion's current classification instead of relying on costume, appearance or Tech class alone.

DECISION 2

Set up Prowess without sacrificing safety

Knockdowns and charged heavies can contribute different Prowess stacks. Build only as many as you can safely protect, and avoid charging a Heavy while an opponent has a live punish window.

DECISION 3

Choose the SP3 payoff for the matchup

SP3 can convert the developed Prowess state into significant damage against Metal enemies. Check whether Bleed sticks; an immune Metal defender changes the finishing effect and the value of a Bleed-oriented plan.
