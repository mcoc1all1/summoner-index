Champion-specific fight decisions

Champion-specific setup, burst, matchup and failure-condition guidance independently written from the research catalog. These decisions are not site-tested clears; confirm the current live kit and node before relying on them. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

TACTIC 1

Develop Fury and Cruelty while spacing safely

Gamora gains damage from Fury and Cruelty buffs. Build the current buff state through controlled attacks, then check their remaining durations before committing to a special rather than chasing another heavy into an active defender.

TACTIC 2

Spend the special inside the buff window

A developed buff stack only matters if the next special can land while the buffs remain active. Bait a dangerous opponent special first if necessary and avoid extending the buildup just to match a theoretical maximum stack count.

TACTIC 3

Adapt to short and long encounters

A short duel can reward earlier damage while a high-health quest opponent may justify more buff development. Use current enemy health, active buffs and the next safe opening to choose the route instead of prescribing one fixed rotation.

TACTIC 4

Avoid Nullify and Neutralize traps

Nullify, Stagger and Neutralize can strip or prevent Gamora's damage engine. Do not equate her Cosmic class with immunity to those counters, and verify changed buff timers before copying older damage demonstrations.
