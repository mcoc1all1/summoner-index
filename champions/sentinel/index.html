Champion-specific fight decisions

Champion-specific setup, burst, matchup and failure-condition guidance independently written from the research catalog. These decisions are not site-tested clears; confirm the current live kit and node before relying on them. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

TACTIC 1

Build Analysis with safe contact

Sentinel's Analysis charges reward repeatable interactions, but building them without managing the opponent's power can be costly. Use predictable punishes and inspect the Analysis count rather than blindly extending a combo.

TACTIC 2

Use a special for the available matchup

His Special Attacks offer different debuff and damage opportunities. Check the defender's immunity before choosing a familiar Incinerate or Shock plan, and make sure the chosen special is safe to finish.

TACTIC 3

Exploit the completed Analysis state

The value of a developed Analysis stack can change offensive and defensive decisions. Verify the visible state and relevant ability text before attempting a heavy or special payoff that assumes the stack is complete.

TACTIC 4

Don't rely on Robot tags alone

Sentinel's Robot identity does not guarantee immunity to every node-generated effect. Read whether the encounter uses passive damage, armor removal, heal prevention or a debuff before depending on a generic technology matchup.

7★ stat focus · eligibility verified

Attacker starting point
Block Proficiency

Sentinel gains Analysis from opponent actions and becomes stronger as Analysis develops; a Block Proficiency trial addresses the cost of deliberately holding block during that ramp. Compare Special Damage after matching Analysis, Armor state and chosen special; this focus cannot directly strengthen Shock or Incinerate ticks or speed Analysis gains.

Alternative to test: Not reviewed

Defender starting point
Armor Rating

Armor Rating tests added survival on top of Sentinel's Analysis-linked Armor in fights where the attacker can land physical hits. It does not grant Analysis or increase the chance to apply Heal Block. Compare Block Proficiency under a matched attacking sequence that actually includes blocked hits, including the opponent's armor-penetration tools.

Original relic attribute research · editorial trial

Comparison priority: compare · Statcast attribute to investigate: Block Proficiency

A Block Proficiency Statcast can test whether the health cost of maintaining Analysis while guarding repeated opponent actions is reduced; it does not itself create Analysis or boost Heal Block and damaging debuffs.

Battlecast comparison: An equipped tech Battlecast may improve control through a striker or verified rune, but the striker must not be credited with Analysis it did not actually generate. Confirm the rune trigger and whether the opponent behaves similarly.

Test plan: Compare equal Analysis charge checkpoints, opponent special pattern, chip damage taken and SP1/SP2 rotation timing in the same fight. Match relic rank and rune activation and note any block damage avoided.
