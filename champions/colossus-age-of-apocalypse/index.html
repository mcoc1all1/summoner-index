INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Build Osmium Prowess before spending a Steel Burst

Colossus (Age of Apocalypse) builds Osmium Prowess through repeated eligible hits. Confirm the count before using the double-tap Block Steel Burst input; an early or missed activation may disrupt the intended combo extension.

DECISION 2

Practice the double-tap input outside a dangerous fight

A community report describes the Steel Burst block input as difficult to execute consistently. This is a player-experience report, not proof the kit always fails; learn the timing on a manageable opponent before using it in a critical quest.

DECISION 3

Use Berserk SP2 only when the remaining window is playable

SP2 can enter Berserk and expand the Prowess setup, while the available Specials change during the state. Check the opponent's power and mobility first, since an overly passive defender can waste the limited Berserk window.
