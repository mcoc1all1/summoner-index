TACTIC 1

Enter Gamma Rage on purpose

Build Fury through clean basic attacks, then use a safe heavy to reach Gamma Rage when the fight allows it. Do not trigger the state merely because the button is available if the next special will be awkward to use.

TACTIC 2

Line up Trauma and the burst

SP2 can provide a Trauma window whose duration interacts with Gamma Rage. Aim to have enough space and power to capitalize immediately rather than spending most of the effect chasing a retreating defender.

TACTIC 3

Use SP1 for a controlled opening

During Rage, SP1's Stun can create room for follow-up damage, but repeated Stuns shorten. Track the actual Stun duration instead of assuming every loop will last as long as the first.

TACTIC 4

Watch stun immunity and spacing

Stun-immune opponents or nodes that punish Heavy charging change both the entry and payoff. A strong Fury count does not justify a risky heavy when the defender is free to intercept.

Scope not reviewed

Hulk is immune to gaining Buffs only with the Silver Surfer synergy on the team. This is self-Buff immunity, not immunity to an opponent's Buff Immunity Debuff. Without the synergy, his base kit does not provide this protection.

Nullify
Scope not reviewed

Requires the Hulkbuster Don't Mention Puny Banner! synergy; Hulk's base kit alone is not Nullify-immune. This effect-level protection does not establish coverage of every quest-node interaction.

Fate Seal
Scope not reviewed

Requires the Hulkbuster Don't Mention Puny Banner! synergy to protect Hulk from Fate Seal. The synergy must be active in the team; base Hulk is not Fate Seal-immune.

Stagger
Scope not reviewed

Requires the Hulkbuster Don't Mention Puny Banner! synergy to protect Hulk from Stagger. Do not assume this ability without that teammate.

Reversed Controls
Scope not reviewed
