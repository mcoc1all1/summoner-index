TACTIC 1

Use the current reworked kit, not 2018 blessings

Doctor Strange received a major rework discussed by players in January 2026. Check the modern Fractured Power and spell indicators before planning the encounter; old Hoggoth/Agamotto rotation tutorials may describe a superseded ability set.

TACTIC 2

Set up the power interaction deliberately

Modern Strange can use Wither-related utility against eligible extra Power Gain. Verify how Fractured Power or the current spell resource is activated rather than assuming it occurs automatically at the start of every fight.

TACTIC 3

Spend specials while the setup is active

The revised rotation involves explicit spell activation and timing. Bait an opponent special, then launch from a safe recovery with the active personal resource; do not assume every double-tap Block interaction can be executed safely under pressure.

TACTIC 4

Do not inherit fan-made rework abilities

Older forum posts propose entirely different blessing cycles and special effects. They are fan ideas, not proof of the January 2026 live kit; verify the current damage, healing and anti-buff conditions before assigning Strange a guaranteed boss counter.
