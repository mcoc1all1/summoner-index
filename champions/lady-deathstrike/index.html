TACTIC 1

Develop the armor-linked attack state

Lady Deathstrike's damage scales through personal armor-related mechanics and not just a generic base-Attack ramp. Check the current armor state before a special and avoid trades against opponents that remove the resources needed for the payoff.

TACTIC 2

Use her targeted Mutant utility when eligible

Her Mutant-oriented kit can interfere with healing and specific Unstoppable or Unblockable behavior. Confirm the defender's class and exact active node instead of assuming the same protection always applies against other classes.

TACTIC 3

Time Rupture pressure to the safe opening

Against an opponent that permits Rupture, plan the special around current resources and remaining debuff duration. If the defender is about to reach three bars of power, take the safe window instead of stretching a damage setup.

TACTIC 4

Remember the Sentinel synergy change

Kabam announced an adjustment to an unusually strong Lady Deathstrike–Sentinel synergy, not a universal nerf to her whole kit. Recheck team composition and whether armor or rupture effects are restricted by the current encounter.

Always active

Heal Block
Scope not reviewed

Lady Deathstrike immediately removes incoming Heal Block effects, but loses access to Willpower healing for the original duration. This prevents the named Heal Block from persisting, not every form of healing suppression.

Conditional

3 effects
Ability Accuracy Reduction
Scope not reviewed

Lady Deathstrike's Ability Accuracy cannot be modified while fighting Mutant opponents. This is a matchup-restricted protection and does not provide an all-class shield from ability-accuracy effects.

Armor Break
Debuffs

Armor Break immunity requires Nanobots Regenerator to be active after being struck by an opponent's SP1 or SP2, and her personal Armor Up must still be present. Armor Break is not always blocked.

Rupture
Debuffs

Rupture Debuff immunity is present during Nanobots Regenerator while her Armor Up is active, generally when struck by the opponent's SP1 or SP2. Without that protection window she is not broadly Rupture-immune.

Resistance / scope note: Bleed/Poison potency reduction is resistance. Armor Break and Rupture-debuff immunity requires active Nanobots Regenerator.
