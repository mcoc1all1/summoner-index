TACTIC 1

Use the 2026 modern Magik kit, not only old Limbo advice

Magik received substantial updates announced in February 2026. Read the current Limbo Buff, Neutralize and Degeneration interactions before planning a fight; an old manual based only on SP2 Power Lock may describe a different kit.

TACTIC 2

Control the timing of the Limbo window

The updated kit can regenerate some damage when its Limbo state expires and influence effect timing. Check the active buff timer and current health before risking contact; the recovery is not a guarantee against an instant knockout or node damage.

TACTIC 3

Convert through a safe spell and special sequence

Her Heavy and dash-related Falter interactions can help create a route to a special, but they need the actual state to trigger. Verify the personal resources and enemy power before launching rather than guessing that every dash prevents a counterattack.

TACTIC 4

Distinguish base Magik from Stellar-Forged Magik

The November 2026 Stellar-Forged variant uses a different Class Shift and fractured power bar, with additional acquisition milestones still scheduled. Do not import those future-version rules into this base Magik profile without confirming the exact roster entry.

Attacker starting point
Block Proficiency

Our attacker pick: Magik's Limbo Degeneration and power-control timing drive long-fight value, rather than a stat that clearly boosts Degeneration ticks. Block Proficiency protects the setup while specials are baited. Compare Block Proficiency against Special Damage in the same fight at the same rank, awakening, relevant buffs, and node.

Energy Resistance

Our defender pick: Energy resistance is a survival test against energy-special attackers; her Limbo does not mean incoming energy damage is universally irrelevant.
