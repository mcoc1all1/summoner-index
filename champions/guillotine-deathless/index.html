DECISION 1

Check Degeneration before the opener

Before bringing Guillotine (Deathless) into this fight, inspect Degeneration and Spectre in the live kit and read the quest node. Start with one safe basic-attack punish and observe which listed mechanic actually activates. Treat this as a research-derived starting checklist, not a verified opening rotation.

DECISION 2

Develop Degeneration and monitor Spectre

For Guillotine (Deathless), Degeneration is a recorded damage interaction, but its damage form and stacking rules are not independently validated here. Check the defender for relevant immunity, Purify or resistance before trying to maintain uptime. Track Spectre separately: the appearance of one ability does not prove that the second triggers on the same attack or persists for the same duration. Reset spacing before committing to another sequence.

DECISION 3

Choose a safe special payoff

Choose an SP1, SP2 or SP3 only after checking the current meter, actual active effects and whether the defender has a safe punish window. For Guillotine (Deathless), the index highlights Degeneration alongside Spectre, but it does not demonstrate a validated special order or measured damage outcome. If the opponent is close to a dangerous special, bait that attack before attempting a longer payoff.

DECISION 4

Handle Degeneration failure conditions

When the target blocks Degeneration through immunity, resistance or purification, prefer a shorter direct-hit route instead of holding power for an effect that never applies. No independently confirmed protection is established by this checklist; check incoming damage types before deliberately trading hits. Recheck the defender after a special, cleanse or node transition, and avoid treating this research draft as a guaranteed counter to a named champion.
