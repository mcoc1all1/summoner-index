TACTIC 1

Keep Taunt active if the target cooperates

Goldpool can refresh Taunt to encourage an opponent Special Attack. Track the actual debuff icon and defender's special behavior; a Taunt does not guarantee that every aggressive or node-controlled opponent throws the expected move.

TACTIC 2

Plan the safe Special 2 dance

His SP2 can reward a correctly performed sequence with Prowess. Confirm the game-specific interaction and keep space for the opponent's response rather than triggering an unverified animation or button-timing trick.

TACTIC 3

Spend the follow-up special with Prowess available

If the safe SP2 sequence creates Prowess, use it while the resource is active and check whether Bleed can apply. A Bleed-immune defender may still allow some direct damage but not the showcased damage-over-time result.

TACTIC 4

Separate healing and Taunt limitations

Regeneration, Stun and Taunt require their own current triggers. Heal Block, Purify or Stun immunity can disrupt different parts of the plan; Goldpool is not a copy of Deadpool or Platinum Pool.
