TACTIC 1

Use Root as a positioning tool, not a shield

Kraven's trapping and Root-oriented kit can pin the opponent into an offensive window. Confirm the Root actually landed and whether the defender can move or launch a special; standing close to a rooted defender is not guaranteed safety.

TACTIC 2

Build the trap state with controlled movement

Plan the next attack around where the opponent will be when the trap takes effect. An aggressive dash can put Kraven into an unblockable special even if his setup looks ready, so keep enough space for a normal bait-and-punish.

TACTIC 3

Convert through a special while the opponent is constrained

Choose an attack that matches the available trap and Bleed state, if the defender can receive those effects. If the target is immune or purifies rapidly, a shorter direct-hit route is safer than holding out for a full damage-over-time combo.

TACTIC 4

Check Root, Bleed and ability-accuracy counters

Root immunity, defensive Purify and nodes that punish repeated intercepts can weaken Kraven's main plan. Verify the live kit's trap duration and signature requirements rather than treating a forum damage showcase as a universal matchup result.

Attacker starting point
Block Proficiency

Our attacker pick: Kraven's major benefit comes from a correctly armed trap, Root and refreshed Bleeds rather than extra ordinary critical hits. Block Proficiency helps preserve health while placing and exploiting the trap. Compare Block Proficiency against Armor Penetration in the same fight at the same rank, awakening, relevant buffs, and node.

Alternative to test: Not reviewed

Defender starting point
Block Penetration

Our defender pick: Root forces an uncomfortable decision to block or take hits; block penetration tests pressure during that state, while physical resistance mitigates punishment when the trap does not activate. Compare Block Penetration against Physical Resistance versus the same attacker and mode before investing.
