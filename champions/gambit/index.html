Champion-specific fight decisions

Champion-specific setup, burst, matchup and failure-condition guidance independently written from the research catalog. These decisions are not site-tested clears; confirm the current live kit and node before relying on them. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

TACTIC 1

Accumulate Prowess through eligible blocks

Gambit's Prowess and block-related mechanics help build a later damage window. Watch the actual passive count and the defender's power bar; repeatedly holding Block without a safe plan can give up too much health.

TACTIC 2

Use a Heavy or special only when the setup is ready

His offensive payoff benefits from developed Prowess. If the opponent is nearing an unavoidable special, take a safer launch rather than waiting for maximum stacks that are no longer achievable in this encounter.

TACTIC 3

Spend SP2 for a real damage opportunity

SP2 can capitalize on current Prowess and related damage effects, but a special into an opponent's active block or immunity may underperform. Bait the defender special, then use a clean recovery window.

TACTIC 4

Check perfect-block assumptions

Gambit's defensive value changes with Prowess and current kit rules, but it is not universal immunity to passive damage. Ability accuracy reduction, unblockable nodes and power-drain interactions can change the safest playstyle.
