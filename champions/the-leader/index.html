TACTIC 1

Create severe debuffs before opening the Green Door

The Leader's plan uses severe debuffs and a Green Door area. Confirm the intended Spectre, Wither or Power Dissolve is on the opponent and position them where the area effect can actually interact.

TACTIC 2

Keep the defender over the active Door safely

The Door's location matters. Maintain controlled spacing and bait enemy specials rather than recklessly forcing them back into the zone; a defender who escapes changes the timing of the damage route.

TACTIC 3

Convert the debuff mix into Degeneration

When Green Door and the relevant debuffs overlap, choose a safe special or punish that can benefit from Degeneration. Verify the target permits it, as Purify or immunity may remove the expected delayed damage.

TACTIC 4

Do not call Wither universal power denial

Wither and Power Dissolve can affect particular power mechanics; independent node power may continue. Spectre and Degeneration have separate target resistances, and positioning cannot prevent every unblockable special.

Attacker starting point
Block Proficiency

Our attacker pick: Spectre and Wither stack over time and Green Door positioning is central to the damage plan. Block Proficiency supports a lengthy positional setup rather than assigning special-hit scaling to the debuffs. Compare Block Proficiency against Special Damage in the same fight at the same rank, awakening, relevant buffs, and node.

Our defender pick: Door positioning and suppression complicate offense but not every direct strike. Physical resistance targets contact hits that land while the attacker ignores the Door. Compare Physical Resistance against Block Penetration versus the same attacker and mode before investing.
