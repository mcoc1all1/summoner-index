TACTIC 1

Check the active pre-fight team role

Heimdall's quest utility includes team support and a survival interaction that can matter more than his own damage. Confirm the relevant ally and synergy or pre-fight conditions instead of assuming every roster automatically gains the same protection.

TACTIC 2

Track the defensive trigger in the fight

His survival effects may prevent one lethal event under specific circumstances, but they do not make the next special safe to block. Verify whether the effect has already triggered and what the current fight's node counts as damage.

TACTIC 3

Choose a special from his current personal state

When using Heimdall directly, plan the special around available personal buffs or state, not just his support role. Bait the defender's next attack before launching, especially where an aggressive node punishes holding power.

TACTIC 4

Recheck his recent kit changes

Heimdall was among champions updated in the 2025–2026 buff cycle. Do not rely on an old synergy-only or special-damage guide without confirming the current live mechanics, any persistent charges and the exact cheat-death conditions.
