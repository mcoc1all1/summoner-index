INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Keep the Gem active by avoiding unnecessary blocks

Juggernaut's Unstoppable and Momentum progression changes when he blocks or takes hits. Prefer safe movement and open attacks to maintain the window, and check whether the defender has Slow or another counter to Unstoppable.

DECISION 2

Launch SP2 at the actual maximum Momentum state

A developed Momentum bank makes SP2 attractive, but a blocked hit just before the Special can spend resources and weaken the payoff. Check stacks and enemy power before launching the prepared burst.

DECISION 3

Reactivate a disabled Gem instead of expecting automatic protection

If the Gem is disabled, a safe Special opening may be needed before the normal offensive plan resumes. Do not assume the champion stays Unstoppable while control effects or node restrictions prevent his primary ability.
