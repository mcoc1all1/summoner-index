TACTIC 1

Protect the Crimson Gem

Juggernaut's offensive rhythm relies on the Crimson Gem and Unstoppable stacks. Verify its current availability; a lost Gem means the usual momentum plan needs restoration.

TACTIC 2

Build Momentum through contact

Advance and land safe hits to build Unstoppable pressure. Blocking or taking damage can spend stacks, so a defensive-looking exchange immediately before SP2 may weaken the burst.

TACTIC 3

Use SP2 while the state is full

A developed Momentum state supports the SP2 payoff. Aim to enter the special without wasting the available stacks on a blocked basic attack or unsafe heavy attempt.

TACTIC 4

Recovery is matchup-dependent

Some control and node effects can disable the Gem or interrupt Unstoppable. Use a safe special opening to restore the kit when permitted; don't treat every opposing Stagger or Neutralize interaction as harmless.
