EDITORIAL FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Editorial decision notes derived from identified game mechanics and attributed source research; these are not in-game performance tests, guarantees, or permissions to reuse third-party text. Checked 2026-11-04; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Aura is not Power Lock

Doom's Aura can stop his own Light, SP1 and SP2 hits from granting the opponent combat power. Kabam clarified that this is not a Power Lock effect; do not assume it triggers nodes or immunity checks that explicitly react to Power Lock.

DECISION 2

Build a controllable opening

If a medium Shock lands, a heavy can turn that short opportunity into Stun and Stagger. Use a safe recovery window rather than charging a heavy into an active opponent; defenders that shrug off Shock can deny that setup.

DECISION 3

Know what breaks the loop

Slow or Petrify can stop Aura from activating, while Shock resistance or immunity interferes with the medium-to-heavy sequence. Check those interactions before planning an SP3 Aura into repeated SP1 power-steal cycle.
