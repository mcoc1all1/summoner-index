TACTIC 1

Watch the Prowess buildup before committing

Sauron's Prowess and mutant resource interactions influence when his offensive state becomes valuable. Build through controlled attacks and track the actual status icons rather than assuming a fixed number of blocked hits always triggers the same payoff.

TACTIC 2

Use Unblockable pressure only when indicated

Certain Sauron states can create Unblockable pressure, but the correct attack and timing must be confirmed. Do not dash at an active defender merely because a Prowess buff is visible; read the current special and buff conditions first.

TACTIC 3

Preserve the advantage through a safe special

When a useful offensive state is active, take the next genuine special recovery window. If the defender is close to maximum power, bait their special rather than extending Sauron's buildup until it becomes unsafe.

TACTIC 4

Check Purify, power control and node effects

Mutant tags and Prowess do not confer blanket immunity to passive damage, Heal Block or power manipulation. Sauron's regeneration and debuff interactions depend on current kit conditions, so verify what the actual opponent can remove or prevent.
