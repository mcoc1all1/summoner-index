INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Follow the visible Clue before accumulating Cruelty

Spider-Man Noir develops Dormant Cruelty from completing specified Clue actions. Check the current on-screen task, since using a generic combo may not earn the resource needed for the intended SP2.

DECISION 2

Use second Lights to establish Disorient safely

Repeated eligible Light attacks can develop Disorient, but the defender may resist ability-accuracy reduction. Choose safe openings and verify the effect before relying on it to disable a dangerous defensive trigger.

DECISION 3

Cash Crumble into a deliberate block-pressure window

SP2 can convert Cruelty and introduce Crumble, after which hitting block can create a Rooted follow-up. This requires a live Crumble state and the correct attack rhythm; the October patch also addressed an Evade-prevention issue, so avoid old bug claims.
