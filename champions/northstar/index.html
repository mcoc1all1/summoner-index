EDITORIAL FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

Champion-specific fight decisions

Champion-specific setup, burst, matchup and failure-condition guidance independently written from the research catalog. These decisions are not site-tested clears; confirm the current live kit and node before relying on them. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

TACTIC 1

Build Momentum using real movement windows

Northstar's Speed Burst and Momentum reward controlled dash-based pressure. Confirm the current Momentum count before chasing another attack, and do not push an opponent to SP3 simply to reach an older maximum stack number.

TACTIC 2

Apply Slow when the target permits it

SP1 Slow helps against selected Evade and Unstoppable defenders, but its duration scales with the current Momentum rules. Kabam's January 2025 rebalance reduced maximum Momentum to six, making pre-rebalance timing guides unreliable.

TACTIC 3

Develop Prowess before the chosen special

His special damage can scale through Prowess generated at eligible Momentum states. Watch the active stack count and take a safe launch window; a longer setup is not worth losing an opening to an aggressive defender.

TACTIC 4

Distinguish current Reverb from launch bugs

The publisher noted that Northstar's early Reverberation was not inflicting its intended extra damage and later fixed it. Do not use launch footage as a baseline, and verify Enervate, Slow immunity and any mode-specific counter before the fight.

Attacker starting point
Special Damage

Our attacker pick: Momentum and Speed Burst are used to cycle fast specials. Special Damage tests each direct special hit at a controlled Momentum count rather than assuming it speeds Momentum growth. Compare Special Damage against Critical Damage Rating in the same fight at the same rank, awakening, relevant buffs, and node.

Alternative to test: Not reviewed

Defender starting point
Block Penetration

Our defender pick: His rapid power rhythm creates defensive pressure for players who block multiple specials. Block penetration is useful for that guard pattern, with physical resistance as a survival alternative. Compare Block Penetration against Physical Resistance versus the same attacker and mode before investing.
