INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Use the real block interaction, not a synergy proposal

Kabam's v13.1 notes confirm New Xavier School Cyclops can land critical beam hits through a defender's Block. That makes blocked special damage a legitimate consideration, but don't assume fan-requested Prowess mechanics or proposed reworks are part of his current kit.

DECISION 2

Choose SP1 when Armor Break will stick

The recorded rotation aims to apply Armor Break with SP1 and benefit from a special-related Critical Rating boost. Against Armor Break immune targets, a rapid SP1 loop loses one of its main reasons to exist; look for a different attacker or take the straightforward direct-damage route.

DECISION 3

Check team-dependent bonuses separately

Professor X and other synergies can change his beam payoff, but they depend on who joins the team and what content allows synergies. Never include those effects in solo damage expectations; distinguish signature scaling from temporary team additions.
