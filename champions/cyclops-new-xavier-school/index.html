TACTIC 1

Keep New Xavier School distinct from Blue Team

Cyclops (New Xavier School) is a different roster entry and may have its own signature and optic attack timings. Inspect the current version and opponent's Energy Resistance instead of borrowing a rotation from the Blue Team profile.

TACTIC 2

Develop offense only from actually observed effects

If the live kit displays a Prowess, Precision or personal damage state, track when it activates and how long it remains. A historical or proposed buff involving optic charges should not be treated as a guaranteed resource in a new fight.

TACTIC 3

Spend a special in controlled recovery

The practical benefit of an optic-blast special depends on landing it safely. If the opponent is approaching SP3, bait earlier and avoid holding meter for a fixed damage threshold that the current kit has not demonstrated.

TACTIC 4

Check non-contact assumptions and synergy rules

A projectile animation does not establish that every hit is non-contact or immune to node retaliation. Verify actual current hit classification, signature state and teammate synergies before recommending a special against a thorny defender.
