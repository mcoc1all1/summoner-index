Limits & caveats

EDITORIAL FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Editorial decision notes derived from identified game mechanics and attributed source research; these are not in-game performance tests, guarantees, or permissions to reuse third-party text. Checked 2026-11-04; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Trade proximity for controllable Death Spores

Omega Red wants to stay close long enough to stack spores, but staying in punish range without a safe opening is costly. Watch the opponent's recovery and use movement to maintain pressure rather than face-tanking hits.

DECISION 2

Use Heavy to protect the setup

A well-timed Heavy can extend spore pressure through Degeneration. If you retreat for too long without maintaining the effect, the stacks can disappear and the following special has much less value.

DECISION 3

Choose SP1 recovery versus SP2 offense

SP1 can consume spores for a recovery trade-off, whereas SP2 supports Death Field pressure. Robots accumulate spores differently; do not interpret identical fight time as evidence the champion is underperforming.

7★ stat focus · eligibility verified

Attacker starting point
Block Proficiency

Omega Red's Death Spores and Death Field deal base-attack-scaling passive damage, and Kabam confirmed that ordinary attack boosts do not amplify those effects. Block Proficiency is a sustain trial for maintaining safe proximity without excessive chip while spores accumulate, not a damage multiplier. Compare Critical Rating only on eligible direct hits, separating heavy Degeneration and spore damage.

Alternative to test: Not reviewed

Defender starting point
Physical Resistance

Physical Resistance is a mitigation hypothesis against eligible incoming physical hits while proximity pressure builds spores; it does not increase the Death Field, convert Bleed reduction into immunity, or help against unavoidable energy effects. Compare Block Proficiency on an attacker who blocks into Omega Red, with the same spore and node context.

Comparison priority: statcast · Statcast attribute to investigate: Block Proficiency

Block Proficiency can help Omega Red preserve health while remaining in spore range; Kabam explicitly states Death Field and Death Spores do not scale from ordinary attack boosts, so avoid marketing a generic damage-stat relic as their multiplier.

Battlecast comparison: A Battlecast striker could help safely maintain proximity or create a Heavy Degeneration opening, but its activation and damage must be verified separately from the existing passive Death Spores.

Test plan: For one non-Robot defender, match signature, distance, bleed state and time within Death Field. Compare block chip, number of maintained spores, heavy damage and fight duration using one equipped relic at a time.
