INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Build Bloodthirst through controlled openings

Purgatory develops Bloodthirst using eligible basics, blocks and SP1. Read the visible counter rather than assuming every combo contributes equally; a defensive node that punishes blocks can make one method too risky.

DECISION 2

Choose Rampage damage or SP1 recovery according to health

During Rampage, SP2 can offer an Unblockable Degeneration route while SP1 may support recovery. Check whether the opponent resists Degeneration and whether the current health deficit justifies using power on sustain.

DECISION 3

Capture a class soul only when the finishing condition is met

A Special knockout can secure a persistent opponent-class benefit for a later fight. Check the active soul and remaining health before saving power for the finishing blow, and do not assume single-fight modes start fully ramped.
