TACTIC 1

Use the Yaka Arrow as a matchup-dependent tool

Yondu's updated Yaka Arrow can create Rupture pressure and interact with the defender's armor, but this requires eligible hits and status conditions. Watch the current effect and opponent's power before trying to sustain a long blocked-attack sequence.

TACTIC 2

Choose a Rupture or Armor Break route from immunity

The current kit can treat Rupture immunity differently from a defender that accepts the debuff. Verify whether Rupture actually lands and whether an Armor Break alternative occurs before deciding which special should carry the damage plan.

TACTIC 3

Use Heal Block when the target is actually healing

Yondu's updated Heal Block can be stronger against an opponent using Regeneration. Identify the real healing trigger, apply the appropriate arrow interaction during a safe recovery, and do not mistake a passive heal for an ordinary Buff unless the kit handles it.

TACTIC 4

Treat Prowess and Armor theft as conditional

A recent Yondu update expanded his interactions with the opponent's Prowess and Armor Up states. Check that the target actually has eligible effects; a Tech tag or projectile animation alone does not make Yondu a universal counter to every Mutant defender.

Attacker starting point
Special Damage

Our attacker pick: Yaka Arrow Ruptures and Stifle prepare specials, with Fury boosting a later SP2 direct strike. Special Damage targets that direct SP2 hit, not the Rupture damage or armor-removal interaction. Compare Special Damage against Armor Penetration in the same fight at the same rank, awakening, relevant buffs, and node.

Defender starting point
Physical Resistance

Our defender pick: Physical resistance protects Yondu against ordinary physical counters when his Stifle or arrows fail to control spacing or when the defender cannot be held at a safe distance during his Fury setup. It does not increase arrow control. Compare Physical Resistance against Block Penetration versus the same attacker and mode before investing.
