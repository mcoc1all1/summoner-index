TACTIC 1

Identify the correct Cyclops before using an old rotation

Cyclops (Blue Team) has historically emphasized optic Special Attack damage, but his current version and the New Xavier School variant must be treated separately. Check the roster ID and the exact special description before choosing a fight.

TACTIC 2

Build power with short, clean combo openings

His optic blasts can supply ranged special damage, but a risky long combo does not automatically improve the next special. Watch the opponent's power bar and bait a safe recovery rather than chasing an unverified special-damage ramp.

TACTIC 3

Choose the special for current resistance and spacing

Before spending one or two bars, verify whether the defender's Energy Resistance or attack-specific immunity matters. Use a window after a punish, keeping enough distance that a blocked optic attack does not expose Cyclops to a quick counter.

TACTIC 4

Avoid imported New Xavier School or fan-buff effects

Cyclops variants and community redesigns have distinct proposed Prowess and optic-power mechanics. Do not promise an unblockable laser, Power Lock or guaranteed critical hit unless that exact effect exists in the active Blue Team kit.
