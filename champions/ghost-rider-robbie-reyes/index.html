EDITORIAL FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

Champion-specific fight decisions

Champion-specific setup, burst, matchup and failure-condition guidance independently written from the research catalog. These decisions are not site-tested clears; confirm the current live kit and node before relying on them. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

TACTIC 1

Build Ignition Buffs before expecting Soul Barb

Robbie Reyes' Ghost Rider kit uses Ignition resources and Soul Barb interactions. Verify the active Ignition count and target buff state; ordinary Ghost Rider's Judgement loop is a different champion guide.

TACTIC 2

Use Grit to maintain spacing rather than tank hits

Grit may help against an eligible Unstoppable effect, but it is not blanket protection. Keep the defender's meter under control as Ignition develops and avoid forcing an intercept into a dangerous special.

TACTIC 3

Spend power when Soul Barb can actually apply

Choose a special that benefits from the active Ignition and Soul Barb setup, then launch during safe recovery. Power Gain can accelerate your own route but does not disable the opponent's independent node power.

TACTIC 4

Check anti-buff and Soul Barb resistance

Nullify immunity, buff denial and opponents resistant to the relevant Soul Barb effect can change the offensive plan. Confirm this 2026 Robbie Reyes kit, not the older Ghost Rider rework notes.
