Champion-specific fight decisions

TACTIC 1

Track Plasma before attacking into Armor

Havok's Plasma Charge interactions and their timing are central to the matchup. Watch both champions' active charges and the defender's Armor Up effects before following an automatic special sequence.

TACTIC 2

Bank resources with spacing

Keep the opponent at a manageable power level while Plasma effects develop. If a node accelerates special gain or restricts movement, favor clean bait-and-punish windows over attempting to wait for a perfect charge state.

TACTIC 3

Choose the special for current charges

SP2 is most meaningful when its damage conditions are met, so inspect the resources and defender's energy resistance before spending power. If the charge state has been disrupted, rebuilding may beat a premature burst.

TACTIC 4

Respect armor and purification differences

Armor Up, Plasma immunity, power-drain interactions and debuff or passive-effect restrictions do not all operate identically. Validate the current defender and node wording instead of treating Havok as a universal energy-damage answer.
