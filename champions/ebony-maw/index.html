TACTIC 1

Work on Persuasion through the actual conditions

Ebony Maw develops Persuasion while completing Thanos-themed missions. Check which mission is active and whether the next attack satisfies it; a random hit does not necessarily advance the correct objective.

TACTIC 2

Use Falter as a timed defensive opportunity

Falter can make eligible attacks Miss under specific rules. Confirm the opponent has the effect before taking an aggressive action, and do not attempt to block an Unblockable special on an assumed Falter trigger.

TACTIC 3

Convert the developed state into Degeneration

Once Persuasion and mission progress permit it, choose a special or follow-up that applies the intended Degeneration or power interaction. Use an actual safe punish and track whether the defender resists the effect.

TACTIC 4

Watch anti-Miss and Nullify counterplay

Vigilance or True Accuracy may defeat Falter, while Nullify requires eligible ordinary Buffs. Power Steal and Degeneration do not guarantee suppression of every node-generated power or passive damage source.
