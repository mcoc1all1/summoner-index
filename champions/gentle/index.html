TACTIC 1

Build Muscle Mass with health cost in mind

Gentle can gain Muscle Mass to increase offensive pressure, but large stacks may carry a health cost. Watch the actual count and remaining health before committing to more buildup; high damage does not automatically mean a safer fight.

TACTIC 2

Use Daunted or Reverb for the right target

These effects can help with specific combat problems, but they are not interchangeable. Verify the defender's existing protections and whether the intended effect is active before pushing for another Muscle Mass stack.

TACTIC 3

Cash out before the maximum stack becomes costly

When the offensive state is developed, a safe special or direct attack may be better than continuing to grow stacks at a health penalty. Inspect enemy power and current Unstoppable availability before the conversion.

TACTIC 4

Do not interpret Unstoppable as damage immunity

Unstoppable may protect a particular attack sequence from interruption, not from every hit. Slow, healing restrictions and passive node damage can make overbuilding Muscle Mass a poor choice.
