TACTIC 1

Plan the potion setup early

Diablo's utility depends on his potion and philosopher's-mixture mechanics. Confirm the active mixture and its remaining duration before relying on a special or a defensive interaction that needs an established resource.

TACTIC 2

Choose healing or damage by matchup

His Poison-focused damage can be attractive against susceptible opponents, but immune or purifying defenders change the tradeoff. Prioritize survival and an appropriate mixture rather than pushing a Poison sequence that cannot stick.

TACTIC 3

Wait for a real conversion window

Watch the active potion state before launching a special and make sure the defender's next attack is manageable. If the timer is nearly over, a safe shorter punish may deliver more value than a late idealized burst.

TACTIC 4

Beware of nullification and poison limits

Poison immunity, Purify, buff control and nodes that shorten or block effects can defeat the planned loop. Recheck the actual mixture and debuff indicators every cycle, particularly after a special or power-drain event.

Stun immunity requires Grain of Uru selected for the fight and the Philosopher's Mix active after a Special Attack. It is not always active before the Mix is triggered or when it expires.

1 effect
Armor Break
Debuffs

Armor Break Debuff immunity requires selecting Grain of Uru in Diablo's pre-fight menu and having the resulting Philosopher's Mix active after a Special Attack. This does not establish immunity to every passive Armor Break effect.

Resistance / scope note: Poison potency reduction is not immunity: Poison can still be present. Stun and Armor Break protection requires Grain of Uru and active Mix.

Attacker starting point
Block Proficiency

For Diablo, Block Proficiency is a survival experiment for the blocking required by the setup, not a direct offensive damage multiplier. Keep the source-linked setup constant: Select only needed pre-fight ingredients; unused ingredient slots grant Vials of Might. Compare Physical Resistance against the same opponent, rank and resources. Block Proficiency addresses damage taken during slow ingredient, Poison and recovery setup; it cannot increase Poison potency by itself.
