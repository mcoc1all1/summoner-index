TACTIC 1

Choose the finish for utility

Different combo endings support Suppression or Heal Block. Watch the current defensive Phase option as well, rather than repeating one combo ending and assuming every utility effect will be present.

TACTIC 2

Translate Armor into Solar Energy

SP2 and its timing-sensitive block interactions can convert Armor Up into Solar Energy. Practice the timing in a low-risk fight first; losing Armor without the expected conversion leaves a weak SP1 follow-up.

TACTIC 3

Use SP1 at the right power state

SP1 spends Solar Energy for a stronger payoff and can influence the opponent's power. A prepared SP1 when the defender is already manageable is safer than attempting the full sequence into three bars.

TACTIC 4

Track stun, armor and phase counters

Armor removal, anti-Phase and node-altered power gain can interrupt the kit. The reference to a defensive Phase mechanic does not mean Viv can ignore Unblockable or anti-Miss specials.
