INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Choose Light or Medium finishers for the defender's actual control need

Viv Vision can use combo enders for Suppression or Heal Block while resetting Phase availability. Target the effect that matters and check the next attack's timing, rather than assuming both types of control are always active.

DECISION 2

Prepare SP2 block inputs with enough Armor to convert

Her SP2 can convert Armor Up into Solar Energy through timed block inputs. Check remaining Armor and practice the timing before expecting full resources from every SP2 animation.

DECISION 3

Use SP1 while the defender's power permits Solar Energy payoff

SP1 can spend prepared Solar Energy on damage and power-related follow-up. If the enemy power bar has moved outside the useful range or the state expired, reconsider the Special rather than forcing a weak cash-out.
