TACTIC 1

Build Rage before expecting the Berserk offense

Weapon X can develop Rage to enter Berserk. Watch the Rage count and the defender's current meter while attacking; do not assume an ordinary Wolverine regeneration rotation applies to Weapon X.

TACTIC 2

Use regeneration without treating Berserk as immortal

Regeneration can sustain him under the right conditions, but Heal Block and passive damage remain threats. Enter Berserk with room for safe aggression rather than allowing a dangerous special just to accelerate the ramp.

TACTIC 3

Spend a special while Berserk pressure is available

During Berserk, Bleed and Unblockable attacks can create value when the target is susceptible. Verify the exact active state and use a safe punish; Unstoppable does not protect against all incoming damage.

TACTIC 4

Watch Slow, Bleed immunity and Heal Block

Slow and other anti-Unstoppable tools, Bleed immunity and healing restrictions may dismantle different parts of his plan. Weapon X is not Classic Wolverine or Wolverine (X-23).
