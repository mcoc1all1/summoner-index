Conditional fight checklist

Four-stage, profile-specific conditional research checklists generated from the site’s structured ability and protection records. DECISION 1

Check Battered before the opener

Before bringing Wave into this fight, inspect Battered, Debilitate, Physical Vulnerability and Tracking in the live kit and read the quest node. Start with one safe basic-attack punish and observe which listed mechanic actually activates. Treat this as a research-derived starting checklist, not a verified opening rotation.

DECISION 2

Develop Battered and monitor Debilitate

The structured index associates Battered with Wave. The ability name alone does not establish activation timing, damage, immunity, or matchup quality; verify its live description and active indicators before using it as a plan. Track Debilitate separately: the appearance of one ability does not prove that the second triggers on the same attack or persists for the same duration. Reset spacing before committing to another sequence.

DECISION 3

Choose a safe special payoff

Choose an SP1, SP2 or SP3 only after checking the current meter, actual active effects and whether the defender has a safe punish window. For Wave, the index highlights Battered alongside Debilitate, but it does not demonstrate a validated special order or measured damage outcome. If the opponent is close to a dangerous special, bait that attack before attempting a longer payoff.

DECISION 4

Handle Battered failure conditions

If the expected effect is absent, stop the proposed sequence, inspect the active node and ability description, and adapt using verified basic-attack and special recovery windows. No independently confirmed protection is established by this checklist; check incoming damage types before deliberately trading hits. Recheck the defender after a special, cleanse or node transition, and avoid treating this research draft as a guaranteed counter to a named champion.
