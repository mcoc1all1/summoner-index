TACTIC 1

Build the relevant debuff state before damage

Elektra's damage and ability-accuracy effects can depend on what debuffs are active on the opponent. Confirm that the selected debuff actually lands before planning to ignore the defender's avoidance or defensive ability.

TACTIC 2

Choose which debuff is needed for this enemy

A defender with Evade requires a different answer from one that heals rapidly or relies on armor. Use the current kit's eligible debuff interaction rather than assuming Elektra suppresses every defensive mechanic just because one state is active.

TACTIC 3

Spend the special inside the debuff window

Launch the next special while the intended condition is still present, from a safe recovery rather than a risky intercept. If the opponent Purifies immediately, a short direct-hit route may outperform a setup that never stabilizes.

TACTIC 4

Do not conflate damage and full shutdown

Ability-accuracy reduction is conditional and may not affect every passive or node. Check the opponent's immunities and whether the attack is a guaranteed critical hit before assigning Elektra a blanket counter role.
