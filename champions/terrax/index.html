Champion-specific fight decisions

Champion-specific setup, burst, matchup and failure-condition guidance independently written from the research catalog. These decisions are not site-tested clears; confirm the current live kit and node before relying on them. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

TACTIC 1

Enter Rock Field with enough space

Terrax's Rock Field applies close-range pressure but does not make every trade safe. Before starting it, control the defender's special bar and your position; backing yourself into a corner can lose much of the field's offensive value.

TACTIC 2

Manage Power Cosmic rather than rushing

Power Cosmic and attack-charge interactions affect the longer fight. Inspect available charges before spending another special; trying to recreate a peak-field sequence without the resources can leave you exposed to an aggressive defender.

TACTIC 3

Keep the defender inside the field

Field pressure is strongest when the opponent remains within the relevant proximity. Use safe spacing and baiting rather than blindly dashing forward; the next Special Attack should come from a recovery window, not from chasing the opponent during its animation.

TACTIC 4

Respect anti-buff and node damage

Nullify, Neutralize, buff duration changes and node effects that punish power gain can disrupt the plan. Do not infer Poison immunity or extra Indestructible charges from community-proposed buffs; verify only effects that exist in the current live kit.
