Champion-specific fight decisions

Champion-specific setup, burst, matchup and failure-condition guidance independently written from the research catalog. These decisions are not site-tested clears; confirm the current live kit and node before relying on them. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

TACTIC 1

Count Virus charges before expecting Infection

Bastion's attacking plan builds Virus charges toward an Infection state. Inspect the charge counter and whether the target can be infected rather than relying on a memorized hit count from another defender.

TACTIC 2

Use Pursuit and Fury in the real pressure window

Once Infection develops, consider whether Pursuit or Fury actually enhances the available attacks. Keep the defender’s power within a baitable range and avoid losing a developed state to an unnecessary extended combo.

TACTIC 3

Spend a special when Infection can amplify damage

Convert a prepared Infection state into the next useful special or basic punish. Disintegration and power-reroute terms require their own triggers; don't claim an SP2 burst if the intended effect is absent.

TACTIC 4

Avoid treating Virus as universal power control

The defender's immunity, Purify or node power mechanics can prevent the planned Infection loop. Read the live Virus trigger and active resources rather than assuming Bastion shuts down all special generation.

Critical Rating

For Bastion, a Critical Rating trial changes eligible would-be critical event frequency; it must not be assumed to increase noncritical debuff tick damage. Keep the source-linked setup constant: Use medium attacks and well-timed blocks to build Virus charges. Compare Critical Damage Rating against the same opponent, rank and resources. Defender starting point
Armor Penetration

For Bastion on defense, Armor Penetration may improve the defender's outgoing direct hits against armored attackers; it does not reduce incoming damage, so a mitigation choice is a real alternative. Kit limitation: More than three unique opponent buffs prevent new Virus charges. Compare Block Proficiency using the same attack type, defensive sequence, relic state and node; no across-mode ranking or gameplay result is claimed.
