TACTIC 1

Start with ordinary critical-hit pressure

X-23 can establish Bleed damage during basic exchanges, especially when her critical-hit interactions cooperate. Count actual active Bleeds instead of assuming the opponent is vulnerable to every trigger; keep enough room to bait a special safely.

TACTIC 2

Value regeneration over a risky setup

Her recovery can make repeated fights manageable under the correct signature and health conditions. Check whether the node restricts healing; intentionally taking a heavy attack to reach a regeneration threshold is rarely a reliable strategy.

TACTIC 3

Choose special payoff from current debuffs

A Special Attack can add useful damage or capitalize on Bleed pressure, but the best time depends on how many Bleeds the opponent retains. If the target is immune to Bleed, use shorter direct-hit openings instead of waiting for an unavailable damage-over-time spike.

TACTIC 4

Watch Purify and Heal Block

Purify, Bleed immunity and Heal Block can remove the two strengths that usually justify X-23 in a path. A high critical-hit rate does not solve those restrictions, and her healing mechanics do not establish immunity to passive damage.
