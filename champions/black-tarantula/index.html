TACTIC 1

Build Cunning through controlled combo progress

Black Tarantula uses combo-based Cunning to prepare a later damage window. Watch the personal count rather than assuming every blocked attack contributes and preserve room to bait the opponent's next special.

TACTIC 2

Use Evade and Regeneration only when available

The current kit lists both avoidance and recovery utility, but their triggers and availability differ. Do not intentionally accept a hit solely on the assumption that Cunning grants a guaranteed Evade or immediate heal.

TACTIC 3

Spend SP2 in the developed Cunning window

The intended payoff involves repeated SP2 bursts once the proper Cunning state exists. Verify the active effect and safe launch opportunity; a long chain fails when the opponent becomes unblockable or power-immune.

TACTIC 4

Respect anti-Evade and anti-healing counters

True Accuracy, Slow, Heal Block and node-driven power changes may disrupt different parts of the plan. Check the defender’s actual countermeasures before relying on a repeated-special sequence.
