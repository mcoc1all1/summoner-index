DECISION 1

Check Evade before the opener

Before bringing Black Tarantula into this fight, inspect Cunning, Evade, Regeneration and Vulnerability in the live kit and read the quest node. Start with one safe basic-attack punish and observe which listed mechanic actually activates. Treat this as a research-derived starting checklist, not a verified opening rotation.

DECISION 2

Develop Evade and monitor Cunning

Evade may affect survival for Black Tarantula, but only while its actual activation condition holds. Read the status icons and avoid deliberately taking an otherwise unsafe attack until the relevant protection has been observed. Track Cunning separately: the appearance of one ability does not prove that the second triggers on the same attack or persists for the same duration. Reset spacing before committing to another sequence.

DECISION 3

Choose a safe special payoff

Choose an SP1, SP2 or SP3 only after checking the current meter, actual active effects and whether the defender has a safe punish window. For Black Tarantula, the index highlights Evade alongside Cunning, but it does not demonstrate a validated special order or measured damage outcome. If the opponent is close to a dangerous special, bait that attack before attempting a longer payoff.

DECISION 4

Handle Evade failure conditions

If Evade is disabled or expires, return to blocks and Dexterity appropriate to the attack; do not treat an inactive protection as permission to intercept or trade. A structured Poison protection is also listed; verify its availability, effect form and conditions instead of calling it unconditional immunity. Recheck the defender after a special, cleanse or node transition, and avoid treating this research draft as a guaranteed counter to a named champion.
