INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Check Coldsnap before relying on anti-Evade

Iceman's Coldsnap can stop Evade while it is active, but that is a debuff applied to the opponent, not an always-on anti-Evade effect. If Coldsnap cannot land or expires, your next basic attack may still trigger Evade.

DECISION 2

Treat Ice Armor as an expendable safety layer

SP2 can offer a stronger immediate damage play while consuming Ice Armor. Watch its restoration timing before accepting any hit; SP3 may be a better route when you need to regain the defensive layer.

DECISION 3

Detonate only stacks you can actually maintain

Build Frostbites through eligible critical hits, then use a safely timed Heavy to cash them out. The best window depends on the visible stack count and the opponent's cold-effect protection; an old champion spotlight may predate later utility changes.

Attacker starting point
Critical Rating

Editorial comparison: Iceman applies Coldsnap and builds Frostbites from critical hits before detonating with Heavy. Critical Rating might improve eligible critical-hit frequency, but must not be described as amplifying the Frostbite detonation itself. Compare equal Ice Armor states and identical enemies; Coldsnap-immune defenders undermine the rotation regardless of focus.

Alternative to test: Not reviewed

Defender starting point
Physical Resistance

Physical Resistance is a matchup-specific mitigation trial against physical incoming hits while Ice Armor is down or recharging. It does not speed Ice Armor recovery or prevent a Coldsnap-immune attacker from avoiding his control. Energy Resistance is an alternative against energy-heavy attackers.
