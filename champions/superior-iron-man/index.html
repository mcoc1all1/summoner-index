TACTIC 1

Build different Superior buffs deliberately

Superior Iron Man's Cosmic kit can create diverse Superior buffs with different offensive and defensive roles. Read the active effects rather than treating this as Classic Iron Man's Arc Overload or Infinity War's Molecular Armor rotation.

TACTIC 2

Keep the desired buffs through the Fury window

His offensive plan benefits from retaining useful buff diversity while a Fury payoff is available. Track duration and opponent power together; converting into damage is only useful if the required buffs survive the next special animation.

TACTIC 3

Choose a special for the available buff mix

A fight with developed offensive buffs can justify a stronger special, while a short encounter or buff-removing defender may favor an early safe punish. Verify the actual active buff count instead of assuming the same order will work from an empty opening.

TACTIC 4

Watch Neutralize and buff-duration nodes

Neutralize, Stagger and Nullify can interrupt the entire Superior-buff engine. Do not import an unofficial proposed Arc Reactor kit or new healing triggers unless the publisher has shipped them; current on-screen buffs are the relevant evidence.
