Champion-specific fight decisions

Champion-specific setup, burst, matchup and failure-condition guidance independently written from the research catalog. These decisions are not site-tested clears; confirm the current live kit and node before relying on them. Checked 2026-11-04; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

TACTIC 1

Build Vibro-Shock methodically

Normal attacks and SP1 help build charges. Track the current stack rather than using every heavy as soon as it becomes available; a low-charge release sacrifices the payoff.

TACTIC 2

Start SP2 with correct spacing

SP2 can apply a passive Stun and Physical Vulnerability opening. Check whether the defender's Stun controls apply and whether you will be near enough to charge the heavy during recovery.

TACTIC 3

Release the heavy before control ends

The charged heavy cashes out Vibro-Shock resources. Watch the remaining passive-Stun window; a maximal charge is worthless if the defender recovers and intercepts before release.

TACTIC 4

Mind charge spending and safety

Auto-Block and other defensive actions can consume charges needed for offense. Against special Stun-immune conditions, use ordinary punish windows instead of assuming the standard SP2-heavy sequence works.

7★ stat focus · eligibility verified

Attacker starting point
Critical Rating

Shocker cannot land critical hits. His kit instead uses Critical Rating to reduce the opponent's Armor Rating on attacks, so this trial measures armor reduction during the SP2 Stun into charged Heavy window rather than extra critical-hit frequency. Compare identical Vibro-Shock charge totals and heavy release timing against an armored opponent; Armor Penetration is an alternative to measure, not a known superior choice.

Alternative to test: Not reviewed

Defender starting point
Armor Penetration

Armor Penetration affects outgoing hits against armor; it does not protect Shocker when an attacker lands or blocks a hit. If the defender objective is to survive long enough to accumulate Vibro-Shock Charges or trigger his charge-based Auto-Block, compare Block Proficiency instead. Neither option replaces his charge requirements.
