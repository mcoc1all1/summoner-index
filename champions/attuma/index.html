TACTIC 1

Use personal Concussion for the actual matchup

Attuma can interact with Concussion and Hydration in ways that improve certain defensive matchups. Check whether the self-effect is active and whether the intended defender ability can truly be reduced.

TACTIC 2

Grow Hydration without forcing risky damage

Watch the Hydration counter and any Cruelty or Bleed state as attacks connect. A useful stack only matters if the next hit is safe; do not intentionally absorb dangerous contact simply to maintain the ramp.

TACTIC 3

Choose a special while True Accuracy matters

Use the available control window when the defender's avoidance or Auto-Block actually needs an answer. True Accuracy and a damaging Bleed solve different problems, so verify the target and special effect before converting.

TACTIC 4

Check anti-Concussion and damage-form limits

Ability-accuracy reduction does not disable every passive or node, and Bleed immunity removes a separate payoff. Hydration is not unconditional damage immunity, especially against non-contact or passive damage.

Attacker starting point
Critical Rating

For Attuma, a Critical Rating trial changes eligible would-be critical event frequency; it must not be assumed to increase noncritical debuff tick damage. Keep the source-linked setup constant: Use a safe heavy to create personal Concussion by converting an eligible debuff or spending Hydration. Compare Critical Damage Rating against the same opponent, rank and resources. Do not mistake a changed direct hit for proof of longer resource uptime or stronger debuff ticks.

Alternative to test: Not reviewed

Defender starting point
Armor Penetration

For Attuma on defense, Armor Penetration may improve the defender's outgoing direct hits against armored attackers; it does not reduce incoming damage, so a mitigation choice is a real alternative. Kit limitation: Coldsnap accelerates Hydration loss; Shock and Incinerate interactions spend stacks. Compare Block Proficiency using the same attack type, defensive sequence, relic state and node; no across-mode ranking or gameplay result is claimed.
