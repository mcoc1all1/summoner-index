Champion-specific fight decisions

Champion-specific setup, burst, matchup and failure-condition guidance independently written from the research catalog. These decisions are not site-tested clears; confirm the current live kit and node before relying on them. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

TACTIC 1

Open with controlled pressure

Killmonger's defensive and offensive value changes with his active states. Establish clean punishes while watching the Reverberation and armor-related indicators, rather than assuming all incoming contact is safe.

TACTIC 2

Use the SP2 protection intentionally

His SP2 can establish Indestructible charges under the relevant kit rules. Treat those charges as a limited safety resource and track how they are consumed; they are not permission to ignore an entire multi-hit special.

TACTIC 3

Select the route for the defender

Bleed pressure and direct damage do not have the same value in every matchup. Check the defender's Bleed protection and the intended special's current description before spending power on a familiar combo.

TACTIC 4

Plan for ability interruption

Defenders who prevent or remove defensive states, as well as nodes that punish blocks or repeated contact, can break a riskier approach. If the Indestructible window is absent, return to normal dexterity and spacing.

7★ stat focus · eligibility verified

Attacker starting point
Critical Rating

Killmonger's critical hits can inflict Bleeds and his SP3 converts prior Bleed applications into a large finishing sequence. Critical Rating is therefore a test for Bleed trigger frequency on eligible hits, not guaranteed extra bleed damage per tick. Compare Special Damage with the same True Strike windows, landed hit count and SP3 setup; keep Bleed-immune opponents separate.

Alternative to test: Not reviewed

Defender starting point
Armor Rating

Armor Rating tests survival in encounters where Killmonger's Vibranium Armor remains active and the defender receives physical hits. It does not produce Indestructible charges or protect his kit from Armor Break. Compare Physical Resistance against the same attack type and record Reverberation windows separately from raw damage reduction.

Alternative to test: Not reviewed

Battlecast comparison: A skill Battlecast's striker may facilitate recovery punish windows that grant True Strike, but only the selected rune's documented benefit should be measured; Indestructible charges remain a separate SP2 mechanic.
