INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Earn a Psi-Charge before planning power denial

Psylocke can obtain a Psi-Charge when the opponent reaches a power threshold. Watch that threshold and use SP1 at an appropriate time; opening with SP1 before a charge is prepared may not start the intended control loop.

DECISION 2

Preserve a charge while the defender has zero power

Her recurring power-suppression plan needs a Psi-Charge and an opponent at zero power. Heavy attacks and certain Specials can spend the charge, so do not use those moves automatically while the defender is contained.

DECISION 3

Choose SP2 Power Lock only when the shorter control fails

SP2 can create a temporary Power Lock option, but it costs more power and may face resistance or immunity. Check the defender's current power bar and node rules before replacing a working SP1 drain cycle.
