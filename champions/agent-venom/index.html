TACTIC 1

Watch his defensive debuff-shrugging trigger

Agent Venom can shrug off eligible incoming debuffs, but that is a chance-based or otherwise conditional mechanic under the current kit. Confirm whether the effect actually purified before acting as though it granted immunity to all damage.

TACTIC 2

Use basic attacks to develop the available damage

His offensive hit strings can create damage opportunities that depend on critical hits, Bleed or other effects in the current version. Check the enemy's immunity before extending a combo and avoid assigning him Venom's or Anti-Venom's separate symbiote rotations.

TACTIC 3

Choose the special from a safe punish

Use a recovery opening and the defender's current power state to pick the next special. A damage-over-time payoff only works if its intended effect can remain active; do not assume every symbiote-themed attack deals the same damage type.

TACTIC 4

Keep synergies separate from the base roster entry

His teammates can add utility or damage changes, but a synergy shown in another guide is not an always-on Agent Venom ability. Review actual roster partners, Purify restrictions and ability-accuracy changes before committing to a matchup.
