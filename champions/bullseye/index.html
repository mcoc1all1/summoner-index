EDITORIAL FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Editorial decision notes derived from identified game mechanics and attributed source research; these are not in-game performance tests, guarantees, or permissions to reuse third-party text. Checked 2026-11-06; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Treat Killer Instinct as a timed threat

After Bullseye reaches a bar of power, Killer Instinct can Evade basic attacks while he is not blocking. Attackers should watch the active timer and avoid forcing unsafe punish attempts; the effect cannot simply be assumed disabled by Ability Accuracy reduction.

DECISION 2

Connect counterattack to the right special

When using Bullseye, an Evade counterattack can create a brief opening for a follow-up special. A successful Perfect Release contributes to Dialed In, but mistiming it changes that progression; practice the release rather than assuming the bonus is automatic.

DECISION 3

Plan for unavoidable-looking Bleed

When choosing Bleed protection against Bullseye, distinguish Basic from Special Attack avoidance. Kabam's 2024 balance announcement specified that Instant Bleed should stop triggering when an opponent uses Dexterity to avoid his Basic Attacks, while retaining the trigger when Special Attacks are avoided. Do not apply an older blanket Dexterity warning to every attack: blocked hits, other Bleed sources and Killer Instinct Evade still require separate defenses. Recheck the current client rather than treating the historic update statement as this site's gameplay test.

Scope not reviewed

Bullseye's I Never Miss ability always prevents Precision effects and triggers a personal Cruelty Buff when immunity activates, subject to that Buff's cooldown. Precision is usually a beneficial effect; this record is not immunity to an incoming Precision Debuff or to critical hits generally.
