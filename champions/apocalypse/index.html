EDITORIAL FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Editorial decision notes derived from identified game mechanics and attributed source research; these are not in-game performance tests, guarantees, or permissions to reuse third-party text. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Build Genetic Code with path planning

Genetic Code can carry over between eligible fights and change the available attack plan. Check your intended first opponents; Robot interactions have exclusions, so a Mutant class label alone does not prove every charge will be granted.

DECISION 2

Choose the combo ender for the debuff

Before SP1 or SP2, decide whether a Light- or Medium-ending sequence applies the effect needed, then refresh personal debuffs with a safely timed Heavy. Node restrictions and ability-accuracy control can alter which debuffs actually stick.

DECISION 3

Decide whether SP3 is worth the time

SP3 can strengthen an established debuff plan but costs significant time and power. In short fights, a simpler attack sequence may be safer; do not assume maximum Genetic Code or Horseman team utility without its required setup.
