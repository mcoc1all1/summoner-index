TACTIC 1

Create Pacifism by baiting enemy specials

Groot's Pacifism route depends on appropriate opponent actions. Instead of attacking continuously, watch the defender's power and bait a safe special when it develops the relevant resource.

TACTIC 2

Observe which buffs Pacifism generates

The resulting Fury, Cruelty or Regeneration buffs can support different goals. Check which one is active before choosing an aggressive sequence; a damage buff is not automatically a defensive heal.

TACTIC 3

Spend a special while offensive buffs are useful

When Pacifism has generated the correct offensive state, a safe special can convert it into damage. If the defender can strip buffs, shorten the setup instead of continuing to bait more specials.

TACTIC 4

Check buff denial and healing restrictions

Nullify, Neutralize, Stagger and Heal Block can remove separate advantages. Groot's regeneration and high health do not grant full safety against passive damage or every unblockable attack.
