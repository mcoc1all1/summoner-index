Champion-specific fight decisions

Champion-specific setup, burst, matchup and failure-condition guidance independently written from the research catalog. These decisions are not site-tested clears; confirm the current live kit and node before relying on them. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

TACTIC 1

Apply Bleed where it sticks

A light-ending combo can help build Bleed pressure and support Internal Bleed. Check Bleed immunity at the start; a target that cannot Bleed changes the value of repeated combo finishers.

TACTIC 2

Earn Tactical Charges for utility

SP1 can develop Tactical Charges. Pay attention to the actual threshold before relying on effects such as anti-Miss, anti-Evade or Unblockable; a single SP1 is not the same as a full tactical setup.

TACTIC 3

Choose SP2 for the punish

SP2 adds Bleed pressure and may create a Stun opening under the charge conditions. If Stun is blocked by the node, use the special for damage rather than planning a follow-up that will not happen.

TACTIC 4

Do not spend the Decoy accidentally

An awakened Nick Fury can use the Life Model Decoy mechanic, which changes the fight after it triggers. Distinguish that one-time state from ordinary healing and check remaining quest health before risking the next exchange.

Awakened Nick Fury becomes Stun Immune after the Life Model Decoy is destroyed, with his permanent Fury's Fury Buff. While striking Auto-Block he also has a separate conditional Stun Immunity interaction. Do not treat unawakened Nick as permanently immune.

Resistance / scope note: Stun immunity after the decoy is destroyed is conditional and is not listed as an unconditional immunity.
