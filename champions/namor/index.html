TACTIC 1

Build Outrage through safe exchanges

Namor's Outrage buildup supports a later offensive window. Monitor its active count while taking ordinary punishes, and avoid deliberately eating damage just to accelerate the resource when the defender has a dangerous Special Attack.

TACTIC 2

Enter Imperius Rex with time to attack

Imperius Rex is a limited damage window, not a permanent state. Trigger it with enough spacing and opponent health to make the follow-up useful, rather than entering it immediately before the defender can launch an unavoidable special.

TACTIC 3

Choose specials from the current mode

An SP2 or other special during the developed mode can have different value than one launched before the ramp. Check remaining mode duration and opponent power; a reliable early punish often beats holding for a theoretical long-fight maximum.

TACTIC 4

Do not promise unqualified reflected damage

Namor's famous damage-reflection interaction depends on signature development and hit timing. Check awakening, the signature level and the incoming damage type before treating him as protection against passive damage or every quest node.

Critical Damage Rating

Our attacker pick: Namor prepares a high-Fury Imperius Rex phase with many ordinary hits. Critical Damage Rating compares value during that established phase; Special Damage is stronger only if the special-hit share dominates. Our defender pick: Reflective defense and a short burst window are not permanent mitigation.
