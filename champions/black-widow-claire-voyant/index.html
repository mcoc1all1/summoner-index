INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Select the curse with fourth-light finishers before choosing SP2

Claire Voyant can cycle Blood, Plague and Hellfire with a fourth-light combo ending. Choose the form for healing, power control or damage before spending power; a mismatched curse makes the next SP2 solve a different problem.

DECISION 2

Use target immunity interactions only for the matching curse

Each curse has an associated damage effect and possible utility from the opponent's immunity. Check which effect is relevant rather than assuming Poison, Bleed and Incinerate immunities are interchangeable.

DECISION 3

Save SP3 Death Touch setup for fights long enough to use it

SP3 can spend accumulated resources on Death Touch, but its value depends on a prepared charge state and time remaining. In short fights, SP2 in the appropriate curse may provide a safer and more direct result.
