TACTIC 1

Choose the correct curse before entering the sequence

Claire Voyant cycles curses with different matching immunities and effects. Identify whether the next fight needs healing, damage or power control and inspect the active curse rather than assuming all three benefits run simultaneously.

TACTIC 2

Cycle curses through safe timing

Change the curse state through eligible attacks and watch whether the next interaction is currently active. Matching immunity applies under its own conditions; an incorrect curse can leave Claire exposed to an effect she normally handles elsewhere.

TACTIC 3

Spend specials for the current curse payoff

Her specials may trade between regeneration, power manipulation and damage depending on the active state. Launch after checking the defender's buffs, power bar and the curse icon, not a fixed SP2 habit.

TACTIC 4

Check immunity timing and buff denial

Nullify or Buff Immunity utility requires an eligible enemy Buff; passive effects may not count. The wrong curse is not universal Bleed, Poison or Incinerate protection, and Heal Block may remove the desired sustain.
