TACTIC 1

Start with the Rupture hits

His first-medium and fourth-light interactions can add Rupture; verify the defender accepts those effects before spending time on a larger Lycanthropy setup.

TACTIC 2

Howl at a safe distance

Create spacing for Chilling Howl, then develop Lycanthropy Charges through the relevant states and power crossings. A howl started into an approaching defender can erase any benefit.

TACTIC 3

Use SP2 during the Curse

Once Curse of Lycanthropy is active, maintain useful offensive contact to support uptime and release SP2 for the payoff. If the opponent breaks the sequence, rebuild rather than counting on the prior Curse.

TACTIC 4

Plan for buff and immunity exceptions

SP1 can interact with selected Regeneration and Precision buffs, but not every passive effect qualifies. Rupture immunity or aggressive anti-Unstoppable nodes make a short, conservative fight plan preferable.
