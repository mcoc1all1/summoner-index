INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Use guaranteed-critical nunchaku hits only on open targets

Hit-Monkey can create a dependable critical sequence with the appropriate Medium-Light-Medium timing against an unblocked opponent. A defender who blocks or applies anti-critical protection changes the outcome; do not force the sequence into a ready block.

DECISION 2

Select Assassin's Cunning or Primal Rage intentionally

After obtaining a Scheme, your next defensive movement or aggressive attacks can lead toward different effects. Pick the state that addresses the defender's evasion or damage requirements instead of alternating at random.

DECISION 3

Launch SP2 after a critical hit only when the opening is safe

A recent critical hit can set up SP2's stronger sequence, but the timing is not worth eating a Special counterattack. Track whether the required Scheme and power are available before treating the critical SP2 as guaranteed.

Defender starting point
Critical Damage Rating

Critical Damage Rating can improve eligible defending critical hits, but it does not make the AI choose a Scheme, force a critical hit on non-guaranteed attacks or protect against a counter. Test against opponents who can withstand critical pressure, and compare a mitigation focus where survival matters.
