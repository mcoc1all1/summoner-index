TACTIC 1

Keep Rupture and power linked

Spider-Man 2099 applies Rupture through basic attacks, with additional burst opportunities connected to changes in the defender's power bars. Watch the opponent's meter before pushing another combo; the value of a Rupture stack depends on when the next power event occurs.

TACTIC 2

Use a buff attempt to extend debuffs

Instead of receiving ordinary Buffs, Spider-Man 2099 pauses his personal Debuffs when he would gain one. A Dexterity dodge can help maintain the setup; check the pause icon and the available Resistance Up stacks rather than assuming every dash preserves all effects.

TACTIC 3

Build the SP2 around unique debuffs

SP2 rewards the number of different Debuff types on the opponent and introduces Wither, which can alter Power Gain interactions. Set up Physical Vulnerability, Exhaustion or other available effects first only when safe; a high Rupture count alone is not the same as varied Debuff types.

TACTIC 4

Do not force a Purify matchup

Frequent Purify removes the debuffs that support his burst, while damage-over-time nodes can bypass his ordinary resistances. Verify whether the target actually permits Rupture and Wither, and remember that his personal avoidance is not immunity to an entire Special Attack.
