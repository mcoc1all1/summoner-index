EDITORIAL FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

Champion-specific fight decisions

Champion-specific tactical drafts assembled from attributed research and original editorial checks. Source-expression comparisons and independent selection reviews are still incomplete; no current-build combat test or guaranteed matchup is claimed. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

TACTIC 1

Bank Armor Up before burst

Well-timed blocks and SP1 can build Armor Up for Colossus. The actual count matters for both resistance and offensive opportunity; don't convert your entire plan into an early special with no developed stack.

TACTIC 2

Heavy cancel during a true opening

A heavy started on a Stunned opponent can create a short Fury, which supports a heavy-to-SP2 punish. Confirm the Stun and distance; against Stun-immune defenders, find another safe recovery window.

TACTIC 3

SP2 is the payoff, not the setup

SP2 can exploit temporary Fury and armor development for an Unblockable burst. If the opponent's recovery is uncertain, spend the special normally rather than losing an attempt to an interrupted heavy.

TACTIC 4

Respect the form of damage

Colossus has meaningful protection against several damage effects, but a debuff immunity is not always passive-damage immunity. Check the node's actual effect form and any Armor Up removal mechanics.
