INDEPENDENT FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Practical decisions based on the linked kit and published mechanics, not an in-game performance test or a guaranteed counter. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Start by reading Armor Up, not just class

Colossus enters combat with indefinite Armor Up buffs, and his X-Men teammates can change the starting stack. Kabam documented a post-beta adjustment to his opening armor. Count today's buffs before judging whether a lengthy Armor-building rotation is necessary.

DECISION 2

Convert a safe Stun into a damage window

After a successful Stun, the recorded heavy interaction offers a short Fury opportunity. Link that to SP2 only when the defender can be safely stunned and spacing permits a heavy cancel. A stun-immune defender requires a different punish.

DECISION 3

Keep conditional Armor Break protection conditional

His roster record has multiple core damage-effect immunities, but protection from Armor Break has a condition. Do not advertise blanket Armor Break immunity in the champion finder; read the actual activation requirement and opponent ability before selection.
