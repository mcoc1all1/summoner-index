EDITORIAL FIELD NOTES · SOURCE-LINKED

What to check in the fight

Editorial decision notes derived from identified game mechanics and attributed source research; these are not in-game performance tests, guarantees, or permissions to reuse third-party text. Checked 2026-11-05; confirm your current champion version, nodes and matchup.

DECISION 1

Map the Judgment sequence before using Specials

Ghost Rider's Judgments arise from different attacks and Specials, so decide which effects the defender needs before consuming the available opportunities. An early irrelevant Judgment may reduce flexibility later in a long fight.

DECISION 2

Save a safe Heavy for a developed Lifesteal payoff

A Heavy Judgment can reward a more complete set of earlier Judgments with stronger sustain. Use a reliable recovery window and confirm healing is permitted, rather than deliberately accepting damage while trying to unlock recovery.

DECISION 3

Use SP3 Damnation as a long-fight reset tool

SP3 can apply Damnation and eventually reset Judgment opportunities, but saving three bars may be impractical against a nearly defeated opponent. Check the fight duration and opponent's damage-effect resistance before investing.
