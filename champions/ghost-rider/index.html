TACTIC 1

Use the reworked Judgement system, not the old combo list

Ghost Rider received an update previewed in May 2026 introducing Hinder, Atrophy and Fear-related interactions. Confirm the currently active Judgements and the live kit; earlier Fate Seal and five-Judgement rotations are not guaranteed to apply unchanged.

TACTIC 2

Choose which passive effect solves this fight

The new kit's conditional effects can help against power, buff or movement problems depending on the defender. Inspect which Judgement was actually applied instead of assuming a single SP1 controls every type of Power Gain or Regeneration.

TACTIC 3

Convert the developed state through specials safely

A special may build or convert the current Judgement state into Degeneration or another payoff. Use a clean recovery window and watch whether the correct effect is passive rather than a purifiable debuff; do not blindly reproduce pre-rework lifesteal loops.

TACTIC 4

Respect the changes to sustain and control

Player testing of the 2026 rework reports a notable shift away from the previous healing and Power Lock behavior. Those are player observations rather than site-verified clears. Confirm current Genesis, Damnation, immunity and target rules before making a survival claim.
