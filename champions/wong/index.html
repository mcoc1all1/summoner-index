TACTIC 1

Identify the current spell state

Wong's Mystic combat tools depend on the active spell and personal resource setup. Verify the current spell icon before treating a combo or Heavy as the start of a fixed damage rotation; build during safe recovery windows rather than chasing the ideal state.

TACTIC 2

Choose the spell for the defender

Different Wong effects support offensive pressure, survivability or control under their own conditions. Inspect the defender's buffs and resistance before choosing the next special, especially where a passive effect cannot be Nullified.

TACTIC 3

Spend the special before the window expires

A developed spell is only useful if its payoff lands safely while the relevant state remains active. If the defender becomes aggressive or reaches a difficult special bar, bait first rather than forcing an unsafe heavy-to-special sequence.

TACTIC 4

Do not claim universal spell immunity

His personal shielding and Mystic utility are conditional, and some nodes can block buffs or alter spell duration. Verify the current kit, awakening and active resource before relying on him against passive damage or an opponent with Neutralize.
