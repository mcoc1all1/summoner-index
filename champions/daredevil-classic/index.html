TACTIC 1

Confirm the relevant Miss counter before attacking

Daredevil (Classic) can offer matchup-specific utility against Miss-style attacks. Check which incoming attack qualifies and whether the current mode or node changes the trigger; avoid importing Hell's Kitchen's combo-resource logic.

TACTIC 2

Create offense through short recovery punishes

Build meter from controlled basic attacks, preserving space to bait enemy specials. His defensive senses and Perfect Block are not a reason to voluntarily accept every Unblockable or special projectile.

TACTIC 3

Use special-granted Precision while it lasts

If a special grants Precision, take the next safe attacks while the buff is active rather than assuming a permanent critical-hit bonus. Check current target critical resistance before extending the window.

TACTIC 4

Differentiate Miss, Evade and node damage

Bypassing Miss does not automatically stop Evade, Auto-Block or passive quest mechanics. Verify Stun immunity and other restrictions; the correct profile is Classic Daredevil, not Daredevil (Hell's Kitchen).
