How to claim MCOC Twitch Drops

Link your Kabam ID, watch an eligible live Marvel Contest of Champions stream on Twitch, claim each earned Drop, then collect it in-game. This independent walkthrough uses official Kabam and Twitch instructions.

Guide reviewed 15 November 2026 · Campaign rewards, watch times and eligible channels can change. Check Twitch for the active campaign.

START HERE

Claim in six steps

  1. 01

    Link your Kabam ID first

    Open Kabam Account Management ↗ and sign in with the Kabam ID used for MCOC. In Connections, select the chain-link icon for Link Your Twitch Account. Sign in to the Twitch username you intend to watch with, then choose Authorize. Confirm Twitch shows as connected to the correct Kabam ID.

    One Twitch account can be linked to only one Kabam ID at a time. Switching connections can redirect future rewards.

  2. 02

    Check the active Drop campaign

    Visit Twitch Drops & Rewards ↗, find MARVEL Contest of Champions, and review that campaign's dates, watch-time milestones and eligible channels. Not every campaign is open to every streamer.

  3. 03

    Watch an eligible LIVE stream

    Open an eligible channel from the MCOC Twitch directory ↗. Check for the Drops Enabled indicator and, for creator-only rewards, verify that the streamer is an eligible Kabam CCP member. YouTube streams, Twitch VODs and ordinary replays do not count.

    Watch-time progress only accrues on one qualifying channel at a time; running multiple streams does not multiply progress.

  4. 04

    Track progress and claim each Drop

    Open Twitch Drops Inventory ↗. Once a milestone is complete, select Claim on that reward. A completed progress bar does not always mean you have claimed it. Claim promptly: Kabam advises claiming within seven days, and Twitch publishes expiry details for each campaign.

  5. 05

    Collect the reward in MCOC

    Open MCOC with the same Kabam ID you linked. Check your in-game inbox, rewards and any selector that needs a final confirmation. Delivery is not always instant. Many Drops use progression-based selectors, so the in-game choice may differ from the highest-tier reward pictured on Twitch.

  6. 06

    Resolve missing rewards

    If you earned and claimed a Drop but nothing arrived, verify the linked accounts, claim status and in-game mailbox first. See the troubleshooting checklist ↓ and contact Kabam's official Drops support ↗ for game-delivery issues.

KNOW THE CAMPAIGN

Which streams count?

OFFICIAL STREAM

Kabam livestream Drops

These are tied to a particular official MCOC broadcast, not every stream in the game directory. Watch Kabam's official Twitch channel ↗ during the specified live event.

CCP EXCLUSIVE

Creator Program Drops

Available only while watching eligible Kabam Content Creator Program channels. Some weeks have an open track and an extra CCP track running together; the official creator directory ↗ can help identify channels.

OPEN CAMPAIGN

Any qualifying MCOC creator

For open tracks, you can earn progress on a qualifying MCOC livestream, even if the creator is not in the CCP. The current campaign listing and a stream's Drops indicator determine eligibility.

Kabam outlined this multi-track system in its Twitch Drops overview ↗ and CCP track clarification ↗. Campaign requirements may change over time; always follow the current Twitch listing.

TROUBLESHOOTING

Not receiving your Twitch Drops?

Progress is stuck at 0%

Confirm you are logged in, the stream is live, the category and campaign match, and the channel is eligible for that reward track. Refresh Twitch or reopen the stream. Twitch handles progress tracking; use Twitch's Drops help ↗ for watch-time issues.

Claimed on Twitch, missing in-game

Check that the Drop says Claimed in Inventory and that the Twitch account is linked to your intended Kabam ID. Reopen MCOC and check in-game mail and reward selectors. For unresolved delivery issues, submit a Kabam Support ticket ↗ within seven days of the relevant livestream.

Reward looks smaller than pictured

Some in-game selector options are gated by story progression. The image on Twitch may show the top available tier, while your account receives an appropriate lower-tier selection. The small number on a Twitch reward icon can also reflect the number of times it was claimed, not how many items it contains.

Wrong Twitch account linked

Sign into Kabam Account Management ↗ and inspect Connections. When linking, confirm the username displayed during Twitch authorization is the account that earned the Drop. Do not repeatedly switch accounts without checking where future rewards will go.

Before you contact Kabam

Keep screenshots of your Twitch account's username, Drops Inventory claim status and Kabam account Connections screen. Submit them only through Kabam's support website, not in a public comment or message. Kabam asks players to file unresolved missing-Drop requests within seven days of the livestream.

QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to subscribe or pay to earn MCOC Twitch Drops?

Ordinary watch-time Drops do not require a subscription. Twitch also supports separate subscription-based campaigns, so check the active campaign's exact requirement before spending money. Do not assume subscribing gives progress toward a watch-time Drop.

Can I watch on YouTube or Twitch VODs?

No. Kabam's Twitch Drops require qualifying LIVE Twitch streams. YouTube watch time, VODs, restreams and clips do not count.

Can I watch two streams at once to earn rewards faster?

No. Twitch counts progress on only one qualifying channel at a time. Switching between eligible channels during a campaign is allowed.

Can one Twitch account send rewards to multiple MCOC accounts?

No. Kabam says a Twitch account links to a single Kabam account at a time. If you use multiple MCOC accounts, each needs a separate Twitch account and its own earned and claimed rewards.

What if my inventory says Ready to Claim, not Claimed?

Open Drops Inventory and press Claim. You must claim each completed reward while it is eligible; a full progress bar alone does not send it to your game.

Does the guide show live MCOC Drops or countdowns?

No. This is a manually reviewed how-to guide, not a live Twitch API tracker. Open Twitch's campaign page for the current milestones, eligible channels and expiry dates.
