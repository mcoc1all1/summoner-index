Act 6 • Progression bosses

Chapter-final fights and selected progression roadblocks. Act 6 has been reworked: follow current in-game node text over older footage. These are preparation guides, not every exploration path.

STORY QUEST · 6.1.5

Crossbones • Act 6 ↗

Prepare for opening Poison, Arc Overload regeneration, Bane and restricted power access. These mechanics are supported by March 2026 footage.

March 2026 commentary supports Fisticuffs, Stun Immunity and a power-reset restriction. Prepare safe openings and avoid saving power through a reset.
