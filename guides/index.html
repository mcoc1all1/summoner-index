How to use Summoner Index guides

1Read the 30-second plan.
2Choose a counter your roster actually has.
3Read the full mechanics only where the fight needs it.

Choose a guide for the problem you need to solve

If you are progressing through story content, begin with the quest directory and open the exact boss encounter. Read the fight objectives and common mistakes before choosing the attacker. Boss mechanics can reward specific actions or punish an otherwise strong champion, so a familiar rotation may need to change. A story boss guide covers the named encounter, not every appearance of that character.

For special challenges, start with team restrictions and the actual objective. Check the permitted roster before adding rank-ups or choosing a strategy. Community completions may rely on particular ranks, synergies and individual fight conditions, which should be compared with your available champions. Use the guides as preparation, then verify the live objective and requirements in the game.

Prepare your team, then review the current event

Mastery choices should depend on what you play and the champions you use. The mastery guides explain general, competitive and specialist approaches rather than treating a single setup as correct for everyone. If an encounter calls for a particular effect, check the champion's current profile and the exact conditions under which the effect is available.

For event decisions, consult Events & News for the calendar and source-linked updates. Use the Counter Finder to compare applicable attacker ideas, the champion database to investigate mechanics, and the mastery guides for preparation. Our methodology explains how editorial recommendations are distinguished from gameplay-tested claims and why some encounters remain under review.
