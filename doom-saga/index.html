THE SHORT VERSION

Your Doom Saga game plan

  1. Check the Weekly Operation.Confirm your current personal milestone, featured Ascension and countdown before clearing content. Weeks reset separately.
  2. Play regular content that awards points.Event Quest, eligible Side Quests, daily Swift Rivalry and supported event objectives can contribute; inspect each live event’s scoring rules.
  3. Join Siege fights.Use four-star attackers for Gatekeepers, claim your milestones and look for Overlord access after the Realm requirement is met.
  4. Plan Doomscrip spending.The Von Doom Armory ties together Victorious Four rewards, Latverian Talismans and the Doctor Doom chase. Read the next unlock before spending.
  5. Check limited-time extras.Alliances, Bounty Missions, the Tower, Recruitment and special weekend events have different dates and requirements.

Kabam clarified that ordinary content can be completed throughout the week; you do not need to rush every task at reset. Some milestone unlocks may affect when an Overlord appears. Read the Reclamation War Community Pulse ↗

HOW RECLAMATION WAR WORKS

Daily, weekly and saga-wide priorities

DAILY / WHEN AVAILABLE

Build points consistently

Look at the active Week’s scoring list. Eligible Event Quest, Side Quest, other Solo/Alliance events, Siege Gatekeepers, and Swift Rivalry can contribute. Swift Rivalry places you in a daily comparison group; results can add Realm points. Avoid assuming unrelated game modes score automatically.

EACH WEEK

Finish the personal milestones

Claim what you qualify for before your Operation resets. Kabam reduced the personal contribution target for later weeks from 450,000 to 350,000 points; verify the exact milestone for the week you are playing. Work toward the Realm goal to unlock the new Overlord encounter and featured Ascension opportunities.

OVER THE WHOLE CAMPAIGN

Keep the long-term rewards in sight

Earn Doomscrip from eligible activities and track your spending in the Von Doom Armory. Weekly rewards and saga-long purchases serve different purposes. The Armory is where the Victorious Four and Doctor Doom chase connects to your overall campaign progress.

Before a weekly reset: Check your personal and Realm milestones, claim unlocked rewards, review any Overlord Location item, and check the expiry of your current Operation. If you are unsure about an expense, confirm the next Armory unlock in-game first.
COMMUNITY BOSSES

Sieges: Gatekeepers vs. Overlords

Gatekeepers

These global-health encounters are available in rotating rarities. You must bring 4-Star Champions. Standard, Uncommon and Rare versions have published windows of approximately 24, 48 and 72 hours. Damage contributes to the community health pool while your own fight milestones grant rewards and Weekly Operation points.

Some uncommon and rare tiers can add Locator Chips or other special rewards. Check the active boss and your personal milestone requirements instead of assuming every health bar pays the same.

Overlords

Overlords unlock after the relevant weekly Realm milestone is reached. Claim the Overlord Location reward if required for the encounter to appear. These bosses have very large shared health pools and evolving nodes. Each attempt spends one Siege Deployment; Kabam states players receive 10 deployments that refresh daily.

Overlord rewards and access differ from ordinary Gatekeepers. Being eligible to see a Siege does not mean an alliance or a particular champion is mandatory.

Where the big rewards fit

Doctor Doom & the Armory

Kabam’s saga overview describes collecting 100 Talismans for a 7-Star Doctor Doom and using Doomscrip in the Von Doom Armory. Ascension-related purchases have additional conditions; check the live Armory description before choosing bundles or Talismans.

Victorious Four

Special Fantastic Four variants form a saga-long acquisition and Ascension track. They are a separate chase from the weekly Operation champion. Some Armory choices can unlock later Doctor Doom Ascension opportunities.

Shuri Ascension

Shuri is a major saga chase through 6 January 2027. Locator Chips appear in eligible content and selected Siege rewards. Track the sources you can actually access rather than assuming all pieces are automatically granted.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Quick answers

Do I need to spend all my Doomscrip every week?

No blanket rule requires spending everything at once. Kabam says players can spend Doomscrip when ready; spending can contribute points toward the active week. One tactical choice is whether you want to use currency immediately to help unlock a weekly milestone or save for a more valuable Armory item. Verify the in-game milestone and purchase rules first.

Do Gatekeeper bosses need an Alliance?

Gatekeeper Sieges are community encounters rather than the same thing as Alliance quests. They use 4-Star attackers. Some related activities, notably Beast’s Bounty Missions and Doom’s Alliance Super Season, have explicit Alliance requirements.

Does the saga end on January 4 or February 10?

Both dates apply to different things. January 4 is the end date published for Reclamation War. February 10 is the broader Empire of Doom Saga listing. An individual quest or shop can close earlier, so check its own timer.

Can I still complete the Tower after November 18?

The published Tower window ends November 18. The game clock is the final authority for availability; do not rely on this guide to extend an expired entry window.

Are all weekly Ascension champions guaranteed?

No. Milestones, rank rewards and individual Ascension tiers have conditions. Kabam’s overview distinguishes the top milestone and ranked placements. Read your week’s event screen before planning around a specific reward.
