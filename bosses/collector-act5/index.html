SOURCED FIGHT GUIDE

Learn the fight loop

Guide reviewed 2026-10-29 · Not gameplay-tested in the current build

A short preparation aid. Read the full in-game boss abilities before entry; this is not an exhaustive mechanics transcript.

Opening — track Bane

Watch the transfer timer and preserve an opening to land a hit. Confirm a parry stun actually sticks: Tenacity can remove it.

Middle — change your defense

Below 55% his specials become Unblockable. Below 40% he resists plans based on Stun, Bleed, Poison or Power Lock through new immunities. Use safe recovery punishes instead of relying on parry combos.

Finish — respect new effects

Below 25%, watch for intermittent Unstoppable before committing. Below 10%, regeneration adds pressure. His ability-accuracy reduction also grows during each attempt, so utility can become unreliable.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming every parry creates a safe opening.

  • Holding block against Unblockable specials.

  • Ignoring Bane while chasing damage.

Team selection and preparation

Favor damage you can access quickly and controls you know. Power control or assisted Evade may help early, but neither is guaranteed throughout the fight. These are preparation notes, not a promised solo.

Attacker profile

These are useful traits, not a claim that one Champion is mandatory.

  • Fast access to damage without depending on long ramp-up.

  • A plan that does not require Parry-Stun to create every opening.

  • Comfort against Unblockable Special Attacks and Bane timing.

Before entering

  • Enter expecting Bane to dictate the pace. Preserve a reliable hit to transfer it instead of extending every combo.

  • Do not assume a Parry-Stun will remain available throughout the fight; verify the Stun before committing.

  • Treat this as the Act 5.2.6 Collector, not the Abyss or other Collector encounters.

When the fight goes wrong

Bane expires while I am waiting for an opening

Shorten the preceding combo and create distance earlier so you have time to land the transfer hit.

My control utility stops working late in the fight

Re-check the health threshold and switch to direct damage plus safe Special Attack punishment instead of forcing the same utility loop.
