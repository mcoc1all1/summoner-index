SOURCED FIGHT GUIDE

Learn the fight loop

Guide reviewed 2026-11-04 · Not gameplay-tested in the current build

A short preparation aid. Read the full in-game boss abilities before entry; this is not an exhaustive mechanics transcript.

Power Loop — match the prompt, not a fixed rotation

When Kang shows a number from zero to three, plan around that many full bars of your own power at countdown expiry. For a nonzero prompt, a matching special that knocks him down can complete it early; zero instead requires holding below one full bar until the timer resolves. A heavy can pause the loop, buying time to adjust your meter. Successful prompts add Power Charges; do not confuse them with path-specific Paradox charges.

Control power with his specials

His SP1 is useful for steering your meter: dodging its hits can give you power, while blocking drains it. Do not dodge every hit automatically when the next prompt asks for zero or one bar. Avoid SP2 contact even through block because it can apply Power Lock and Special Lock, disrupting the next loop.

100–91% — earn three Power Charges

Three successful Power Loop prompts open Time Collapse, the window when the boss's damage reduction falls and your damage matters. Save reliable bursts for that period. On phase transition Kang becomes temporarily Indestructible and later rushes aggressively while Unblockable; stop your rotation and evade instead of forcing damage. Both power meters reset.

90–51% — earn four Power Charges

The next Time Collapse requires four charges. Passive power gain and cheaper specials alter the timing of each number prompt, so check your meter after every special. Once Time Collapse begins, prioritize usable damage before the brief opening ends.

50–2% — earn six Power Charges

This stretch needs six charges while passive power drain makes storing a requested bar harder. Repeating the same combo-ending attack also drains power. Vary your endings and use the heavy pause or SP1 block/dodge choices deliberately rather than reacting to the prompt too late.

Final 2% — launch SP3 before Kang

When he reaches the finish, ordinary hits and lower specials no longer damage him. Both power meters reset, Kang is Unblockable, and staying closer increases your power gain. Manage spacing and build your SP3 before his lethal SP3; this is a separate finishing race, not another Power Loop prompt.

Common mistakes

  • Using the 7.4.6 Superior Kang Slipstream strategy for the different 7.3.6 Tech Kang fight.

  • Using the right special without actually knocking Kang down on a nonzero Power Loop prompt.

  • Trying to solve a zero-bar prompt with a special instead of managing the power meter until expiry.

  • Dodging every SP1 hit even when the current power target is low.

  • Forcing damage into the Indestructible transition or forgetting the power reset.

  • Assuming Time Collapse alone bypasses additional Paradox-linked damage reduction on every path.

Team selection and preparation

The base Power Loop is not the only possible restriction. Some linked routes use Neuro-Kinetic Paradox conditions that can cause little or no damage despite correct Power Charges; review the exact route and its eligible class/buff or Paradox requirements. Historical ability-accuracy reports describe issues, but they are not proof of a current bug or a reason to skip the prompt. No current-client solo test is recorded.

Attacker profile

  • Choose a champion with controllable power and specials that reliably knock down; fast or passive power gain may help or hinder different numbers.

  • Practice using SP1 dodge-versus-block choices to adjust power; avoiding SP2 remains the safer starting approach.

  • For Paradox-linked paths, confirm that your chosen attacker and carried path effects meet that path's separate damage condition before committing to the boss.

Before entering

  • Confirm you selected Kang the Conqueror in story 7.3.6—not Superior Kang from 7.4.6.

  • Read all linked nodes before leaving the path. Some Paradox-linked routes demand specific charges, class combinations or effects before normal Time Collapse damage works.

  • Pick an attacker whose specials can score knockdowns and whose power meter you can steer. Remember that phase transitions reset power.

When the fight goes wrong

I perform the right special but don't receive a charge

Make sure the nonzero prompt matches the special and that its hit actually knocked Kang down. For zero, wait until the timer ends while holding below one bar. Historical reports mention ability-accuracy interactions, but they do not establish a current bug.

I enter Time Collapse but deal zero damage

Separate boss mechanics from the chosen lane. Power Loop success opens Time Collapse, but linked Paradox nodes can still require an appropriate champion class, matching buffs or saved charges from the preceding path. Re-read the specific node and check which champion holds the required effects before spending another revive. Older community reports cannot confirm a current bug.

I keep overshooting the power target

SP1 offers a power-control choice: successful dodges add power while blocked hits drain it. In later phases, passive gain, reduced special costs or drain can change your usual timing.

Kang stops taking damage and turns aggressive

Check whether you triggered a phase transition. He becomes temporarily Indestructible and later rushes Unblockable; back off, avoid the rush and rebuild power after the reset.

The last 2% won't move

The finish requires SP3, not another Time Collapse. Build power by managing distance during his Unblockable phase and launch yours before his lethal SP3.
