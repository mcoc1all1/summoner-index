SOURCED FIGHT GUIDE

Learn the fight loop

Guide reviewed 2026-11-01 · Not gameplay-tested in the current build

A short preparation aid. Read the full in-game boss abilities before entry; this is not an exhaustive mechanics transcript.

Survive — cover Poison

Caustic Temper - 1 gives the defender a 50% chance when struck to inflict a Poison debuff dealing 5% of the attacker’s maximum health as direct damage over 10 seconds. Bring reliable Poison protection and check its activation conditions.

Damage — Fury buffs and passives qualify

The current node panel says Fury effect, not Fury buff. Sinister takes 90% less damage when the attacker has no Fury effect. Both Fury buffs and passive Furies satisfy this wording. A generic Attack increase without a Fury effect does not. Keep Fury active when damage lands.

Dex-Fury — gain Fury from special attacks

Dex-Fury - 1 grants a stacking 40% Fury buff whenever you activate Dexterity for a hit from the defender’s special attacks. Maximum 10 stacks; duration 7 seconds, paused during the defender’s special attacks. Bait a special, dex a hit safely, then attack during the Fury window. Dexing basic attacks does not meet this trigger. An attacker does not need an innate Fury if they can gain and maintain this node’s buff.

Healing and transfer — avoid feeding the defender

Critical hits can restore his health. Do not assume a large yellow hit is lasting progress. Damaging debuffs can be transferred back: the March 2026 run demonstrates trouble with Spiral’s Ruptures and warns against Hyperion’s Incinerates. Poison immunity does not protect you from every transferred damage type.

Common mistakes

  • Excluding passive Fury because an older guide said buffs only.

  • Assuming any Attack increase counts as Fury.

  • Letting Fury expire before your damage rotation.

  • Solving Fury but neglecting Poison protection.

Team selection and preparation

Prioritize Poison protection, safe special evasion and Fury uptime. Dex-Fury broadens options beyond champions with their own Fury; it does not remove Poison, critical-hit healing or debuff-transfer risks.

  • The October 2025 v53.0 announcement confirms reduced signature levels, simpler nodes and tuning for The Champion and Grandmaster. It does not list every individual boss node.

  • Post-October-8-2025 players report removed global nodes. Do not carry a pre-retune quest-wide restriction into this fight automatically: inspect the current quest, path and boss panels separately. These reports do not establish every remaining boss node.

  • Post-retune footage check: 2026-03-14, 0:12–0:21; 7:46–13:55. Post-retune commentary confirms Caustic Temper, Poison, Fury uptime, critical-hit healing and damaging-debuff transfer.

  • Current node screenshot: supplied by the owner and reviewed November 1, 2026. Caustic Temper - 1 requires a Fury effect, so Fury buffs and passive Furies qualify. Dex-Fury - 1 supplies Fury buffs from Dexterity activations against special hits. This resolves the earlier buff-versus-passive uncertainty.

  • Genetic Perfection: Sinister begins with 3 random passive effects from his Signature Ability. Check his starting effects on each attempt.

When the fight goes wrong

A Fury effect does not restore damage

Both Fury buffs and passive Furies qualify. Check that the effect is actually Fury and is active when damage lands. A generic Attack increase is not enough. Distinguish reduced damage from Sinister healing after critical hits.

My Poison-immune attacker still loses health

Check transferred damaging debuffs and incoming hit or block damage. Match protection to the actual damage type; Poison immunity is only one part of this fight.
