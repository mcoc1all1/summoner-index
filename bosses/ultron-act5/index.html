SOURCED FIGHT GUIDE

Learn the fight loop

Guide reviewed 2026-10-29 · Not gameplay-tested in the current build

A short preparation aid. Read the full in-game boss abilities before entry; this is not an exhaustive mechanics transcript.

Choose an opening your attacker can control

List the debuffs your rotation applies, including Parry Stun. If an opening would apply one, check Ultron's dormant icon first: it determines the buff that application activates.

Decide whether the resulting buff is manageable

Power Gain can accelerate his specials; Regeneration can undo your damage. Delay the debuff unless you can handle that result. Armor Up and Fury present different tradeoffs, so keep reading the changing icon.

Leave yourself an exit after each hit

An Evade can interrupt your intended combo. Use short commitments and disengage when it happens; reassess the dormant effect before starting another debuff-based opening.

Common mistakes

  • Parrying automatically without reading the icon.

  • Forgetting a special attack also applies a debuff.

  • Continuing a combo after an Evade.

Team selection and preparation

Choose a controllable rotation. Buff-control or Metal-specific strategies require their own setup and should not be treated as automatic wins. Practice an opening that does not depend on parry.

Attacker profile

These are useful traits, not a claim that one Champion is mandatory.

  • A controllable rotation that can delay Debuffs when the dormant effect is dangerous.

  • Comfort fighting without automatic Parry openings.

  • Buff control can help, but it is not treated as an automatic win condition.

Before entering

  • Before the first Parry, identify the dormant icon. A Debuff can activate the corresponding effect.

  • Remember that Stun, Special Attack Debuffs and Basic Attack Debuffs all count as Debuff applications.

  • Use measured combos so an Evade does not turn a full combo into an unnecessary hit.

When the fight goes wrong

Ultron keeps healing after my Parry

You likely triggered dormant Regeneration with the Stun Debuff. Wait for a safer dormant effect or use a non-Debuff opening.

His Power spikes unexpectedly

Check whether dormant Power Gain was active when your Debuff landed. Change the timing rather than repeating the same opening.
