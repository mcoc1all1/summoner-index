SOURCED FIGHT GUIDE

Learn the fight loop

Guide reviewed 2026-10-29 · Not gameplay-tested in the current build

A short preparation aid. Read the full in-game boss abilities before entry; this is not an exhaustive mechanics transcript.

Opening — distinguish a ban from a stop signal

Track which actions have been banned and read SP1's symbols. Red light overrides your rotation: release Block and stop every input until the signal ends.

Middle — decide whether the instruction counts

Check for Grandmaster says before acting. That prefix requires compliance; its absence requires failure. Use openings that do not depend on Stun.

Late — reassess your control plan

Expect continuous inversion and rising power pressure. Give him space away from his corner. Reverse-control immunity carries a Degeneration penalty in this phase.

Finish — separate progress from input direction

Track intercept, SP1, SP2 and SP3 completion separately. Blocking preserves the current control direction; other actions toggle it. After a knockout, inspect which protections remain before repeating completed work.

Common mistakes

  • Moving or blocking during red light.

  • Obeying every prompt without reading its prefix.

  • Assuming one control direction remains stable in the finale.

Team selection and preparation

Plan separately for the late phase and finale: reversed-control immunity can help in the finale despite its earlier penalty. Save Striker use for Wounded damage windows. This covers the boss, not the Necropolis paths.

Attacker profile

These are useful traits, not a claim that one Champion is mandatory.

  • A strong general damage dealer for repeated Wounded windows.

  • A reverse-control-immune Champion is especially valuable for the final phase, even if reverse-control immunity is not always beneficial earlier.

  • A usable Striker helps extend Wounded damage windows.

Before entering

  • Practice stopping every input during Red Light. Blocking still counts as an input for this mechanic.

  • Read the entire prompt before acting; Phase 2 depends on whether 'Grandmaster Says' is present.

  • Keep a reverse-control plan for the final phase. A dedicated reverse-control-immune Champion can save revives there.

  • Use the Striker during Wounded when safe so the damage window lasts longer.

When the fight goes wrong

I gain failure tokens during Red Light even though I did not attack

Do not move, dash or hold Block. The safe response is no input until Red Light ends.

Phase 2 punishes me for doing the displayed action

Check the wording. Only obey the action when the prompt includes 'Grandmaster Says'; otherwise the intended response is to fail it.

The final phase consumes many revives

Use a reverse-control plan and track each remaining objective separately: intercept, SP1, SP2 and SP3.

What this guide covers

Nameless Grandmaster at the end of Necropolis. Path fights and full Necropolis team planning are outside this guide.
