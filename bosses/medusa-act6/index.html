A short preparation aid. Read the full in-game boss abilities before entry; this is not an exhaustive mechanics transcript.

Damage — keep Bleed active

The March 2026 panel shows Bloody Mess. Bleed damage is increased by 25%; all damage is reduced by 90% unless she has a Bleed debuff or passive. Applying Bleed therefore removes this damage reduction for other damage sources too. The old bleed-only Do You Bleed? instructions do not describe this panel.

Openings — respect Auto-Block

Maintain Bleed through safe heavy or special openings. Watch her Fury state and do not force long basic combos through Auto-Block.

Blocking — check Explosive Personality

Explosive Personality is visible on the post-retune panel. Avoid repeatedly hitting her block and building its Armor Up stacks; bait a safe opening instead. Confirm the displayed stack threshold before using block-heavy rotations.

Common mistakes

  • Following the old claim that only Bleed itself can damage her.

  • Letting Bleed expire before a major damage rotation.

  • Repeatedly hitting her block despite Explosive Personality or Auto-Block.

Team selection and preparation

Prioritize reliable Bleed uptime and safe openings. Bleed debuffs and passives both satisfy the displayed gate; a champion with sporadic Bleeds may leave your main damage rotation heavily reduced.

  • The October 2025 v53.0 announcement confirms reduced signature levels, simpler nodes and tuning for The Champion and Grandmaster. It does not list every individual boss node.

  • Post-retune footage check: 2026-03-17, 1:10; 16:01–17:20. Node panel visually inspected: Bloody Mess increases Bleed damage by 25% and reduces all damage by 90% unless the defender has a Bleed debuff or passive. Explosive Personality is also visible.

When the fight goes wrong

My damage suddenly drops

Check whether the last Bleed expired. Bloody Mess restores its 90% reduction when no Bleed debuff or passive is present. Reapply Bleed before spending your main damage special.
