SOURCED FIGHT GUIDE

Learn the fight loop

Guide reviewed 2026-10-30 · Not gameplay-tested in the current build

A short preparation aid. Read the full in-game boss abilities before entry; this is not an exhaustive mechanics transcript.

Armor loop

Knock Cerastes down to damage Leg Armor; hit his Block or bypass/break Auto-Block to damage Shield Armor; connect the final hit of your Striker to damage Spirit Armor. Damaging one armor type locks it until another armor type is damaged. Breaking all three creates the boss-generated Stun and your main damage window.

Opening • 100–91%

Armor repairs over time. If damaged armor fully repairs in this phase, it becomes Overcharged. Keep rotating the three armor interactions. During an Overdrive transition, land a Light or Medium intercept to remove Overdrive.

Middle • 90–51%

Armor begins with more durability. Hitting a locked armor type repairs it, so read the armor icons before repeating a knockdown, Block hit or Striker.

Late • 50–6%

Armor durability varies, and hitting locked armor can Overcharge it. External Stun no longer supplies the opening; use spacing and the Stun created by breaking all three armor types. Older footage that relied on Parry-Stun in this phase is not a dependable tactic.

Finish • 5–0%

At 5%, the armors Overcharge and ordinary attacks cannot damage Cerastes. Survive while his Health drains toward 1%. At 1%, strike Cerastes' Block with a Light Attack to trigger the encounter finish.

Common mistakes

  • Repeating an armor action while its icon is locked.

  • Using a Striker but missing its final hit.

  • Pushing him to Special 3, which causes an unavoidable knockout.

Team selection and preparation

Bring a usable bound Striker and an attacker whose intercepts and special spacing you know. Unique buffs help armor damage. Mutant attackers and Infuriate speed up armor repair. Do not rely on Immortality or Indestructible to survive this boss.

Attacker profile

These are useful traits, not a claim that one Champion is mandatory.

  • A bound Striker is effectively part of the core armor rotation because Spirit Armor is damaged by the Striker's final hit.

  • Comfortable intercepts help remove Overdrive and reduce failed armor rotations.

  • Champions that maintain several Buffs can improve armor damage, but execution of the three armor interactions remains mandatory.

Before entering

  • Bind a usable Striker before entering.

  • Learn which icon corresponds to each armor type and do not repeat an interaction while that armor is locked.

  • Plan to dodge Special Attacks rather than allowing Special Attack 3; Cerastes' Special Attack 3 causes an unavoidable knockout.

When the fight goes wrong

One armor type keeps repairing

You are probably repeating its interaction while it is locked. Damage a different armor type before returning to it.

Spirit Armor does not move

Make sure the final hit of the Striker actually connects.

Cerastes is stuck at 1%

Do not block and wait for his Light Attack. Land your own Light Attack into Cerastes' Block to trigger the finish condition.
