SOURCED FIGHT GUIDE

Learn the fight loop

Guide reviewed 2026-11-04 · Not gameplay-tested in the current build

A short preparation aid. Read the full in-game boss abilities before entry; this is not an exhaustive mechanics transcript.

Setup — wait for an armed trap

First identify which special produces the trap in the current phase. Give it time to arm, then position Scytalis for a knockdown over it. If she is blocking or the trap is still inactive, pushing her across the marker will not open the fight.

Opening • 100–81%

Look for SP1. Once its trap is armed, move Scytalis across it with a well-spaced heavy or special knockdown. Use the resulting armor-down interval for damage; when she changes phase, expect protection to return.

Middle • 80–41%

The trap now comes from SP2. Contact from ordinary combo hits is no longer enough to spring it on Scytalis: line up an actual knockdown. If the trap disappears before you connect, reset rather than forcing an attack into Corrupted Armor.

Late • 40–6%

Return to baiting SP1, but keep the middle-phase habit of using a knockdown to connect with the armed trap. This is still a temporary armor-down opening; do not confuse it with the different finish mechanic.

Finish • 5–0%

Change the plan at 5%. Bait SP2, allow the resulting trap to arm for 7.5 seconds, and avoid relying on heavies, which Scytalis evades even when stunned. A trap can add Prowess, but will not remove Corrupted Armor. Instead, spend those Prowess effects through the hits of your special attacks to reduce the protection.

Common mistakes

  • Trying to move Scytalis before the current trap has armed.

  • Continuing to bait SP1 after the fight has entered a phase that needs SP2.

  • Expecting ordinary combo contact to trigger a trap after the opening phase.

  • Throwing repeated heavies into the final 5% Evade mechanic.

  • Waiting for the armor to drop at the finish instead of using Prowess-backed special hits.

Attacker profile

These are useful traits, not a claim that one Champion is mandatory.

  • A champion comfortable with special-attack timing and repositioning can execute the trap loop without relying on one specific matchup recommendation.

  • Access to Prowess helps in the finish, when special hits rather than an armor-down interval determine progress.

  • Reliable special damage and controlled spacing are more important than forcing repeated basic attacks into Corrupted Armor.

Before entering

  • Before each bait, check both the current health phase and which special is required; the sequence is SP1, SP2, SP1, SP2.

  • Observe the trap's armed state before pushing Scytalis through it. Avoid stepping over the marker yourself.

  • Reserve a safe method of landing specials for the final 5%, when Prowess becomes more important than opening a normal armor-down window.

When the fight goes wrong

She crosses the trap but it does nothing

Check three independent things: whether the phase requires an SP1 or SP2 trap, whether that trap has armed, and whether the contact was a genuine knockdown. Walking or hitting her across a marker may not be enough in later phases. At 5% health or lower, the objective switches to gaining Prowess and using special hits rather than removing Corrupted Armor with a trap.

My attacks barely damage her again

Look at her current phase and armor status. The trap window is temporary, and protection resets at transitions; build the next valid opening rather than spending specials blindly.

The trap or damage window changes near 5%

At the final threshold, switch from opening her armor to generating Prowess and spending it through special hits. Repeated heavies are unreliable because she Evades them.

The displayed trap behavior seems inconsistent

Check the on-screen phase and in-game ability text. Community posts describe apparent bugs, but this source-based guide cannot confirm whether your current game build has the same issue.
