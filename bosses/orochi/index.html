SOURCED FIGHT GUIDE

Learn the fight loop

Guide reviewed 2026-10-29 · Not gameplay-tested in the current build

A short preparation aid. Read the full in-game boss abilities before entry; this is not an exhaustive mechanics transcript.

Core loop — trigger Backfire

Ten damaging effects trigger Violent Purge. Before its timer expires, interrupt her heavy with a special. Backfire stops healing during that special and turns removed or prevented damaging effects into burst damage on its final hit.

1 · Patience

Learn the heavy-interrupt loop. Ordinary damage largely heals back; debuff stacking creates the intended opening.

2 · Guile

Specials give her Inexorable, preventing normal pushback. A landed heavy removes it. Charging a heavy also builds your Striker gauge.

3 · Strength

Inexorable now persists longer after specials. Leave room to retreat instead of assuming your next basic hit will push her away.

4 · Malice

Her specials become Unblockable. Dodge their hits; blocking is no longer the fallback.

5 · Violence

Fury increases the danger of mistakes. Continue the same controlled heavy interruption rather than forcing a damage race.

6 · Rage

Healing and Violent Purge stop. She repeatedly uses SP1 without spending power. Attacking within reach during or just after her special drains her power; exploit safe timings and finish this final bar.

Common mistakes

  • Expecting a parry stun: Orochi is Stun Immune.

  • Letting Violent Purge expire and convert your stacked effects into Degeneration on you.

  • Treating the first health reset as victory.

Team selection and preparation

Bring reliable non-Bleed damaging effects and a special suited to heavy interruption. Intercepts, Striker hits and attempted Heal Block also generate her self-Poison. Ordinary blocking can expose you to Bleed and Poison; protection helps, but does not replace learning the openings.

Attacker profile

These are useful traits, not a claim that one Champion is mandatory.

  • Can apply many damaging effects quickly; the fight loop is built around reaching ten damaging effects.

  • Has a Special Attack that can reliably interrupt Orochi's Heavy Attack during Violent Purge.

  • Does not need Heal Block as the primary answer, because the encounter is designed around Backfire rather than ordinary healing denial.

Before entering

  • Choose damaging effects you can stack consistently instead of relying on one slow Debuff.

  • Reserve enough Power to interrupt the Heavy during Violent Purge.

  • Enter expecting Stun Immunity; Parry may still be useful defensively, but it is not your Stun opening.

When the fight goes wrong

Her Health immediately returns after my combo

Ordinary damage is not the main finish route. Build damaging effects, trigger Violent Purge and create Backfire with the required Special Attack interrupt.

My own damaging effects turn against me

You allowed Violent Purge to expire. Preserve Power so you can interrupt the Heavy before the timer ends.
