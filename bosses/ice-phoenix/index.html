SOURCED FIGHT GUIDE

Learn the fight loop

Guide reviewed 2026-11-12 · Not gameplay-tested in the current build

A short preparation aid. Read the full in-game boss abilities before entry; this is not an exhaustive mechanics transcript.

Opening — cover Coldsnap

Before entering, verify that your attacker can withstand or prevent the Coldsnap applied as the fight starts. If you cannot survive long enough to establish the first safe opening, change that pre-fight protection plan rather than relying on a special attack to rescue the attempt. Confirm the encounter's current effects and your chosen champion's actual Coldsnap protection.

Repeat — reserve a special

Be inside your special animation when Encroaching Stun expires. Power Axis 1 favors building the first bar, so avoid a plan that requires repeatedly saving for SP3.

Punish — inspect Ice Armor

Active Ice Armor consumes an incoming stun instead of leaving her stunned. Confirm the stun landed before committing. Armor can also limit a large single hit; it reforms after removal.

Common mistakes

  • Entering without an answer to starting Coldsnap.

  • Confusing the Ice Armor timer with your Encroaching Stun timer.

  • Treating every parry as a guaranteed attack opening.

Team selection and preparation

Choose Coldsnap protection plus a special animation you can time reliably. Bleed, Poison and Incinerate damage are poor foundations against her immunities. Keep her specials under control rather than chasing one large hit.

Attacker profile

These are useful traits, not a claim that one Champion is mandatory.

  • Coldsnap Immunity or dependable Coldsnap protection for the opening.

  • A Special Attack rotation that can be timed around Encroaching Stun.

  • Comfort confirming whether Ice Armor consumed the Stun before committing.

Before entering

  • Bring a real answer to starting Coldsnap; do not infer protection from Champion theme or class.

  • Choose a Special Attack you can reach and time reliably for Encroaching Stun.

  • Treat Ice Armor as a separate timer from Encroaching Stun.

When the fight goes wrong

Parry did not create an opening

Check Ice Armor. If it consumed the Stun, disengage instead of assuming the defender is still Stunned.

Encroaching Stun catches me with no Power

Reduce unnecessary Special Attack spending and use the Power Axis to plan the next required Special.
