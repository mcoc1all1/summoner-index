SOURCED FIGHT GUIDE

Learn the fight loop

Guide reviewed 2026-11-01 · Not gameplay-tested in the current build

A short preparation aid. Read the full in-game boss abilities before entry; this is not an exhaustive mechanics transcript.

SP1 — read each symbol

Dodge the opening Unblockable beam. Then follow the three displayed actions in order: block, dodge or take the projectile. Do not automatically dodge everything.

Opening — track banned actions

Parry, Dexterity and light attacks can become banned. Stop repeating an action once forbidden; after all three bans, prepare for SP1 and the following damage window.

Middle — complete the challenge cycle

Read each prompt and perform its required action, including intercepting, gaining a buff or applying a damage effect. Burst during Wounded, not while trying to improvise a prompt.

Late — prepare for reversed controls

Follow the new movement, block and knockdown prompts. Watch the inversion indicator; use verified reversed-control immunity or practice the input change.

Final 1% — survive, then SP3

Survive the Infuriated, Unstoppable countdown. Once he becomes permanently Wounded and grants your power, use SP3 to finish.

Common mistakes

  • Ignoring the projectile symbols after dodging the first beam.

  • Assuming parry remains available in later phases.

  • Attacking through the final countdown instead of preserving health.

Before entering

  • The October 2025 v53.0 announcement confirms reduced signature levels, simpler nodes and tuning for The Champion and Grandmaster. It does not list every individual boss node.

  • An October 9, 2025 player report describes a ten-Blunder allowance after the retune. This is community evidence, not an official per-phase table; use the live counter rather than older three- or five-Blunder instructions.

  • Post-October-8-2025 players report removed global nodes. Do not carry a pre-retune quest-wide restriction into this fight automatically: inspect the current quest, path and boss panels separately. These reports do not establish every remaining boss node.

  • Post-retune footage check: 2026-03-26, 6:49–25:41. Post-retune commentary supports action bans, the three SP1 responses, challenges, Wounded windows and later reversed controls.
