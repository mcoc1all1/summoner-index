SOURCED FIGHT GUIDE

Learn the fight loop

Guide reviewed 2026-11-01 · Not gameplay-tested in the current build

A short preparation aid. Read the full in-game boss abilities before entry; this is not an exhaustive mechanics transcript.

Core loop — build and preserve Vigor

Interrupt Basic Attacks, punish Heavy Attacks, and punish Special Attacks to gain personal Vigor Passives. Hitting the Chronoserpent or his Block refreshes Vigor. The fight is tuned around using Vigor for damage rather than relying on ordinary output.

Phase 1 • 20–17 bars

Track Major and Minor Mode. He changes Mode whenever a Health Bar is depleted. Major Mode raises Combat Power Rate; Minor Mode makes him more aggressive and increases Block Penetration.

Phase 2 • 16–13 bars

Special Attack timing begins changing with Mode. Learn both tempos instead of using one fixed Dexterity rhythm.

Phase 3 • 12–9 bars

Blocking Special Attack hits can unpause your Vigor. Major Mode changes his reaction during later hits of your Basic Attack combo, while Minor Mode can remove your ability to Block or trigger Reversal against Combo-Opening Attacks.

Phase 4 • 8–5 bars

Your Special Attacks can change his Mode. Special Attack 1 can apply Corrosion, and Special Attack 2 can apply Wound of Defiance through Block. Keep enough space to dodge rather than treating Block as the safe answer.

Phase 5 • 4–1 bars

Interrupting his Medium or Heavy Attacks changes Mode, and his Special Attacks move faster. Re-check the current Mode before every Special Attack 2 directional input.

Phase 6 • final bar below 50%

Time flow randomly changes after the first hit of an attack and determines Major or Minor Mode. To knock him out, land a Special Attack while you have more than one personal Vigor.

Common mistakes

  • Spending every Vigor window on Basic Attacks instead of preserving a strong Special Attack damage window.

  • Using the same Special Attack dodge timing after Phase 2 changes the tempo.

  • Choosing the wrong swipe direction during Special Attack 2: Major Mode calls for swipe up after the third claw hit, while Minor Mode calls for swipe down.

  • Trying to finish the final Health Bar without more than one personal Vigor.

Team selection and preparation

Do not build a plan around Stun, Sleep or Power-gain prevention; the Chronoserpent is immune to those control effects. Prefer Champions whose damage or sustain scales well during Vigor windows and whose rotation leaves room to punish Special Attacks.

Attacker profile

These are useful traits, not a claim that one Champion is mandatory.

  • A rotation that can punish Special Attacks consistently, because Special Attack punishes build multiple Vigor stacks.

  • Strong Special Attack damage or damage-over-time that can exploit Vigor-enhanced windows.

  • Sustain is valuable because the encounter spans 20 Health Bars and later phases add Corrosion, Wound of Defiance and faster Special Attacks.

  • Do not require Stun, Sleep or Power Lock as the core control plan.

Before entering

  • Practice Special Attack 2 with both directional inputs before committing resources: swipe up after the third claw hit in Major Mode, swipe down in Minor Mode.

  • Treat every four depleted Health Bars as a new mechanics checkpoint. A new Phase also creates a short period where he cannot take damage and you cannot gain Power.

  • Punishing Special Attacks is the fastest route to multiple Vigor stacks; Heavy punishes and intercepts are useful when the Special Attack pattern is unsafe.

When the fight goes wrong

My damage feels extremely low

Rebuild Vigor. The encounter heavily rewards Vigor windows, while the Chronoserpent also suppresses most Attribute changes from their starting values.

Special Attack 2 keeps clipping me

Confirm the current Mode before the third claw hit. Use the Mode-specific vertical swipe; the wrong direction makes the final beam unavoidable.

The last hit will not knock him out

The final phase requires a Special Attack while more than one personal Vigor is active. A normal Basic Attack finish is not sufficient.
