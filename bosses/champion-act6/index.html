SOURCED FIGHT GUIDE

Learn the fight loop

Guide reviewed 2026-11-01 · Not gameplay-tested in the current build

A short preparation aid. Read the full in-game boss abilities before entry; this is not an exhaustive mechanics transcript.

Historical 100–71% — establish control

Watch Fury and Unstoppable before committing. Nullify can remove Fury, but ordinary Heal Block, Petrify and Fate Seal are not dependable healing answers against his immunity rules.

Historical 70–31% — watch Unblockable charges

Check the Unblockable indicator before blocking. Do not trade hits while he is Unstoppable: being struck can also stun you.

Historical 30–11% — check Purify

His Purify can remove damaging and Stun debuffs. Confirm an opening visually before continuing a combo; do not depend on permanent parry control.

Final phase — check the current charge counter

Earlier official revisions reduced the final-phase Indestructible charges to three and made removal progress persist across attempts. Confirm the current counter and wording before using that older sequence. Trigger Dexterity, or an explicitly eligible replacement, against special hits and confirm a charge disappears; respect any displayed removal cooldown. Under the historical finishing rule, use specials once charges are cleared. Post-retune players report Root here: if active, time the dodge against the incoming hit rather than relying on movement distance.

Common mistakes

  • Following the original five-dodge instructions.

  • Dodging from too far away to trigger the required Dexterity event.

  • Trying to finish with basic attacks after removing the charges.

Before entering

  • The October 2025 v53.0 announcement confirms reduced signature levels, simpler nodes and tuning for The Champion and Grandmaster. It does not list every individual boss node.

  • The three-charge finish and removal of No Retreat belong to the earlier v28.0 update. Persistent final-phase progress and phase healing caps were documented in June 2022. The v53.0 announcement confirms further tuning but does not enumerate the complete revised kit.

  • Post-October-8-2025 players report removed global nodes. Do not carry a pre-retune quest-wide restriction into this fight automatically: inspect the current quest, path and boss panels separately. These reports do not establish every remaining boss node.

  • Post-retune footage check: 2026-03-16, 11:04–15:43. Post-retune commentary supports Unstoppable, regeneration, final Indestructible removal and a special-attack finish.

When the fight goes wrong

The retuned finish applies Root

An October 11, 2025 player report describes Root helping the final Dexterity sequence. If it appears in your encounter, time the dodge against the incoming special hit rather than expecting the dash to create distance. This report is not a verified full Root rule.
