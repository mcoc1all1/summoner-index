SOURCED FIGHT GUIDE

Learn the fight loop

Guide reviewed 2026-10-30 · Not gameplay-tested in the current build

A short preparation aid. Read the full in-game boss abilities before entry; this is not an exhaustive mechanics transcript.

Core loop — consume Amplify

With Amplify active, attempt hits during his special animation to consume it and remove Will. Landing your own special’s final hit also reduces Will. Watch the counters rather than judging progress by ordinary damage.

First stage — learn SP1 timing

His specials generate Amplify. Learn safe attack timings during SP1. The Aura makes his attacks Unblockable and prevents most knockdowns; do not treat him like ordinary Doctor Doom.

Second stage — create charges

Knockdowns now generate Amplify. Wait for the Aura to end for normal knockdowns, then use the new charges against his special animation.

Third stage — manage SP2

SP2 generates Amplify through Countdown Charges, but exhausting the countdown gives him SP3. Track both counters and avoid spending every special cycle on SP2.

Final 1% — empty Will, then heavy

Amplify accumulates over time. Clear the remaining Will and land a heavy to finish. His basic hits are now especially lethal; a damage race is not the objective. Kabam fixed the phase-four bug that limited Amplify to two stacks in v59.0; do not treat that old bug as the intended cap.

Common mistakes

  • Trying to parry-stun a Stun Immune boss.

  • Interrupting his heavy: evade it, then punish its recovery.

  • Ignoring the path-specific modifier or the SP2 countdown.

Team selection and preparation

Choose an attacker with controllable special timing and reliable hit placement. His heavy and SP3 are lethal, while most power-control tools do not work. Higher rarity affects Will removal; inspect the encounter’s wording before comparing attackers.

Attacker profile

These are useful traits, not a claim that one Champion is mandatory.

  • Can create and consume Amplify opportunities without relying on Stun.

  • Comfort baiting and fully dodging Heavy and Special Attacks.

  • Can preserve a Heavy Attack for the final 1% requirement.

Before entering

  • Enter expecting Stun Immunity; build your openings around spacing, Heavy recovery and Special Attack recovery.

  • Treat Will as the main progress meter. If Will is not falling, changing Champions will not fix an incorrect mechanic loop.

  • Read the path modifier before attempting to copy a rotation from another route.

When the fight goes wrong

I am dealing damage but the fight is not progressing

Check Will and your Amplify interactions. The intended loop is to reduce Will, not simply race the Health bar.

I get punished when I try to interrupt his Heavy

Do not intercept the Heavy wind-up. Evade it and punish the recovery as the guide calls for.

He remains alive at 1%

Confirm Will is empty, then use the required Heavy Attack finish.
