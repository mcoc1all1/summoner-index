SOURCED FIGHT GUIDE

Learn the fight loop

Guide reviewed 2026-11-01 · Not gameplay-tested in the current build

A short preparation aid. Read the full in-game boss abilities before entry; this is not an exhaustive mechanics transcript.

Opening — protect against Poison

The March 2026 run still applies Poison at the start. Bring dependable Poison protection; Bleed immunity alone is not enough. Do not use the launch-era Biohazard plan.

Healing — answer Arc Overload

The inspected post-retune panel shows Arc Overload - 1, not the old Vigor-based preparation. Plan for recurring Armor Up and regeneration buffs. Check that your healing-control or buff-control plan can function under his other restrictions.

Bane and power — time your openings

Bane - 1, Force of Will and Power Struggle are visible in the March 2026 panel. Plan the Bane transfer around a safe hit and watch its timer; do not back away through the transfer blindly. Do not assume normal power access or rely on ability-accuracy reduction against Force of Will.

Common mistakes

  • Using Bleed immunity as an answer to Poison.

  • Preparing survival but no answer to regeneration.

  • Following an old node list without checking the encounter.

Team selection and preparation

Cover Poison, recurring regeneration, Bane transfers and power access together. A champion who solves only one of these is not automatically a complete counter.

Before entering

  • The October 2025 v53.0 announcement confirms reduced signature levels, simpler nodes and tuning for The Champion and Grandmaster. It does not list every individual boss node.

  • Post-October-8-2025 players report removed global nodes. Do not carry a pre-retune quest-wide restriction into this fight automatically: inspect the current quest, path and boss panels separately. These reports do not establish every remaining boss node.

  • Post-retune footage check: 2026-03-11, 3:53–4:04. Node panel visually inspected: Arc Overload - 1, Stun, Bane - 1, Force of Will and Power Struggle. Opening Poison is described and visible in the encounter.

When the fight goes wrong

If your attacker survives but no health progress sticks

Check regeneration and power restrictions together before choosing a different rotation. A Poison counter alone may not cover the active fight.
