SOURCED FIGHT GUIDE

Learn the fight loop

Guide reviewed 2026-10-29 · Not gameplay-tested in the current build

A short preparation aid. Read the full in-game boss abilities before entry; this is not an exhaustive mechanics transcript.

Prepare — cover incoming effects

Poison and Incinerate protection make this mash-up safer. Backblast can punish large or repeated guaranteed critical hits; do not choose solely by critical damage.

Specials — anticipate inversion

Special Chaos Inversion reverses dash and dodge inputs during specials. Practice that response or verify a suitable immunity before entry.

Openings — respect Spider-Sense

Spider-Sense supplies Evade and grows as she is hit. She can remove a True Strike buff when struck, so that buff alone is not a dependable answer. Use confirmed openings and check the actual Evade-control interaction.

Reset — manage buffs and spacing

Her critical hits can Nullify your buffs; critical hits from either fighter can trigger chaos effects. Watch new icons before continuing a rotation. Step back to let proximity-based Staggers fall away.

Common mistakes

  • Using normal dodge inputs during inversion.

  • Assuming True Strike buffs permanently solve Evade.

  • Relying on buffs that her Nullify or Stagger can remove.

Team selection and preparation

Prioritize controlled openings and protection from Poison and Incinerate. A short safe punish is preferable to forcing a full combo through Evade. This guide covers the original story encounter, not Crucible modifiers.

Attacker profile

These are useful traits, not a claim that one Champion is mandatory.

  • Comfort with reversed dash and Dexterity inputs during Special Attacks.

  • Evade control that does not depend only on a removable True Strike Buff.

  • Poison and Incinerate protection improves safety against the mixed kit.

Before entering

  • Practice the reversed-control response before attempting to maximize damage.

  • Use confirmed openings rather than forcing long combos through Spider-Sense Evade.

  • Do not assume a True Strike Buff permanently solves the fight if the encounter can remove that Buff.

When the fight goes wrong

My Evade counter suddenly stops working

Check whether it was a True Strike Buff that got removed. Re-establish the effect or switch to a different controlled opening.

I keep dashing the wrong way during Specials

Treat Special Chaos Inversion as the priority mechanic and deliberately slow the fight until the reversed input becomes consistent.
