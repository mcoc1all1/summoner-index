Guide reviewed 2026-11-01 · Not gameplay-tested in the current build

A short preparation aid. Read the full in-game boss abilities before entry; this is not an exhaustive mechanics transcript.

Damage — check the critical-hit gate

The post-retune run still identifies critical hits as the damage requirement. A damage-over-time rotation alone is not the intended answer; check that your chosen attacks can actually crit.

Timing — respect the damage shield

If Destructive Feedback remains, save your damage rotation for the shield cooldown. The historical node stores damage while shielded; the danger is the defender hitting you OR your block, which returns stored damage to you. Blocking is therefore not a safe substitute for avoiding the hit. Historical reports describe the critical-hit gate also preventing the stored non-critical damage from hurting this boss on expiry; do not rely on that delayed payout.

Power — plan beyond SP2

The post-retune run confirms a sequence progressing from SP1 to SP2 to SP3. Plan power control or survival before reaching the last step; repeatedly waiting for another SP1 will not solve it.

Common mistakes

  • Selecting a non-critical damage specialist for an active critical-hit gate.

  • Ignoring the feedback shield timer.

  • Entering without checking how to handle the boss reaching SP3.
