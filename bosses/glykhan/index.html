SOURCED FIGHT GUIDE

Learn the fight loop

Guide reviewed 2026-10-29 · Not gameplay-tested in the current build

A short preparation aid. Read the full in-game boss abilities before entry; this is not an exhaustive mechanics transcript.

Damage entry

Dodge his heavy or special and immediately punish its recovery. Only hits chained from that opening damage him. A special-inflicted Stun or Striker Opening can extend the chain. Normal parry-and-combo play does not establish a damage window.

For Justice: first health bar

Punishments track Stuns, first light hits into block, consecutive intercepts, first Striker hits and first special hits. Each type caps at three. Repeating a capped trigger gives Counter-Attack Charge: stop basic attacks and connect the first hit of a special or Striker to remove it. Punishing a completely avoided special removes one random Punishment.

For Vengeance: second health bar

After the first knockout, his health refills. At two bars he can choose either special, then rapidly recover power. Special 1 grants Unstoppable; Special 2 grants Unblockable. Avoiding their hits shortens these effects. Read the actual animation rather than assuming Special 2 from his power meter.

For Ouroboros: third health bar

All Punishments start capped; their triggers now remove stacks while granting Prowess. Once all are cleared, they return after the next damaging chain. Continue dodging and punishing specials. Near-range heavies and specials also apply Never Back Down: watch that phase-specific prompt.

Final survival window

A lethal hit triggers his once-per-fight 16-second Indestructible. Disengage and evade until it ends: any hit he lands during this window instantly knocks you out. Then finish through a valid punish opening.

Common mistakes

  • Attacking normally into Counter-Attack Charge.

  • Blocking special hits: Wound damage can trigger through block.

  • Treating each refill as a percentage checkpoint instead of a new health bar.

Team selection and preparation

Choose an attacker with reliable special punishment and a useful Striker or special Stun for chain extension. Avoid abilities that grant Immortality or Indestructible: either gives Glykhan an immediate lethal Special 3. Do not assume attack boosts or critical-damage buffs bypass his locked base ratings.

Attacker profile

These are useful traits, not a claim that one Champion is mandatory.

  • Reliable Heavy and Special Attack punishment, because damage begins from a punish opening.

  • A useful Striker or Special Attack Stun can extend a valid damage chain.

  • Comfort dodging Special Attacks is important because Wound of Defiance can punish blocked hits.

Before entering

  • Treat blocked Special Attack hits as unsafe: Wound of Defiance can deal Direct Damage through Block.

  • Learn the Counter-Attack Charge response before attempting long Basic Attack strings.

  • Do not bring a strategy that depends on your own Immortality or Indestructible; those effects can trigger a lethal Special Attack 3 response.

When the fight goes wrong

My Basic Attacks stop doing useful damage

Re-establish a valid damage chain by dodging a Heavy or Special Attack and punishing the recovery.

Counter-Attack Charge appears

Stop ordinary Basic Attacks and use the first hit of a Special Attack or Striker to remove the charge.

I die while blocking a Special Attack

Wound of Defiance can trigger through Block. Practice the full dodge instead of treating Block as the safety option.
