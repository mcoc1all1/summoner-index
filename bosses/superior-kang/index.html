SOURCED FIGHT GUIDE

Learn the fight loop

Guide reviewed 2026-10-29 · Not gameplay-tested in the current build

A short preparation aid. Read the full in-game boss abilities before entry; this is not an exhaustive mechanics transcript.

Core loop — rotate openings

Strike while he is stunned, dashing, using a heavy or recovering from a special. He retreats and fires a beam: dodge or well-timed block it for Slipstream. Do not repeat the same opening; he blocks and stuns you.

Ten Slipstream — trigger Wounded

At ten charges, use another eligible opening to trigger Wounded. You have 20 seconds with his armor disabled and no block or counterattack. Spend damage here. Avoiding every SP1 hit also earns a charge.

1 · Unique buffs

Different buff types on your attacker boost Wounded damage. SP2 is locked; learn the SP1 and counter-beam rhythm.

2 · Unique debuffs

Different debuff types on Kang boost Wounded damage. SP2 unlocks and counterattacks can change fight speed: react to the animation rather than repeating a fixed dodge rhythm.

3 · Buff stacks

Total attacker buffs now boost Wounded damage. Kang remembers your last TWO opening types, so rotate at least three. Nullify, Stagger and Fate Seal cannot remove his buffs here.

4 · Damaging debuffs and finish

Damaging debuffs boost Wounded damage. Specials can change speed. Beat the 80-second Evangelos deadline; at lethal damage he becomes Wounded again, requiring five basic hits to finish.

Archive — the escalating clock

Buffs and debuffs enter his quest-persistent Archive. Periodic Archive effects eventually give way to the Evangelos countdown. Its expiry forces a lethal SP3; progress through openings and Wounded windows instead of stalling.

Common mistakes

  • Repeating parry-hit openings until Kang punishes you.

  • Treating a health reset as the end of the encounter.

  • Expecting Heal Block to prevent his rewind or Willpower to heal Archive debuffs.

Team selection and preparation

Plan buff-producing and debuff-producing attackers around the phase bonuses. Favor reliable openings over theoretical damage. Power Lock and Special Lock cannot shut him down; check your path nodes and current in-game descriptions before committing resources.

Attacker profile

These are useful traits, not a claim that one Champion is mandatory.

  • Can rotate several opening types without repeating the same trigger.

  • Can produce multiple Buff or Debuff types to exploit the changing Wounded bonuses.

  • Reliable Special Attack dodges and counter-beam timing matter more than theoretical burst damage.

Before entering

  • Practice at least three different openings: Stun, intercept/dash punish, Heavy punish, or Special Attack recovery punish.

  • Treat each Health Bar refill as the next phase, not a revive or ordinary healing event.

  • Do not rely on Power Lock or Special Lock as the control plan.

When the fight goes wrong

I stopped gaining Slipstream

Change the opening type. Kang punishes repeated openings, and Phase 3 remembers the last two types.

My Wounded window feels weak

Match your damage plan to the current Health Bar: unique Buffs, unique Debuffs, Buff stacks, then damaging Debuffs.

The fight ends in a forced Special Attack 3

You have reached the Evangelos deadline. Prioritize reliable openings and Wounded windows instead of stalling.
