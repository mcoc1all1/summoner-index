SOURCED FIGHT GUIDE

Learn the fight loop

Guide reviewed 2026-11-04 · Not gameplay-tested in the current build

A short preparation aid. Read the full in-game boss abilities before entry; this is not an exhaustive mechanics transcript.

Setup — read both charge counters

Watch two different resources: Bahamet's Volatility helps trigger the wave, while your Removal charges determine which armor interaction you can perform. Stay near enough during his buildup to encourage Volatility, then bait a special once he has the required stacks. Plan to answer the resulting radiation wave with your own special.

Opening • 100–81%

Intercept a radiation blast with SP1 or SP2 to obtain a Removal charge, then knock Bahamet down while you hold it. That knockdown, not the charge alone, creates the temporary armor-down opening. Use the window, then rebuild when the armor returns.

Middle • 80–41%

The required action changes: use a special into a radiation blast first to collect Removal charges, and reflect a later blast back toward Bahamet with a heavy release into the radiation blast while a Removal charge is active; a well-timed block can also reflect and extend the resulting armor-down window. If reflection fails, verify your charge before blaming the timing.

Late • 40–11%

Keep the charge-first reflection plan, but inspect the blast's appearance. A red Fire Blast must be avoided, not reflected. Only attempt the reflection on a suitable radiation blast while your Removal charge remains active.

Finish • 10–0%

Stop searching for a new Removal charge; it is no longer required for reflecting. The final exchange may continue because Bahamet can return a reflected radiation blast. Respond to each return until it reaches him and reduces his armor's damage reduction; continue dodging fire blasts.

Between damage windows

A phase change restores Corrupted Armor and clears your Removal charges. Re-read the counters before the next interaction. Repeating the same special twice in a row can also invite Auto-Block while the armor is active, so do not assume a repeated burst will land safely.

Common mistakes

  • Confusing Bahamet's Volatility with your own Removal charges.

  • Trying a phase-two or phase-three reflection without first acquiring a Removal charge.

  • Treating a red Fire Blast as though it were a reflectable radiation wave.

  • Spending the special needed to earn a charge before the blast appears.

  • Expecting the final phase to follow the same knockdown or charged-reflection rules as earlier phases.

Attacker profile

These are useful traits, not a claim that one Champion is mandatory.

  • Comfort with well-timed blocks or heavy spacing matters once reflecting the blast becomes the damage entry.

  • Plan access to an SP1 or SP2 for charge generation instead of spending all power immediately before the wave.

  • Consider a champion you can control reliably during repeated radiation exchanges, especially in the final phase.

Before entering

  • Learn which icon belongs to Bahamet's Volatility and which represents the attacker's Removal charges; do not treat them as one counter.

  • Keep power ready for a special response to the radiation blast, especially when preparing the phase-two or phase-three reflect.

  • Watch projectile color before responding and check linked path modifiers; the base fight guide does not cover every node combination.

When the fight goes wrong

No radiation wave follows his special

Check whether Bahamet had enough Volatility when he threw it. Your own Removal charge is not the trigger for his wave.

I got a charge but still cannot hurt him in phase one

A knockdown is the next required step. Use the charge to create the armor-down window rather than trying to damage through the protection.

My parry or heavy does not reflect the blast

Check that you still hold a Removal charge from landing a special into an earlier radiation blast: a well-timed block alone does not replace the charge requirement in phases two and three. Also check whether the projectile is red (a Fire Blast, which cannot be reflected). Community reports note visually similar Cleanse and Removal icons; read the active effect rather than trusting appearance alone.

He sends the final wave back at me

Treat it as a multi-step exchange. Read his response and prepare to reflect the radiation blast again; do not advance as though the first reflection has completed the mechanic.
