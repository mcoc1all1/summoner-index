SOURCED FIGHT GUIDE

Learn the fight loop

Guide reviewed 2026-11-04 · Not gameplay-tested in the current build

A short preparation aid. Read the full in-game boss abilities before entry; this is not an exhaustive mechanics transcript.

Countdown — complete the current objective

At the start of each Plot Armor, identify the task under the timer. Intercepts and punishes of recovery can buy time. Clear the task before the countdown ends and Gwenmaster's subsequent SP2 damages her. If time expires with the task incomplete, the same armor returns and the SP2 hurts the attacker. On a new attempt, check the displayed armor and counters rather than assuming partial task progress was saved.

1 · Emma Frost — build four Prowess, then Dexterity

Use the power-bar difference to cycle Diamond Form: put your power above hers to trigger the form, then let her power move ahead to remove it and award Prowess. When four Prowess effects are showing, activate Dexterity during her SP1. Its reverse-control segment is the main execution hazard; practice the input rather than dashing by instinct.

2 · Killmonger — clear Counterpunch safely

This objective is a charge-removal exercise. Repeated safe contacts reduce Counterpunch, with knockdowns taking off more. Hitting into her block can activate a short Unstoppable response; do not chase it recklessly. Her SP1 can also enable Vibranium Armor that reflects contact damage from your specials, so check the icon before attacking into it.

3 · Invisible Woman — strip the Force Field

Plan several controlled intercept or special openings to earn Fury and reduce the Force Field. After Gwenmaster uses SP1, Invisibility can cause Miss. Its duration begins paused and may resume following a Miss or Bleed; let the visible state resolve rather than forcing a full combo through it. Removing the shield—not simply outlasting the timer—is the objective.

4 · Doctor Doom — use four different knockdowns

Keep a checklist of Heavy, SP1, SP2 and SP3. Each must knock her down once; repeating a used method is not a substitute and can be punished by Unstoppable. She also steals power passively, so plan how to reach SP3. Be cautious after her SP1 Shock: a follow-up charged heavy can convert it into a Stun.

5 · Aarkus — collect Armor Breaks at range

Create distance until ten Armor Breaks have accumulated on your attacker, then get the knockdown. Remaining too close instead feeds Gwenmaster power. Her SP1 can make approaching risky while Armor Broken because of Coldsnap, so do not rush in before a workable opening.

6 · Iron Man — remove the Armor Ups

Use the fourth light attack in a combo or break her block to remove Armor Up effects. Watch the Auto-Block granted by her SP1: an appropriately timed heavy used to interrupt her heavy can clear it and remove another Armor Up. Once the armor is gone, the final objective is complete.

Common mistakes

  • Treating the encounter as a normal damage race instead of solving the current Plot Armor challenge.

  • Assuming partial objective counters persist after a knockout without checking the displayed state.

  • Trying a normal Dexterity input during Emma Frost's reversed-control SP1.

  • Using contact-damage specials during Killmonger's reflecting Vibranium Armor.

  • Repeating an already-counted knockdown in Doctor Doom's objective.

  • Ignoring Iron Man's Auto-Block after SP1.

  • Prioritize predictable power management and comfort reversing the Dexterity input in Emma Frost's challenge.

  • A familiar attacker and clean objective execution matter more here than assuming one specific champion is a universal solution.

Before entering

  • Read the linked quest node and current in-game objective text before starting; this guide does not cover every linked-path modifier.

  • Choose an attacker with predictable power generation and a usable Heavy, SP1, SP2 and SP3. This is an execution checklist, not a ranked counter list.

  • Prepare for reverse controls, Miss, reflection and Auto-Block across different objectives; reassess when Plot Armor changes.

When the fight goes wrong

Her SP2 damages me and the same objective returns

First identify which Plot Armor objective is still active. Completing it before the countdown ends redirects the SP2 damage to Gwenmaster; ordinary attacks alone may not solve the task. Use controlled intercepts and recovery punishes to gain decision time. If a new attempt begins, recheck the currently displayed armor rather than guessing that partial objective counts survived.

I can't clear Emma Frost's challenge

Count four Prowess effects from cycling the power-bar relationship. Then activate Dexterity during her SP1 with the reversed controls in mind.

My specials hit into damage reflection

Check whether Killmonger's Vibranium Armor is active after SP1. Avoid contact-damage specials while that effect is present.

The Invisible Woman objective seems stuck

Look for the Force Field and Invisibility effects separately. Avoid repeatedly missing into Invisibility; return to safe intercept/special openings when you can hit.

Doom's objective refuses another knockdown

Track all four distinct methods and whether the knockdown connected. Repeating a method does not satisfy a missing one; account for passive power steal.

Iron Man Auto-Blocks my combo

After SP1, seek a safe heavy-versus-heavy interruption to remove Auto-Block, or continue with a valid armor-removal method when the opening is available.
