SOURCED FIGHT GUIDE

Learn the fight loop

Guide reviewed 2026-11-01 · Not gameplay-tested in the current build

A short preparation aid. Read the full in-game boss abilities before entry; this is not an exhaustive mechanics transcript.

Before entry — inspect the live nodes

The March 2026 run calls out Fisticuffs and Stun Immunity. It also warns to spend power before the reset. These have post-retune support; Hurt Locker is still only a historical lead in this review.

Openings — fight without relying on parry

Use heavy or special recovery to create openings rather than assuming a parry stun. Confirm the defender is actually stunned before attacking; a successful block is not proof of a stun.

Rotation — adapt to the active restrictions

If Hurt Locker remains, alternate special levels. If Plagued Mind remains, spend power before its reset. If Fisticuffs remains, wait for its opening restriction before relying on buffs or debuffs.

Common mistakes

  • Treating a pre-rework node list as current.

  • Attacking after a parry without checking whether the boss was stunned.

  • Choosing a rotation that conflicts with active special or power restrictions.

Team selection and preparation

Before entering

  • The October 2025 v53.0 announcement confirms reduced signature levels, simpler nodes and tuning for The Champion and Grandmaster. It does not list every individual boss node.

  • Do not confuse the 6.1.2 Sentinel miniboss with this 6.1.6 finale. Reports about Biohazard removal from 6.1.2 do not verify the nodes on this boss.

  • Post-October-8-2025 players report removed global nodes. Do not carry a pre-retune quest-wide restriction into this fight automatically: inspect the current quest, path and boss panels separately. These reports do not establish every remaining boss node.

  • Post-retune footage check: 2026-03-13, 13:16–15:15. Post-retune commentary identifies Fisticuffs, Stun Immunity and a power reset.

When the fight goes wrong

An online guide says Biohazard was removed

Check the quest number first. That report concerns the 6.1.2 miniboss; it is not evidence that this boss lost Fisticuffs, Hurt Locker or Plagued Mind.
