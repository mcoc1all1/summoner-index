SOURCED FIGHT GUIDE

Learn the fight loop

Guide reviewed 2026-10-29 · Not gameplay-tested in the current build

A short preparation aid. Read the full in-game boss abilities before entry; this is not an exhaustive mechanics transcript.

Prepare — stop the reset

Selective Timestream periodically restores health and clears debuffs. Arrange reliable Shock or Incinerate coverage, or a reviewed passive Heal Block setup. Indomitable prevents heal reversal.

Attack — choose damage carefully

High Energy Diet punishes energy damage with healing. Favor physical damage and watch the reset timer before relying on a stun opening.

Control — keep specials manageable

Her Cosmic Charges become Power Gain buffs. Bait specials rather than letting power accumulate; spending time above two bars builds Prowess and can eventually make specials unblockable.

Common mistakes

  • Using heal reversal as the healing solution.

  • Launching an energy-heavy rotation without checking High Energy Diet.

  • Attacking through the debuff-clear timer as though the stun will remain.

Team selection and preparation

Prioritize sustained healing control and familiar special dodges. Gwenperion is not the ordinary Gwenpool kit. Check the original story nodes and any synergy requirements before entering.

Attacker profile

These are useful traits, not a claim that one Champion is mandatory.

  • Reliable Shock or Incinerate access, or another reviewed way to stop the Selective Timestream heal.

  • Predominantly Physical damage is easier to manage into High Energy Diet than an Energy-heavy rotation.

  • Comfort baiting Special Attacks before Power Gain and Prowess snowball.

Before entering

  • Identify the healing-control mechanic before entry; do not rely on ordinary heal reversal where Indomitable prevents it.

  • Build a rotation that can maintain Shock or Incinerate without forcing unsafe openings.

  • Keep Power under control by baiting Specials rather than allowing multiple Power Gain buffs to accumulate.

When the fight goes wrong

Her health keeps resetting

Your healing-control effect is not active at the Selective Timestream check. Rebuild it before the timer resolves.

My damage causes more healing than progress

Check whether your main damage is Energy damage into High Energy Diet. Shift to Physical damage or another attacker profile.
