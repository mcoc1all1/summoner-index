SOURCED FIGHT GUIDE

Learn the fight loop

Guide reviewed 2026-10-29 · Not gameplay-tested in the current build

A short preparation aid. Read the full in-game boss abilities before entry; this is not an exhaustive mechanics transcript.

Core loop — earn the opening

Watch Smite before performing the phase action. Remove all Desecrated Charges, then attack during Sanctified. SP3 is unavailable in that window. A health refill advances the seal, not the end of the fight.

1 · Form — her corner

Put Lotan against her wall. Use a second medium or SP1 to remove charges while Smite is active.

2 · Mind — your corner

Move to your wall, then use first and fourth light hits or a Striker. Her heavy becomes Unstoppable; do not interrupt it carelessly.

3 · Direction — outside the storm

Keep both fighters outside the Maelstrom and away from walls. Well-timed blocks and SP2 remove charges. Lotan is now Stun Immune: a successful parry does not create a stun opening.

4 · Luck — involve the storm

At least one fighter must be in the Maelstrom. Dodge attacks or land heavies with Smite active. Learn the SP1 dodge rhythm; its hits are now Unblockable.

5 · Soul — dodge, then dash

The storm fills the arena. Dodge the penultimate special hit to earn Untouchable, then dash into the final hit so it Misses. Basic and special hits refresh your Degeneration timer. Do not dash early.

Common mistakes

  • Performing the correct attack from the wrong position.

  • Assuming parries still stun from the third seal onward.

  • Using ordinary blocking against later Unblockable specials.

Team selection and preparation

Favor a Science attacker: other classes suffer reduced damaging-effect ability accuracy. Bring a usable Striker and familiar special timings. Leave the storm to clear its Degeneration before the final seal. Review path modifiers separately; later seals progressively banish survival and offensive effects.

Attacker profile

These are useful traits, not a claim that one Champion is mandatory.

  • Comfort fighting at both corners and in open space; the required action changes with the active Seal.

  • Reliable Well-Timed Blocks and Special Attack timing help with the middle seals.

  • Good movement control matters more than a specific damage type because position is part of the mechanic.

Before entering

  • Read the active Seal before acting; the same input can fail if the position requirement is wrong.

  • Use short combos so you can reposition for the next Seal objective.

  • From the third Seal onward, do not assume a Parry creates a Stun opening.

When the fight goes wrong

The correct attack does not advance the Seal

Check position first. Lotan's mechanics combine an action with a location requirement.

I get hit while trying to block later Specials

Some later Special Attack interactions are Unblockable. Use the required dodge or Well-Timed Block only where the active Seal calls for it.
