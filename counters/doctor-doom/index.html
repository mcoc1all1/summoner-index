Fight plan

Build Physical Vulnerability with controlled basics. Only use a heavy setup when Doom can be stunned safely, then cancel the first heavy hit into Special 2. Track the special-Evade cooldown and dodge manually when it is unavailable.

Check before choosing

Matchup rationale and conditions
Questing

Proposed interaction: Community players list Immortal Abomination as a Doom option. Poison and Acid Burn provide damage without relying on Shock.

Poison

Fight plan

Build self-Poisons with safe basics and Special 1, then activate Special 2 for Toxic Aura. Stay near Doom only while you have a safe opening, and bait specials before recommitting.

Check before choosing

  • Doom must be susceptible to Poison; check nodes for immunity or cleansing.
  • Maintain enough self-Poisons to support Toxic Aura; do not trade unsafe hits merely to stay close.
Awakening / signature
Self-Poisons and Toxic Aura are available unawakened. Awakening improves setup and duration; no matchup signature breakpoint is claimed.

Watch out The aura needs proximity and does not protect against Doom’s heavy or specials. Poison-resistant or debuff-restricting nodes can undermine the plan.

Why this confidence?

Direct matchup report with supporting kit mechanics; no independent current-build gameplay test or seasonal ranking.
