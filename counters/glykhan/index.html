Fight plan

Create damage chains by fully avoiding a Heavy or Special Attack and punishing the recovery; use Storm's damage inside those valid chains.

Check before choosing

  • Start damage from a valid punish opening.
  • Stop Basic Attacks when Counter-Attack Charge requires a Special Attack or Striker response.

How confidence labels work

Limited evidence means only part of the matchup is supported. Community recommended means players have reported the counter. Community-backed · kit supported also has documented utility that matches the fight. Tested on documented nodes and Repeatedly tested are reserved for recorded gameplay tests. Confidence describes the evidence behind the recommendation; it is not a champion rating or win-rate score.
