How evidence labels work

A single cited source is a research lead, not independent confirmation. Linked player reports, official pages and kit references describe the source types, not a win-rate ranking. Only a dated, documented in-game test may receive a tested label. A citation does not grant permission to reproduce third-party writing, data compilations or artwork.
