Her Special 2 Power Sting can turn into Petrify after it completes its duration, giving a limited regeneration-control window; her passive Stun access is not reliable during Sasquatch's Stun-immune Wrath phase.

Only a dated, documented in-game test may receive a tested label. A citation does not grant permission to reproduce third-party writing, data compilations or artwork.
