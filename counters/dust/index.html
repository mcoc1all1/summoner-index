Incinerate

Fight plan

Apply Incinerate consistently, avoid unnecessary hits into Dust's Block, and use the contact Medium Attack if you need a Parry-Stun opening.

Check before choosing

  • Use Incinerate to manage Sand rather than relying on Shock or Bleed.
  • Parry only a contact attack when you need the Stun opening.

Watch out Dust's Light Attacks are non-contact, so careless Parry attempts can build Sand instead of giving you control.

Why this confidence?

Community counter discussions repeatedly support Sunspot; no independent current-season timing test is recorded.

How evidence labels work

Only a dated, documented in-game test may receive a tested label. A citation does not grant permission to reproduce third-party writing, data compilations or artwork.
