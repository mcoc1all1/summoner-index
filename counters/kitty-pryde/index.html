Why it works: Player reports support the reworked Iron Man: special hits remove Kitty’s Prowess.

Prowess removalArmor Up

Fight plan

Maintain Armor Up, bait a special or heavy, and launch a safely connected special to remove Prowess. Recheck her Prowess before attempting an intercept.

Check before choosing

  • Use the reworked Iron Man, not Iron Man (Infinity War).
  • Land the special in a safe window; do not assume it can hit through Phase.
  • Retain Armor Up for Incinerate protection.
Awakening / signature
The stated Prowess-removal tool is in the base kit; awakening adds survivability, with no matchup minimum established.

Watch out Prowess removal is not permanent Miss prevention. A poorly timed attack can still meet Phase; Armor Break can remove protection.

Why this counter works and what to check

Why it works: Crucible players recommend Ironheart; Tracking and Prowess removal address Kitty’s Phase setup.

TrackingProwess removalRupture

Fight plan

Select Rupture before entry. Build five debuffs for Tracking, use safe heavy openings to remove Prowess, and use Special 1 to preserve debuff uptime before spending a damage special.

Check before choosing

  • Choose Rupture pre-fight.
  • Keep at least five debuffs for Tracking and check node debuff restrictions.
  • Use Prowess removal to prevent Phase rather than relying solely on Miss bypass.
Awakening / signature
