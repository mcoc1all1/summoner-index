NeurotoxinBleedPoison

Fight plan

Punish special recovery with a safe heavy to remove the form, then establish Bleed and Poison for Neurotoxins. Watch for the form returning.

Check before choosing

  • Use an awakened Archangel for Neurotoxin healing prevention.
  • Remove Black Uru before relying on Bleed; verify Bleed and Poison are allowed by nodes.
  • A missed heavy punish can leave the form and regeneration active.

Petrify

Fight plan

Cycle Special 1 and allow Intimidating Presence to build. Establish Petrify, then use awakened Fear of the Void plus further Petrify for sustained healing control. Continue baiting specials safely.

Check before choosing

  • This plan uses awakened Fear of the Void; no numerical signature minimum is established.
  • Check debuff access and healing modifiers.
  • Despair mastery can assist but is not assumed in the stated rotation.
Awakening / signature
Required for the Fear of the Void extension used in this plan; base Petrify is available without awakening.

Watch out Random debuff order delays control. Purification, debuff immunity, or altered healing nodes can break this plan.
