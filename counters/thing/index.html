Battlegrounds

Proposed interaction: Black Cat can reduce Thing's defensive ability accuracy as her Heist setup develops, limiting the Rock Stacks he gains from being struck. Bleed attempts can also remove stacks, but her defensive-ability control is the more distinctive matchup tool.

Fight plan

Build and maintain Black Cat's defensive ability-accuracy reduction before relying on it to prevent Rock Stacks. Watch the Rock Stack counter throughout; add Bleed pressure when useful and take safe openings rather than assuming the control is active immediately.

Check before choosing

  • Bleed attempts control Rock Stacks rather than deal Bleed damage.
  • Check for debuff-related nodes.

Watch out Debuff-immunity or debuff-punishing nodes can remove the advantage.
