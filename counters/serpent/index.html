Power control

Fight plan

Use safe heavy openings to establish Negative Zone and refresh it. Once power is controlled, build the other Dimensions and use Special 2 during a safe heavy-cancel opening.

Check before choosing

  • Negative Zone must be active; do not confuse ability power control with control of every power source.
  • If Stun is unavailable, punish a heavy rather than relying on a stun-assisted setup.
Awakening / signature
Negative Zone is a base-kit ability; awakening enhances the Dimension setup but is not required for the power-rate reduction.

Watch out A lost Dimension can expose you to rapid power gain. Death Immunity and block damage remain; no guaranteed fast finish is claimed.
