7★ stat focus · eligibility verified

Attacker starting point
Critical Rating

For Adam Warlock, a Critical Rating trial changes eligible would-be critical event frequency; it must not be assumed to increase noncritical debuff tick damage. Keep the source-linked setup constant: Start the Sovereignty counter with the second light into block or Special 1 or 2. Compare Critical Damage Rating against the same opponent, rank and resources. Do not mistake a changed direct hit for proof of longer resource uptime or stronger debuff ticks.

Alternative to test: Not reviewed

Defender starting point
Special Damage

For Adam Warlock on defense, Special Damage helps eligible direct Special hits on defense, not the defender's blocking or passive damage-over-time effects. Kit limitation: Power Steal interrupts passive power gain. Special 3 forces him out of the pod. Compare Block Proficiency using the same attack type, defensive sequence, relic state and node; no across-mode ranking or gameplay result is claimed.

Alternative to test: Not reviewed

Learn relic types & stat-focus options
RELICS

Battlecast or Statcast?

Battlecasts combine a Striker with ability-based rune effects. Select the runes and check their triggers against the champion’s kit.

Statcasts also have Strikers. They boost a primary and a secondary attribute; a statue identifies the class and primary attribute, while variants differ in their secondary attribute. Their runes have a single option.

Compare the exact item you own, not just its name. Some stats have diminishing returns. A larger displayed rating need not produce a proportionally larger benefit.

Statcasts were designed to provide rune effects on defense, without a defender Striker. Mode restrictions and later fixes require a current in-game check; this guide does not certify availability in every mode.

Kabam’s Statcast design notes ↗
7-STAR CHAMPIONS

Two roles. Separate settings.

Stat focus is a 7★ champion feature. Attacker focus is for the champion you control; defender focus is for AI defense. A defensive-sounding stat such as Block Proficiency can still be useful in the attacker slot. Adrenaline is attacker-only in Kabam’s published focus chart; it is not a defender choice.

Start by confirming that the champion version in question is eligible for Stat Focus. Only then compare a focus that addresses the problem you are testing.

Do not add relic and focus percentages together as a promised damage gain. Check the current values in your game; balance changes can alter the choice.

Kabam’s stat-focus balance principles ↗

What the focus options do

Practical selection notes, not formulas or a substitute for the current in-game descriptions.

Critical Rating

Improves the chance of eligible hits being critical. Check guaranteed-critical and cannot-crit mechanics first.

Critical Damage Rating

Improves damage from eligible critical hits. It does not make a hit critical.

Special Damage

Supports special-attack damage. Check separately whether associated effects scale with it.

Armor Penetration

Reduces the effectiveness of the opponent’s armor for applicable attacks; it is not Armor Break.

Block Penetration

Targets damage through the opponent’s block. It gives little value when the opponent avoids blocking.

Adrenaline

Supports the recovery of recoverable health through the Adrenaline system. It is not a passive regeneration or immunity effect; read the current in-game modifier.

Armor Rating

Improves armor-based mitigation. A stat increase is not necessarily an Armor Up buff or an extra stack.

Block Proficiency

Reduces damage taken through your block. An attacker can choose it to preserve health.

Physical Resistance

Helps against physical damage. It does not cover every damage type or prevent debuffs.

Energy Resistance

Helps against energy damage. It is not the same as immunity to Shock, Incinerate or other effects.

BEFORE SPENDING MATERIALS

Make one change. Measure the difference.

  1. Confirm 7★ Stat Focus eligibility, binding eligibility, mode rules and your owned relic’s exact effects in the game. Check any awakening or signature-dependent effects.
  2. Pick a repeatable opponent and record nodes, champion rank, signature, masteries and rotation. Keep those fixed.
  3. Compare your existing loadout with one changed focus or relic. Repeat runs and record fight time, remaining health and execution mistakes.
  4. Practice Striker timing before relying on it to extend a combo. Check opponent mechanics, power and stun-related restrictions before calling it.
  5. Keep the choice that improves the outcome you need. Use multiple matchups before spending scarce rank-up or awakening materials.

Choosing the exact item you own

  1. Read the primary and secondary attributes on your Statcast. The statue name alone does not identify the full variant.
  2. Match the attribute to the damage actually used in your rotation. Critical statistics need critical hits or an explicit conversion mechanic; damage-over-time effects do not automatically benefit from Special Damage.
  3. For a Battlecast, open each rune and check its trigger. Confirm your champion creates that effect, and whether the benefit requires an awakened relic.
  4. Test Striker placement at a safe point in your rotation. An extra combo is useful only if it does not break a required timing, debuff window or opponent mechanic.
  5. If the suggested relic is unavailable, compare an eligible owned item against no relic under the same conditions. Do not spend materials solely because a community shortlist names a statue.

Common mistakes

Using a Stat Focus draft before confirming a 7★ version exists, critical rating on guaranteed-critical hits, block penetration when no one blocks, treating resistance as immunity, assuming one Statcast statue means one variant, and copying a high-signature relic build onto an unawakened relic.

What remains unverified

Exact rune and variant builds, minimum relic rarity/rank/signature, numeric performance gains, many champions’ current 7★ availability, and current-season defensive results. Unverified Stat Focus drafts are now deliberately hidden from public recommendations. Relic and focus settings do not replace the awakening or node requirements in counter guides.
