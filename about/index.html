ABOUT SUMMONER INDEX

An independent companion for better fights.

Built around MCOC mechanics, roster-aware decisions and transparent limits.

Who runs the site?

Summoner Index is an independent Marvel Contest of Champions companion project associated with the MCOC 1ALL1 creator channel. It is operated from India and is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Kabam, Marvel or the game rights holders.

What can you do here?

Find source-linked matchup ideas, browse recorded champion utilities, compare protections and prepare with a locally saved roster. Boss guides, Relic experiments and Stat Focus starting points explain relevant fight conditions and limitations. We do not claim every recommendation was tested in the current game client.

How is advice reviewed?

We distinguish factual mechanics from independently written editorial advice. A source link is a research lead, not proof of a current-game test or permission to copy another work. Some protections, counter choices and Relic attributes still require additional review. Explore the methodology, coverage inventory and content policy.

Contact and corrections

Use Report an issue or email mcoc1all1@proton.me for factual corrections, accessibility problems or rights concerns. The feedback form creates an email draft rather than submitting anything to a site server. Share the affected page and game version when relevant.

Advertising transparency

As of 15 November 2026, this site serves no advertisements or paid placements. If monetization is introduced, advertising will remain separate from editorial recommendations and will follow the published privacy notice and advertising standards.
