General / Non-Recoil Setup

A flexible setup for questing, story progression and mixed rosters without the self-damage of the Recoil branch.

Best for

Story and event questingMixed rostersLong-form contentPlayers who do not want Recoil damage

Build priorities

  1. Start with Parry and Dexterity, then build toward Precision, Cruelty and Block Proficiency.

  2. Use Willpower and Recovery when you value sustain, especially if your common fights place debuffs on you.

  3. Spend remaining points according to roster: Deep Wounds for Bleed teams, Mystic Dispersion for Mystic-heavy rosters, Despair for debuff-heavy attackers, Assassin for faster finishes.

  4. Treat bridge masteries as unlock costs first; do not assume more points in every early node are better than reaching stronger end-of-branch options.

What to watch

  • There is no universal best all-around build: roster, progression and content matter.

  • Glass Cannon trades health for damage, so do not max it automatically if you rely on durability.

  • Deep Wounds and Mystic Dispersion are poor investments when your roster rarely uses the mechanic they support.
