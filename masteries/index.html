MASTERY SETUPS

Build masteries around how you actually play.

There is no single best mastery tree for every account. Start with the universal core, then spend the flexible points around your roster, game mode and tolerance for self-damage.

How to use these guides

These are starter frameworks, not rigid screenshots to copy forever. Unlock the core movement and offense pieces first, then tune specialist masteries when your roster or game mode gives them a reason to exist.

Core and commonly-used masteries

Proficiencies · Core

Parry

Points: 1 minimum; add more when stun duration matters

Unlocks the basic Parry play pattern. Extra ranks are mainly about stronger/longer stun utility rather than unlocking the mechanic itself.

Proficiencies · Core

Dexterity

Points: 1 point for the essential evade utility

One of the most important universal movement tools. Additional ranks are usually lower priority than unlocking other high-value masteries.

Offense · Core offense

Precision

Points: Usually worth maxing

Reliable critical-rate value across a broad roster. Community setups consistently prioritize the full Precision node over bridge points.

Offense · Core offense

Cruelty

Points: Usually worth maxing

Broad critical-damage value and a common high-priority offensive investment.

Defense · Core defense

Block Proficiency

Points: High priority; commonly maxed

Reduces chip from blocked hits and Parry-heavy play, which matters in questing, war and difficult boss content.

Defense · Flexible core

Willpower

Points: 1-3 depending on build

Provides sustain while non-damaging or damaging debuffs are active. It becomes much more important in Recoil/Liquid Courage/Double Edge setups.

Defense · Support

Recovery

Points: Roster/build dependent

Improves healing received and pairs naturally with Willpower-heavy sustain builds.

Offense · High-value offense

Glass Cannon

Points: 1-3 depending on comfort

Efficient attack increase at the cost of health. Strong for players comfortable trading durability for damage.

Offense · Specialist

Despair

Points: 1-3 when your roster applies many debuffs

Adds heal-control value to debuff-heavy attackers and is often useful in regeneration matchups.

Offense · Roster dependent

Deep Wounds

Points: Invest when Bleed attackers are a major part of your roster

Strongly benefits Bleed-centric champions but gives much less value to rosters that rarely rely on Bleed.

Offense · Finisher

Assassin

Points: 1-5 depending on available points and goals

Improves finishing power when the opponent is low on health. This can matter in timed fights such as Battlegrounds.

Proficiencies · Roster dependent

Mystic Dispersion

Points: Invest when Mystics are a major part of your offense/defense

Excellent for Mystic-heavy rosters that regularly interact with opponent buffs, but not universally valuable.

Decision rules

  • Universal before specialist: Parry, Dexterity, Precision, Cruelty and Block Proficiency generally give more accounts value than immediately maxing a niche branch.

  • Roster decides specialist points: Deep Wounds needs Bleed attackers, Mystic Dispersion needs Mystics that benefit from opponent buffs, and Despair needs debuff-heavy attackers.

  • Mode matters: health preservation is more important in Battlegrounds than in many farming situations, so Recoil setups need a stronger reason there.

  • Do not confuse an unlock path with an ideal final investment: some early masteries are mainly bridge points used to reach more valuable nodes.

Mastery costs and availability can change. Check the in-game mastery screen before spending Units or cores. These pages focus on build logic and tradeoffs rather than claiming a permanent exact point-cost chart.
