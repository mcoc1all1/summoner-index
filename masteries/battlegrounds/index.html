Battlegrounds Setup

A health-score-aware approach that values fast finishes on attack and annoying defensive masteries without automatically sacrificing health to Recoil.

Best for

Victory TrackGladiator CircuitDedicated BG mastery loadouts

Build priorities

  1. Use a non-Recoil baseline by default unless a specific meta or roster clearly benefits from Liquid Courage / Double Edge.

  2. Keep Precision and Cruelty strong on offense; Assassin can be valuable because finishing the final portion of a fight faster can improve time score.

  3. Despair is valuable when your attackers stack debuffs into healing defenders.

  4. Defensive options such as Limber, Stand Your Ground and Inequity/Resonate can add friction to opponent fights; prioritize them only if your BG defense benefits from them.

  5. Mystic Dispersion is a strong specialist choice when your deck is Mystic-heavy and repeatedly fights buff-heavy attackers.

What to watch

  • Recoil, Double Edge and Liquid Courage can reduce remaining-health score or punish Special-heavy attackers, so they are not the default recommendation for BG.

  • Masteries should match the current season meta; a node package can change the value of a normally strong choice.

  • Saved Battleground mastery loadouts can preserve a dedicated BG setup even when your normal questing masteries later change.
