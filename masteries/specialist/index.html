Specialist Masteries

How to choose between Mystic Dispersion, Deep Wounds, Despair, Assassin and defensive branches once your universal core is in place.

Best for

Endgame tuningRoster specializationWar/Battleground defensePlayers with spare mastery points

Build priorities

  1. Mystic Dispersion: prioritize when Mystics form a large part of your active roster and frequently interact with opponent buffs.

  2. Deep Wounds: prioritize when a significant share of your best attackers rely on Bleed duration for damage.

  3. Despair: prioritize for debuff-heavy attackers and heal-control matchups.

  4. Assassin: prioritize when faster final-phase damage is valuable, especially in timed fights.

  5. Limber / Stand Your Ground / Inequity-style defense: consider when Battlegrounds or Alliance War defense matters more than general questing efficiency.

What to watch

  • Do not max every specialist mastery at once; each one has an opportunity cost.

  • A specialist mastery can be excellent for one roster and almost wasted for another.

  • Revisit these choices when your main roster, BG deck or Alliance War role changes.
