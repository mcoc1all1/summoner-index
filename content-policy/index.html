EDITORIAL POLICY

Independent guidance. Clear standards.

How we assess sources, explain uncertainty and keep advertising separate.

Independent guides and tools

Summoner Index is an independent fan project operated from India. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Marvel or Kabam. Game and character names identify the subjects discussed; they do not indicate a partnership.

Facts, sources and original writing

Summoner Index brings together factual game mechanics, source-linked community research and editorial fight advice. Facts learned from references can inform our research; copied descriptions, distinctive compilations, code and artwork require a separate reuse assessment. A citation identifies evidence; a citation is not a license or proof of an independently performed gameplay test. Encounter phases and in-game terminology may naturally match across independently written guides.

We do not treat publicly accessible guides, translations, tier lists or databases as freely reusable. Material requiring permission must have a documented license or other lawful basis before publication. A fan-site disclaimer does not supply that basis.

How to read the guidance

Source-linked means reference material has been recorded; it does not by itself verify every mechanic or activation condition. Source-checked means a claim was compared with cited evidence, not independently confirmed in the current game client. Gameplay-tested requires a recorded test with game version, date, conditions and supporting evidence. Unknown or unreviewed protection conditions must not be treated as guaranteed immunities. Neither label means official approval. Advice may depend on paths, nodes, awakening and patches; read each guide's limits before spending resources.

Corrections

Report a disputed claim with the affected page, evidence and current game version through the feedback page. That form prepares an email draft; the website does not send it automatically. Unsupported claims are corrected, narrowed or withdrawn.

Advertising and sponsorship

No advertising, affiliate placements or sponsorships are active. If introduced, ads and paid relationships will be clearly disclosed, and payment will not determine recommendations. Advertising requires a separate content, privacy and ad-network review. Ads will not be enabled merely because other fan sites display them.
